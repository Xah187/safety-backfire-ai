## 1. Purpose & Architectural Mission

This specification defines the mandatory governance rules, data schemas, validation procedures, quality control metrics, and blind evaluation protocols for acquiring and validating external facility evidence under **Safety Backfire AI Version V3.4**.

The primary objective of Milestone V3.4 is **REAL_WORLD_EVIDENCE_COMPLETION**. Real-world external video evidence and dataset-provided camera calibrations have been acquired in V3.3.3 (`Warehouse_026` and `Warehouse_027`); however, several critical independent evidence dimensions remain unavailable (human detection ground truth, trajectory ground truth, physical lux telemetry, independent physical camera surveys, safety intervention event annotations, and Safety Backfire event ground truth). Milestone V3.4 establishes the formal governance, validation infrastructure, and acquisition schemas required to complete these evidence dimensions without modifying frozen Model H parameters.

### 3.2 Immutability Policy
1. Files placed in `data/external_v3_4/raw/` must have POSIX read-only permissions (`0444`).
2. Modification, overwriting, truncation, or re-encoding of raw video streams or raw telemetry is strictly prohibited.
3. Every file must have an entry in the corresponding `acquisition_manifest.json` containing its exact SHA-256 digest and file byte size.

## 4. Ground Truth & Telemetry Specifications

### 4.1 Detection Ground Truth (`detection_gt.schema.json`)
* **Scope**: Per-camera, per-frame spatial localization of human workers and mobile industrial machinery (forklifts, AGVs, AMRs).
* **Mandatory Coordinate Format**: `bbox` array formatted as `[x_min, y_min, x_max, y_max]` in floating-point pixels normalized to the native sensor resolution, where $0.0 \le x_{\text{min}} < x_{\text{max}} \le W$ and $0.0 \le y_{\text{min}} < y_{\text{max}} \le H$.
* **Foot Point**: `foot_point` array formatted as `[u, v]` indicating the ground contact point of the entity. For upright pedestrians, this is the center-bottom of the bounding box $(0.5 \cdot (x_{\text{min}} + x_{\text{max}}), y_{\text{max}})$.
* **Occlusion & Visibility**:
  * `visibility`: Continuous score $[0.0, 1.0]$ representing unoccluded visual surface proportion.
  * `occlusion`: Discrete classification restricted to `"NONE"` ($<10\%$ occluded), `"PARTIAL"` ($10\% - 50\%$ occluded), or `"HEAVY"` ($>50\%$ occluded).

### 4.2 Trajectory Ground Truth (`trajectory_gt.schema.json`)
* **Scope**: Metric world-coordinate trajectories tracking unique entities persistently across time and across multiple cameras.
* **Ground Coordinates**: `world_x`, `world_y`, and `world_z` in meters relative to the facility origin ($Z=0$ represents the nominal ground plane).
* **Zone Identification**: `zone_id` string referencing surveyed physical areas (e.g., `"AISLE_04"`, `"INTERSECTION_NORTH"`, `"LOADING_BAY_B"`).

### 4.3 Lux Telemetry (`lux_telemetry.schema.json`)
* **Sensor Requirements**: Data must originate from calibrated physical lux meters (illuminance sensors) with NIST-traceable or ISO/IEC 17025 calibration certificates.
* **Telemetry Fields**:
  * `lux`: Measured physical illuminance in lumens/$\text{m}^2$ ($L \ge 0.0$). If a sensor drops offline or fails, the field must explicitly register `"UNKNOWN"`.
  * `sampling_rate_hz`: Nominal sensor sampling frequency (minimum recommended $1.0\text{ Hz}$).
  * `measurement_uncertainty`: Declared expanded uncertainty ($\pm \text{lux}$).
  * `calibration_reference`: Identifier of the traceable calibration document.
  * `location`: Coordinates $[X, Y, Z]$ of the lux meter within the facility coordinate frame.

### 4.4 Camera Surveying (`camera_survey.schema.json`)
* **Survey Requirements**: Physical camera installations must be surveyed using laser rangefinders, total stations, or calibrated electronic inclinometers.
* **Geometric Definitions**:
  * `camera_height_m`: Height of the camera optical center above the ground plane in meters.
  * `optical_axis_vector`: Unit vector $[v_x, v_y, v_z]$ in facility world coordinates defining the optical gaze direction ($v_x^2 + v_y^2 + v_z^2 = 1.0 \pm 10^{-4}$).
  * `depression_angle_deg`: Angle of the optical axis below the horizontal plane: $\theta_{\text{dep}} = -\arcsin(v_z)$.
  * `extrinsics`: $3 \times 4$ transformation matrix $[R \mid t]$ mapping world coordinates to camera coordinates.
  * `intrinsics`: $3 \times 3$ camera calibration matrix $K$ containing focal lengths and principal points.
  * `distortion`: Lens distortion coefficients $[k_1, k_2, p_1, p_2, k_3]$.

### 4.5 Intervention Event Specification (`intervention_event.schema.json`)
Every industrial safety intervention event (e.g., AGV emergency stop, vehicle deceleration, audible operator horn alert) is parameterized by three strictly non-overlapping temporal windows:
1. **Pre-Intervention Window (`pre_start` to `pre_end`)**: Nominal operational baseline before hazard onset.
2. **Intervention Window (`intervention_start` to `intervention_end`)**: Active safety system engagement or human evasive reaction.
3. **Post-Intervention Window (`post_start` to `post_end`)**: Resulting operational state (resolution, clearance, or collision).

```
         PRE-INTERVENTION            INTERVENTION               POST-INTERVENTION
      [─────────────────────]   [─────────────────────]     [──────────────────────]
      pre_start       pre_end   int_start       int_end     post_start      post_end
──────────────────┬─────────────────┬───────────────────────────┬──────────────────────► Time
                  │                 │                           │
                  └── strictly < ───┴──────── strictly < ───────┘
```

* **Adjudicated Labels**: Restricted strictly to:
  * `"BACKFIRE"`: The automated safety intervention increased aggregate hazard (e.g., abrupt braking provoked rear-end collision or trapped a worker in a blind spot).
  * `"NO_BACKFIRE"`: The intervention safely mitigated the hazard without introducing collateral risk.
  * `"AMBIGUOUS"`: Evidence is insufficient or reviewer consensus could not be established.
* **Reviewer Consensus**: Must record `reviewer_count` ($\ge 2$ certified safety engineers required for final adjudication) and `adjudication_notes`.

## 5. Quality Control & Validation Pipeline

The executable script `scripts/v3_4/validate_acquisition_package.py` enforces twenty mandatory validation checks on every candidate acquisition package:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        20 MANDATORY PACKAGE VALIDATION CHECKS                          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  1. Required Files Existence     : Video, metadata, survey, and telemetry files exist. │
│  2. Cryptographic Hash Integrity : Computed SHA-256 digests match manifest entries.    │
│  3. Video Container Readability  : Containers decode via OpenCV without frame errors.  │
│  4. Frame Rate Validity          : FPS is constant and within expected bounds.         │
│  5. Timestamp Monotonicity       : Timestamps strictly increase across consecutive frames│
│  6. Camera ID Consistency        : IDs match exactly across video, survey, and GT.     │
│  7. Object ID Validity           : Entity IDs are non-empty alphanumeric strings.      │
│  8. Bounding Box Boundary Check  : 0.0 <= x_min < x_max <= W and 0.0 <= y_min < y_max. │
│  9. Trajectory ID Consistency    : Trajectories link consistently across timestamps.   │
│ 10. Lux Timestamp Correlation    : Telemetry timestamps align with video time range.   │
│ 11. Explicit "UNKNOWN" for Lux   : Missing lux values must be "UNKNOWN", never zero.   │
│ 12. Camera Survey Completeness   : All 13 survey fields are populated and conformal.   │
│ 13. Window Chronology            : pre_start <= pre_end < int_start <= int_end etc.    │
│ 14. Temporal Non-Overlap         : Windows must not overlap incorrectly.               │
│ 15. Zone Definition Validity     : Referenced zones exist in facility CAD registry.    │
│ 16. Restricted GT Labels         : Exactly one of BACKFIRE, NO_BACKFIRE, AMBIGUOUS.    │
│ 17. Event ID Uniqueness          : Zero duplicate event IDs allowed in manifest.       │
│ 18. Identity Spatial Uniqueness  : Entity cannot occupy two distinct bboxes in 1 frame.│
│ 19. Raw File Immutability        : File byte counts and access timestamps unaltered.   │
│ 20. Manifest Conformance         : Manifest matches physical on-disk file topology.    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Failure Protocol**: If any check fails, the validation tool exits with a non-zero return code and logs the exact violation, offending file, and line number. **Silent recovery, auto-repair, and default substitution are strictly prohibited.**

## 6. Facility-Level Blind Evaluation Protocol

To prevent model overfitting, data leakage, and confirmation bias, V3.4 enforces a **Facility-Level Blind Split**:

1. **Unit of Partition**: Data is split exclusively at the facility level (e.g., `Facility_A` in Training/Exploration, `Facility_B` in Blind Evaluation).
2. **Prohibition of View Splitting**: Splitting different camera angles of the same physical facility between training and evaluation is strictly forbidden due to spatial-temporal leakage.
3. **Withheld Ground Truth**: During evaluation, ground truth files (`detection_gt.json`, `trajectory_gt.json`, `intervention_event.json`) are quarantined in an isolated, encrypted, or restricted storage tier. Evaluation code receives only the raw video streams, camera survey metadata, and unlabelled evaluation manifests generated by `scripts/v3_4/create_blind_manifest.py`.
4. **Cryptographic Release Seal**: Ground-truth labels are decrypted and matched against pipeline predictions only during the formal gate execution, supervised by an independent audit observer.

## 7. Model H Protection & Immutability Lock

The canonical Model H formula and its parameters represent the inviolable baseline of the Safety Backfire research program:

$$k_0 = 2.40, \quad \beta_d = 0.08, \quad \beta_l = 0.12, \quad \beta_{\text{pitch}} = 0.22$$

Any proposal to modify, re-tune, or recalibrate these parameters requires a formal Milestone Transition Request approved across all safety engineering stakeholders. Within V3.4, automated test assertions permanently enforce parameter immobility against `safety_backfire_v3_3_3/uncertainty/model_h.py` and `outputs/v3_4/manifests/model_h_frozen_reference.json`.
