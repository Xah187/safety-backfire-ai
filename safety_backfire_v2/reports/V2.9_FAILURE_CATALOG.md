## 1. Failure Severity Classification Framework

Failures are categorized into four strict engineering tiers:
* **CRITICAL (P0):** Causes complete safety failure (unreported critical hazard, complete system crash, or severe multi-agent corruption).
* **HIGH (P1):** Causes severe degradation of safety decisions (suppresses real hazards, fragments events, or violates core safety invariants).
* **MEDIUM (P2):** Introduces systematic numerical or geometric distortion, unphysical kinematic spikes, or architectural incompatibilities.
* **LOW (P3):** Hidden coupling, unvalidated magic numbers, or code maintenance risks.

## 3. Deep Forensic Profiles for Critical & High Failures

### [FAIL-01] Multi-Vehicle Relation Overwrite & Cross-Contamination
* **Component:** `OperatorVehicleRelationEngineV2.classify_frame`
* **Trigger:** A frame containing 1 person and $\ge 2$ forklifts (e.g. F1 close, F2 far).
* **Propagation Path:**
  ```text
  Person near F1 (overlap=0.9)
    ↓
  Loop continues to F2 (overlap=0.0)
    ↓
  relations.append() called outside loop with forklift_id = F2["id"]
    ↓
  Relation with F1 never stored (get_person_relation(P, F1) -> None)
    ↓
  F2 assigned F1's overlap evidence or marked independent
    ↓
  Safety Engine evaluates wrong vehicle relation
  ```
* **Why Safeguards Failed:** Existing tests only tested 1 Person + 1 Forklift. Multi-vehicle scenarios were never asserted at the unit level.
* **Risk Severity:** **CRITICAL**.

---

### [FAIL-02] 1D Range-Rate TTC False Collision Alerts on Crossing Paths
* **Component:** `core/kinematics.compute_metric_ttc`
* **Trigger:** An independent pedestrian crossing an aisle 5 to 10 meters ahead of a traveling forklift.
* **Propagation Path:**
  ```text
  d(t) decreasing momentarily (v_approach > 0.1 m/s)
    ↓
  TTC = d / v_approach computed without 2D trajectory extrapolation
    ↓
  TTC < 3.0s flagged as valid collision course
    ↓
  EventEngine escalates UNKNOWN candidate to CONFIRMED_EVENT
    ↓
  False alarm emitted despite 5.6m Miss-Distance (CPA)
  ```
* **Why Safeguards Failed:** System assumed that negative distance derivative ($-\dot{d} > 0$) implies an impending collision.
* **Risk Severity:** **CRITICAL**.

### [FAIL-03] Aspect Ratio Filter Erases Fallen Worker ("Man Down")
* **Component:** `core/observation_gate.ObservationIntegrityGate` (Rule 3)
* **Trigger:** A worker falls, collapses, or crawls on the warehouse floor (aspect ratio $\text{height}/\text{width} = 0.44 < 1.0$) with obscured joints.
* **Propagation Path:**
  ```text
  Fallen worker detected by YOLO as "person"
    ↓
  Gate inspects aspect ratio: 0.44 < 1.0 (incompatible_horizontal)
    ↓
  Decision: INVALID (Impossible human aspect ratio)
    ↓
  safety_eligible set to False
    ↓
  Worker completely invisible to Proximity, TTC, and Event Engine!
  ```
* **Why Safeguards Failed:** Rule assumed all upright humans have aspect ratio $\ge 1.0$ to filter tire reflections, ignoring industrial "Man Down" scenarios.
* **Risk Severity:** **CRITICAL**.

### [FAIL-04] Complete Execution Crash in RiskEngine on EventEngineV2

* **Trigger:** Calling `calculate_risk_exposure()` on any event generated by `EventEngineV2`.
* **Propagation Path:**

  EventEngineV2 produces InteractionEventV2

* **Why Safeguards Failed:** `RiskEngine` was decoupled from video scripts in V2.8 and never regression-tested with `InteractionEventV2`.
* **Risk Severity:** **CRITICAL**.

### [FAIL-05] Running Pedestrian Misclassified as Vehicle Operator
* **Component:** `generate_v2_8_5_video.py` line 138
* **Trigger:** A pedestrian walking briskly or running in an open aisle ($v > 0.8$ m/s).
* **Propagation Path:**
  ```text
  Pedestrian speed exceeds 0.8 m/s
    ↓
  p_is_op evaluated as True via speed heuristic shortcut
    ↓
  Pedestrian labeled as OPERATOR ON MOVING FORKLIFT
    ↓
  Near-miss proximity to any parked vehicle suppressed as normal traffic!
  ```
* **Why Safeguards Failed:** Over-reliance on speed as a proxy for vehicle riding without verifying physical attachment to a forklift.
* **Risk Severity:** **HIGH**.

### [FAIL-08] Pipeline Latency & Inability to Run Live 25 FPS
* **Component:** Pipeline End-to-End Throughput
* **Measured Metric:** **15.5 FPS** on Apple Silicon Mac (`test2.mp4` 49.8s took 80.3s).
* **Root Cause Breakdown:**
  * Frame Decode: $1.57$s ($794$ FPS) — Negligible.
  * YOLO Detection: $41.69$s ($29.9$ FPS) — Real-time capable alone.
  * YOLOv8n Pose: $36.94$s ($33.7$ FPS) — Sequential bottleneck.
  * Tracking + Geometry + Events: $<0.1$s ($>20,000$ FPS) — Negligible.
* **System Implication:** Sequential execution of Detector + Pose takes $\approx 63$ms per frame ($15.5$ FPS). When connected to a 25 FPS live RTSP feed, the internal buffer accumulates a 10-second latency lag every 25 seconds.
* **Risk Severity:** **HIGH**.
