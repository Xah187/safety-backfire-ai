## 3. Detailed Audit of Recovered Defects (FAIL-23 and FAIL-24)

### 3.1 FAIL-23: Calibrated Uncertainty Under-Coverage
- **Identified**: V3.0.3 independent audit revealed empirical coverage of $89.0\%$ against nominal $95.0\%$.
- **V3.0.4 Resolution**:
  - Identified Jensen convexity bias on 2D Euclidean norm: $+0.1269\,\text{m}$.
  - Applied second-order debiasing: $d_{\text{unbiased}} = d_{\text{obs}} - \frac{\sigma_\perp^2}{2 d_{\text{obs}}}$.
  - Calibrated coverage multiplier for 2-DOF Rice distribution: $k = 2.24$.
  - Post-fix empirical coverage: **$95.3\%$** ($[90.7\%, 97.7\%]$ Wilson 95% CI).

### 3.2 FAIL-24: Linear Elevation Truncation Breakdown
- **Identified**: First-order Taylor approximation $\Delta y = (f_y \cdot h_o \cdot \cos\theta) / D$ collapsed at steep pitch ($45^\circ$) or close range ($1.5\,\text{m}$), producing up to $667.94\,\text{px}$ truncation error.
- **V3.0.4 Resolution**:
  - Implemented exact closed-form projective camera ray equations in `compute_exact_projective_elevation_shift_px()`.
  - Replaced linear approximation in Method E with the exact formulation.
  - Truncation error reduced to **$0.00\,\text{px}$** across all 480 tested parameter combinations.

## 4. Newly Registered System Boundaries (FAIL-26 and FAIL-27)

### 4.1 FAIL-26: Far-Field Monocular Depth Dilution ($D > 10.0\,\text{m}$)
- **Description**: In a single monocular pinhole camera, ground coordinate resolution diminishes quadratically with range:
  $$\frac{\partial Y_w}{\partial v} = \frac{Y_w^2 + H_c^2}{f_y \cdot H_c}$$
  At $D = 10.0\,\text{m}$, a $1\,\text{px}$ bounding box bottom jitter causes $\approx 0.32\,\text{m}$ ground drift. At $D = 15.0\,\text{m}$, it causes $> 0.75\,\text{m}$ drift.
- **Boundary Contract**: Monocular tracking is certified for high-precision clearance decisions only within $D \le 8.0\,\text{m}$. Beyond $8.0\,\text{m}$, the system automatically inflates position uncertainty and triggers `MONITORING` mode.

### 4.2 FAIL-27: Non-Planar Floor Gradients ($> 3^\circ$ Incline Ramps)
- **Description**: The ray-plane ground intersection assumes planar floor $Z_w = 0$. On loading dock ramps or transition inclines $> 3^\circ$, ground projection over- or under-estimates forward distance by up to $1.2\,\text{m}$.
- **Boundary Contract**: Planar calibration is invalid on sloped ramps without multi-plane homography.
