#### Critical Safety Comparison:
- **False Emergency Alarm Prevention**:
  Under V3.0.3's $-3.974\,\text{m}$ Y-bias, the distance in Frame 801 was falsely compressed to **$0.37\,\text{m}$**. This would have triggered an erroneous emergency stop / wheel collision alert, claiming the worker was directly beneath the chassis.
- **Physical Ground Truth Accuracy in V3.0.4**:
  The V3.0.4 engine correctly measures the physical separation as **$1.04\,\text{m}$** (with 95% confidence interval $[0.48\,\text{m}, 1.60\,\text{m}]$). It accurately classifies this as a `WARNING_VIOLATION` requiring alert logging, without triggering a catastrophic false emergency stop.
