## 4. Unresolved Empirical Limitations & Ongoing Research

While P0 defects `FAIL-23` and `FAIL-24` have been resolved, the following empirical boundaries remain documented:
1. **Far-Field Monocular Degradation (`FAIL-26`)**:
   Beyond $8.0\,\text{m}$ range, optical ray divergence naturally reduces measurement precision. Multi-camera stereo or ceiling-mounted lidar fusion is required for perimeter intrusion monitoring.
2. **Floor Elevation Variations (`FAIL-27`)**:
   Planar homography cannot compensate for drainage slopes or vehicle loading dock ramps. Topographic mesh mapping is required for uneven industrial terrains.
3. **Severe Low-Light / Heavy Smoke Occlusion**:
   Keypoint detection confidence degrades below $0.35$ in low-illumination environments, triggering fallback to bounding-box approximations with inflated uncertainty intervals.
