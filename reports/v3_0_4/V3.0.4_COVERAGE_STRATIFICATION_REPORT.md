# V3.0.4 — Empirical Coverage Stratification Report

## 1. Executive Summary

Aggregate coverage metrics can mask severe localized failures in critical safety zones. In V3.0.3, although overall coverage was $89.0\%$, the critical warning band ($1.0\,\text{m} - 2.0\,\text{m}$) experienced severe under-coverage at **$86.7\%$**.
In V3.0.4, empirical coverage is rigorously stratified across:
1. Six operational distance bands ($0.35\,\text{m}$ to $12.0\,\text{m}$).
2. Three uncertainty levels (LOW, MODERATE, HIGH).
3. Four industrial vehicle classes.

### 2.1 Critical Safety Finding: The $1.0 - 2.0\,\text{m}$ Zone Recovery
The $1.0 - 2.0\,\text{m}$ zone represents the primary human-robot safety clearance envelope in logistics facilities:
- In V3.0.3, $13.3\%$ of interactions in this zone were missed because Euclidean convexity expanded observed distance and lifted the lower bound.
- In V3.0.4, with Jensen debiasing and the 2D Rice multiplier ($k=2.24$), coverage increased to **$96.7\%$** ($29 / 30$ covered), with a well-calibrated mean interval width of $1.517\,\text{m}$.
- The single remaining outlier had high sensor noise ($\sigma = 0.44\,\text{m}$) where the lower bound was conservative.
