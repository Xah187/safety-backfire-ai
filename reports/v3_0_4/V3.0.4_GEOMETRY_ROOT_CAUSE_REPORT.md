## 3. Mathematical Proof of Root Cause

### 3.1 The Two Incompatible Coordinate Frames

Let a camera be mounted at world coordinates $(0, 0, H_c)$ tilted downwards by pitch angle $\theta = 28^\circ$.

#### Frame Convention 1: Horizontal Depression Angle (Used in `pixel_to_world`)
- When $\theta = 0^\circ$, the camera points **horizontally forward** along $+Y_w$.
- Optical axis $Z_c$ points along $+Y_w$.
- Downward image axis $Y_c$ points along $-Z_w$ (down towards the ground).
- As $\theta$ increases, the camera pitches downwards toward the floor.
- An optical ray passing through image row $v$ has depression angle $\alpha = \arctan\left(\frac{v - c_y}{f_y}\right)$.
- Total ray depression angle from the horizontal: $\theta_{\text{total}} = \theta + \alpha$.
- The ground intersection ($Z_w = 0$) is:
  $$Y_w = \frac{H_c}{\tan(\theta + \alpha)}$$

#### Frame Convention 2: Nadir Tilt Angle (Erroneously Used in V3.0.3 `world_to_pixel`)
In V3.0.3 `SurveyedGroundCalibrator.world_to_pixel`:
```python
# V3.0.3 Buggy Formulation:
Z_c = Y * math.sin(self.pitch_rad) + (self.H - z) * math.cos(self.pitch_rad)
Y_c = Y * math.cos(self.pitch_rad) - (self.H - z) * math.sin(self.pitch_rad)
```
Notice the trigonometric functions when pitch $\theta = 0$:
- $Z_c = (H_c - z) \cdot \cos(0) = H_c - z$ (points straight **DOWN at nadir**!).
- $Y_c = Y \cdot \cos(0) = Y$ (points along $+Y_w$!).
Thus, this equation defines $\theta$ as the tilt angle from the **vertical nadir**, NOT the horizontal!

### 3.2 The Mathematical Consequence of Swapped Angles
When a point at $Y_w = 6.0\,\text{m}$ was evaluated with nominal pitch $\theta = 28^\circ$:
1. `world_to_pixel` treated $\theta$ as nadir tilt: the camera was modeled as tilted $28^\circ$ from the vertical, which is equivalent to a horizontal depression angle of:
   $$\theta_{\text{eff}} = 90^\circ - 28^\circ = 62^\circ$$
2. A steep depression angle of $62^\circ$ caused the forward target to project far down in the image (high $v$ pixel coordinate: $v = 947.73\,\text{px}$).
3. Then, `pixel_to_world` inverted this pixel $v = 947.73$ assuming $\theta = 28^\circ$ from the horizontal:
   $$\alpha = \arctan\left(\frac{947.73 - 360.0}{950.0}\right) = \arctan(0.6186) \approx 31.74^\circ$$
   $$\theta_{\text{total}} = 28.0^\circ + 31.74^\circ = 59.74^\circ$$
   $$Y_{\text{reconstructed}} = \frac{3.5}{\tan(59.74^\circ)} = \frac{3.5}{1.714} \approx 2.04\,\text{m}$$
4. Reconstruction error:
   $$e_y = Y_{\text{reconstructed}} - Y_{\text{true}} = 2.04\,\text{m} - 6.00\,\text{m} = -3.96\,\text{m}!$$

## 4. The Corrected Rigorous Pinhole Formulation

In V3.0.4, the coordinate transformation is unified under standard right-handed robotics / photogrammetric conventions:
- World frame: $X_w$ right, $Y_w$ forward, $Z_w$ up. Camera origin at $(0, 0, H_c)$.
- Pitch angle $\theta$: depression angle downward from horizontal ($0^\circ \le \theta < 90^\circ$).

### 4.1 Forward Projection (World to Pixel)
$$Z_c = Y_w \cos\theta + (H_c - Z_w) \sin\theta$$
$$Y_c = (H_c - Z_w) \cos\theta - Y_w \sin\theta$$
$$X_c = X_w$$
$$u = f_x \frac{X_c}{Z_c} + c_x, \quad v = f_y \frac{Y_c}{Z_c} + c_y$$

### 4.2 Inverse Projection (Pixel to Planar Ground $Z_w = 0$)
Let $t_y = \frac{v - c_y}{f_y}$.
$$Y_w = H_c \frac{\cos\theta - t_y \sin\theta}{\sin\theta + t_y \cos\theta}$$
$$Z_c = Y_w \cos\theta + H_c \sin\theta$$
$$X_w = \left(\frac{u - c_x}{f_x}\right) Z_c$$
