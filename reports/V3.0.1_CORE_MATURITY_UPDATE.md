## 4. Known Architectural Limitations & Operational Boundaries

Before deployable field trials, the engineering team must operate within the following characterized boundaries:

1. **Illumination Invariance Boundary (`FAIL-13`)**:
   - The current appearance descriptor relies on HSV color distribution and vertical body partitioning. Under heavy sodium-vapor or tungsten lighting, similarity falls from $1.00$ to $0.36$. Cameras deployed in field trials must enforce auto-white-balance locking.
2. **Camera Pitch Vibration Sensitivity (`FAIL-15`)**:
   - Planar homography assumes a fixed camera extrinsic matrix. Physical vibrations causing pitch angular deflections of $\pm 2.5^\circ$ introduce up to $0.80\,\text{m}$ ground localization error at $10\,\text{m}$ range.
3. **Fixed Perspective Offset Geometry (`FAIL-19`)**:
   - The $28\,\text{px}$ elevation offset compensation is calibrated specifically for camera mounting heights of $3.5-4.0\,\text{m}$ and forklift cabin elevations of $1.2\,\text{m}$. Sites with alternate geometry must supply extrinsic calibration parameters.
4. **Point-Estimate Risk Evaluation (`FAIL-22`)**:
   - Downstream Risk Engine currently evaluates nominal Euclidean distance. When ankle keypoints are occluded, the uncertainty margin ($\pm 0.83\,\text{m}$) must be factored into zone classification to prevent optimistic safety suppression.
