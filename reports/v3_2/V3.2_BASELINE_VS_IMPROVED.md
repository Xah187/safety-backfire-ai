# V3.2 — Baseline vs. Improved: Architectural & Empirical Evolution

## 1. Executive Summary

This report establishes the traceable, line-by-line comparative analysis between **Frozen V3.1 Baseline** and the **Improved V3.2 Real-World Validated Core**.

V3.1 generalized the analytical safety core from a single-pair (`Person ↔ Forklift`) detector into an entity-agnostic warehouse safety ontology and universal risk engine. However, V3.1 relied primarily on synthetic and controlled laboratory validation. **V3.2 subjects the V3.1 architecture to empirical warehouse video, independent human ground truth, adversarial physical perturbations, and red-team stress testing.**

## 2. Preserved V3.1 Core Invariants & Mathematical Foundations

In accordance with the immutable baseline freeze protocol (`safety_backfire_v3/outputs/v3_2/v3_1_frozen/` and `baseline_v3_1_frozen.json`), all core mathematical theorems and invariants of V3.1 are 100% preserved:
1. **2D Rice Spatial Uncertainty:** Non-central Chi distribution formulation with unilateral coverage factor $k = 2.24$ and Jensen convex debiasing ($\Delta_{\text{Jensen}}$) remains active.
2. **Decomposed Confidence Contract:** Evidence vector $\mathbf{C} \in [0, 1]^8$ (sensor, detection, tracking, spatial, temporal, classification, model, contextual) preserved without epistemic compression into a single scalar.
3. **Ontology Typings & Taxonomies:** The 6 base entity classes, 5 state types, 15 spatial relations, 8 interaction categories, and 20 hazard taxonomies remain structurally intact.
4. **Decoupled ID Namespaces:** Strict separation between `EntityID`, `PairID`, `EpisodeID`, `HazardID`, `EventID`, and `AlertID` preserved.
5. **Universal Risk Engine:** Deterministic worst-case risk aggregation ($\text{risk} = \max_i R_i$) preserved without benign hazard suppression.
