## 3. Four-Tier Validation Hierarchy

To prevent false claims of universal readiness, all system capabilities are partitioned into four strict epistemological tiers:

### 3.1 Tier A: Mathematically Validated
Capabilities guaranteed by closed-form equations, proofs, and numerical invariants:
1. Closed-form ground plane projection homography ($Z_w = 0$).
2. 2D Rice Chi-distribution clearance lower bounds ($k = 2.24$).
3. Jensen inequality convex variance correction ($\Delta_{\text{Jensen}}$).
4. Near-zero Euclidean clamping ($d \to 0$, $d_{\text{safe}} \ge 0.0$).
5. Covariance matrix regularization floor ($\lambda_{\min} \ge 10^{-4}$).
6. Difference-in-Differences quasi-experimental estimator.

### 3.2 Tier B: Synthetically Validated
Capabilities validated through physics engines and controlled synthetic failure injection:
1. High-altitude pallet falling object trajectories ($> 6.0\,\text{m}$ drops).
2. Extreme camera pitch drift perturbations ($\pm 5.0^\circ$).
3. Monte Carlo latency jitter ($0 - 3,000\,\text{ms}$) and packet loss injection.
4. Sustained 60-minute stress testing ($108,000\,\text{frames}$).

### 3.3 Tier C: Real-World Validated
Capabilities verified against real warehouse CCTV video, surveyed laser markers, and independent human annotations:
1. Multi-entity detection (Person, Forklift, Reach Truck, Storage Rack, Load).
2. Planar ground coordinate accuracy ($1.69\,\text{cm}$ MAE across 100 surveyed points).
3. Continuous trajectory tracking and crossing path resolution.
4. Five operational hazards: Person-Forklift collision, Forklift-Rack impact, Blocked aisle, Blocked emergency exit, Restricted-zone intrusion.
5. Real-world Safety Backfire and multi-zone risk displacement detection.

### 3.4 Tier D: Not Yet Validated / Sensor-Required
Capabilities strictly outside the operational boundaries of passive monocular CCTV:
1. Airborne hazardous gas or toxic vapor leaks (chemical sensor required).
2. Structural micro-fissures inside steel rack uprights (ultrasonic/strain sensor required).
3. Non-planar loading dock ramps exceeding $3.0^\circ$ incline (3D LiDAR mesh required).
