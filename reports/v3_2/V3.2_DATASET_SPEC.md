# V3.2 — Real-World Warehouse Dataset Specification

## 1. Executive Summary

This document establishes the official dataset specification for **Safety Backfire AI V3.2**. In accordance with the non-negotiable data provenance and lineage requirements, every video clip, camera viewpoint, environmental condition, and ground truth annotation used to evaluate the V3.1 Safety Core is cataloged with complete hardware, optical, spatial, and lineage attributes.

No video is accepted into the evaluation corpus without an authenticated provenance record, an explicit dataset partition assignment, and a verifiable camera/session identifier.

---

## 3. Metadata Specification: `VideoMetadataV1`

Every video stream within V3.2 is paired with a strictly validated `VideoMetadataV1` metadata record conforming to the schema in `safety_backfire_v3/validation/v3_2/metadata.py`.

```text
VideoMetadataV1:
  video_id:                 str (UUID / canonical identifier)
  camera_id:                str (unique physical hardware mount)
  location_id:              str (physical warehouse sector / facility)
  capture_session_id:       str (continuous recording session ID)
  capture_date:             str (ISO-8601 date)
  capture_duration_sec:     float (duration in seconds)
  source_type:              str (FIXED_CCTV | MOBILE_SURVEY | DRONE_AUDIT)
  resolution:               Tuple[int, int] (width, height in pixels)
  fps:                      float (frames per second)
  camera_height_m:          float (measured physical height above floor)
  camera_pitch_deg:         float (tilt angle down from horizontal)
  camera_roll_deg:          float (optical roll around optical axis)
  camera_yaw_deg:           float (compass yaw angle)
  lens_type:                str (FIXED_FOCAL | VARIFOCAL | FISHEYE)
  focal_length_px:          float (effective focal length f_x, f_y)
  principal_point:          Tuple[float, float] (c_x, c_y in pixels)
  distortion_k1:            float (radial distortion coefficient)
  calibration_version:      str (CAL_RIGID_SURVEY_V1 | ESTIMATED_AUTO)
  floor_type:               str (POLISHED_CONCRETE | EPOXY_COATED | ASPHALT)
  floor_slope_deg:          float (measured ground incline)
  lighting_condition:       str (HIGH_BAY_SODIUM | INDUSTRIAL_FLUORESCENT | LOW_LIGHT)
  traffic_density:          str (LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH | CONGESTED)
  dataset_split:            str (DEVELOPMENT | CALIBRATION | VALIDATION | BLIND_TEST)
  lineage_status:           str (KNOWN_DEVELOPMENT | CALIBRATION | VALIDATION | BLIND_TEST | UNKNOWN)
```

### 3.1 Unknown Value Enforcement
Under V3.2 safety governance:
1. Any unmeasured physical parameter must be explicitly set to `UNKNOWN`.
2. Model-inferred or auto-estimated values must never be stored as surveyed parameters; they are tagged `ESTIMATED_AUTO`.
3. Planar ground homography assumptions are invalid if `floor_slope_deg` $> 3.0^\circ$ or if `floor_type` contains uneven transitions.

## 4. Ground Truth Annotation Schema

To prevent AI self-validation circularity, the ground truth annotations are produced independently by certified human safety annotators according to the formal schema below.

### 4.1 Supported Entity Classes
```text
- person            - forklift          - reach_truck       - pallet_jack
- rack_upright      - rack_beam         - pallet            - box_cluster
- industrial_robot  - conveyor_belt     - loading_dock      - emergency_exit
- safety_bollard    - pedestrian_walkway- staging_zone      - blind_corner
```

### 4.2 Visibility & Epistemic State Contract
Every object annotation contains a mandatory `visibility_state`:
- `VISIBLE`: Fully or substantially visible ($\ge 75\%$ unoccluded).
- `OCCLUDED`: Partially occluded by racks, cargo, or other entities ($25\% - 75\%$ occluded).
- `AMBIGUOUS`: Severe visual degradation, heavy motion blur, or edge-of-frame truncation ($< 25\%$ visible).
- `NOT_VISIBLE`: Known to be physically present behind an obstruction, but zero visual pixels visible.
- `UNKNOWN`: Insufficient evidence to determine physical state.

> [!CRITICAL]
> **Safety Invariant:** `NOT_VISIBLE` must NEVER be interpreted as `ABSENT`. The tracking and safety engines must maintain probabilistic existence hypotheses until explicit clearing evidence is observed.

### 4.3 Ground Truth Coordinates
For each frame, human annotators establish:
1. **Bounding Box:** $[x_{\min}, y_{\min}, x_{\max}, y_{\max}]$ in normalized image coordinates.
2. **Contact Point:** $[u_{\text{ground}}, v_{\text{ground}}]$ representing the physical contact point with the warehouse floor.
3. **Surveyed World Position:** $[X_w, Y_w]$ in physical ground meters relative to the surveyed camera nadir.
4. **Motion State:** `MOVING`, `STATIONARY`, `REVERSING`, `TURNING`, `STOPPED`.
5. **Operational State:** `LOADED`, `UNLOADED`, `DAMAGED`, `BLOCKED`, `RESTRICTED`.

## 5. Temporal Sequence Annotation Protocol

For critical interaction scenarios, annotations are not captured as isolated, static snapshots. Annotators annotate **consecutive temporal sequences** spanning continuous windows ($t - 30$ to $t + 30$ frames minimum; $60 - 300$ continuous frames per episode).

This continuous temporal ground truth enables unambiguous empirical evaluation of:
1. Tracklet continuity and identity persistence.
2. Velocity vector convergence and acceleration estimation.
3. Time-to-Collision (TTC) and Closest Point of Approach (CPA) curves.
4. Alert lifecycle phases: Candidate onset $\to$ Confirmation $\to$ Danger persistence $\to$ Dwell revocation.
