### Documented Physical ODD Boundaries:
1. **FAIL-13 / FAIL-40 (Visually Identical PPE Vests):** Spatial Kalman trajectory gating is required; visual embeddings alone cannot distinguish workers wearing identical high-vis vests after close crossing passes.
2. **FAIL-26 / FAIL-38 (Far-Field Monocular Ray Dilution):** Beyond $8.0\,\text{m}$, monocular perspective ground projection uncertainty exceeds defensive clearance buffers. Emergency braking is restricted to $D \le 8.0\,\text{m}$.
3. **FAIL-27 / FAIL-45 (Non-Planar Ramp Floor Gradients):** Planar homography violates on floor ramps exceeding $3^\circ$ incline. Ramps must be modeled via 3D meshes or marked `OUT_OF_ODD`.
4. **FAIL-30 / FAIL-44 (Camera Pitch Mechanical Deflection):** Forward ground coordinates scale quadratically with depth. Camera mounting brackets must maintain mechanical rigidity within $\pm 0.35^\circ$.
