# V3.2 — Level A: Real Perception Validation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This report delivers the empirical performance evaluation of the perception layer of Safety Backfire AI V3.2 across real warehouse CCTV video streams. Evaluated against independent human ground truth across $N = 500$ annotated object instances, the perception layer achieved:
- **Overall Precision:** $0.9442$ ($94.42\%$) [$474 / 502$ detections]
- **Overall Recall:** $0.9480$ ($94.80\%$) [$474 / 500$ ground truth targets]
- **Overall F1 Score:** $0.9461$ ($94.61\%$)
- **Mean Bounding Box IoU:** $0.8220$ ($82.20\%$)

While aggregate figures indicate strong performance, granular stratification exposes critical physical boundaries in far-field detection and severe occlusion, confirming `CANDIDATE-38` as an official failure mode (`FAIL-38`).

### 2.1 Key Per-Class Observations
1. **Vehicles (Forklifts, Reach Trucks):** Highest recall ($97.50\%$) and IoU ($88.50\%$) due to large pixel footprints and distinct rigid geometry.
2. **Pedestrians:** High overall recall ($95.00\%$), but degraded under occlusion ($74.20\%$) and far distances ($68.40\%$).
3. **Loads (Pallets, Boxes):** Lowest precision ($86.27\%$) and highest miss rate ($12.00\%$). Irregular cardboard shapes, plastic shrink-wrap reflections, and visual blending with concrete floor surfaces cause false positives and contour fragmentation.

## 4. Failure Mode Forensics: `FAIL-38` (Far-Field Detector Miss)

### 4.1 Defect Reproduction & Physics
- **Hypothesis (`CANDIDATE-38`):** Objects projected beyond $D > 8.5\,\text{m}$ from the camera experience monocular ray dilution. A human standing at $9.5\,\text{m}$ occupies fewer than $45 \times 18\,\text{pixels}$ on a $1080\text{p}$ stream, dropping below the confidence threshold ($0.50$).
- **Empirical Evidence:** In `VID_TEST_001`, out of 38 pedestrian instances observed at $D > 8.5\,\text{m}$, 12 were completely missed by the detector (recall dropped to $68.4\%$).
- **Safety Impact:** If automated braking relies on far-field perception, an oncoming fast forklift ($4.0\,\text{m/s}$) will breach the safety buffer before confirmation occurs.
- **Classification:** **CONFIRMED AS FAIL-38.**
- **Remediation & ODD Boundary:** The operational boundary of the automated intervention policy is formally restricted to $D \le 8.0\,\text{m}$. For objects between $8.0\,\text{m}$ and $10.0\,\text{m}$, the system outputs `HAZARD_ADVISORY_FAR_FIELD` but refuses safety-critical emergency braking claims without secondary sensor verification.
