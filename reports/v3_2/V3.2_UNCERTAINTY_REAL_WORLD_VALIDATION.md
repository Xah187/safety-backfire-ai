## 2. Statistical Coverage Formulation & Target Satisfaction

$$d_{\text{safe}} = \max\left(0.0, \; \hat{d} - k \cdot \sigma_d - \Delta_{\text{Jensen}}\right)$$
where $k = 2.24$ guarantees $95\%$ unilateral coverage for 2D Gaussian footpoint uncertainty, and $\Delta_{\text{Jensen}}$ corrects for the convex upward curvature of Euclidean distance.

## 3. Near-Zero Boundary Stress Testing ($d \to 0$, $\sigma \to 0$, $\sigma \to \infty$)

A major mathematical vulnerability in previous versions was the behavior of Rice/Jensen corrections as clearance distance approaches zero ($d \to 0$). Under naive Taylor expansion:
$$\Delta_{\text{Jensen}} \approx \frac{\sigma_x^2 + \sigma_y^2}{2d}$$
As $d \to 0$, the denominator collapses, causing $\Delta_{\text{Jensen}} \to \infty$, which erroneously drives the lower clearance bound to zero or creates negative distance artifacts.
