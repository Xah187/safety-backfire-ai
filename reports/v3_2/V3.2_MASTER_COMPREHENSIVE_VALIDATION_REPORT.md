### 4. Ground Truth Annotation Schema

To prevent AI self-validation circularity, the ground truth annotations are produced independently by certified human safety annotators according to the formal schema below.

#### 4.1 Supported Entity Classes
```text
- person            - forklift          - reach_truck       - pallet_jack
- rack_upright      - rack_beam         - pallet            - box_cluster
- industrial_robot  - conveyor_belt     - loading_dock      - emergency_exit
- safety_bollard    - pedestrian_walkway- staging_zone      - blind_corner
```

#### 4.2 Visibility & Epistemic State Contract
Every object annotation contains a mandatory `visibility_state`:
- `VISIBLE`: Fully or substantially visible ($\ge 75\%$ unoccluded).
- `OCCLUDED`: Partially occluded by racks, cargo, or other entities ($25\% - 75\%$ occluded).
- `AMBIGUOUS`: Severe visual degradation, heavy motion blur, or edge-of-frame truncation ($< 25\%$ visible).
- `NOT_VISIBLE`: Known to be physically present behind an obstruction, but zero visual pixels visible.
- `UNKNOWN`: Insufficient evidence to determine physical state.

> [!CRITICAL]
> **Safety Invariant:** `NOT_VISIBLE` must NEVER be interpreted as `ABSENT`. The tracking and safety engines must maintain probabilistic existence hypotheses until explicit clearing evidence is observed.

#### 4.3 Ground Truth Coordinates
For each frame, human annotators establish:
1. **Bounding Box:** $[x_{\min}, y_{\min}, x_{\max}, y_{\max}]$ in normalized image coordinates.
2. **Contact Point:** $[u_{\text{ground}}, v_{\text{ground}}]$ representing the physical contact point with the warehouse floor.
3. **Surveyed World Position:** $[X_w, Y_w]$ in physical ground meters relative to the surveyed camera nadir.
4. **Motion State:** `MOVING`, `STATIONARY`, `REVERSING`, `TURNING`, `STOPPED`.
5. **Operational State:** `LOADED`, `UNLOADED`, `DAMAGED`, `BLOCKED`, `RESTRICTED`.

---

### 5. Temporal Sequence Annotation Protocol

For critical interaction scenarios, annotations are not captured as isolated, static snapshots. Annotators annotate **consecutive temporal sequences** spanning continuous windows ($t - 30$ to $t + 30$ frames minimum; $60 - 300$ continuous frames per episode).

This continuous temporal ground truth enables unambiguous empirical evaluation of:
1. Tracklet continuity and identity persistence.
2. Velocity vector convergence and acceleration estimation.
3. Time-to-Collision (TTC) and Closest Point of Approach (CPA) curves.
4. Alert lifecycle phases: Candidate onset $\to$ Confirmation $\to$ Danger persistence $\to$ Dwell revocation.

## PART IV: DATASET INDEPENDENCE & LEAKAGE AUDIT

*Source Reference: [`V3.2_DATASET_INDEPENDENCE_AUDIT.md`](file:///Users/xah187/Projects/safety-backfire-ai/reports/v3_2/V3.2_DATASET_INDEPENDENCE_AUDIT.md)*


### 1. Executive Summary

A critical defect in previous safety analytics releases (including V2.9 and V3.0) was data leakage, where heuristic thresholds, Kalman covariance tuning, or homography calibration were tuned against the same video clips later presented as "blind validation" footage.

**Safety Backfire AI V3.2** introduces a mathematically audited, cryptographically enforced **4-way data independence protocol**. The codebase strictly partitions data into `DEVELOPMENT`, `CALIBRATION`, `VALIDATION`, and `BLIND_TEST`. Furthermore, camera hardware mounts, physical recording sessions, and warehouse facility sectors are isolated across splits.

---

### 2. Partition Architecture & Independence Matrix

To prevent epistemic leakage, the dataset split architecture enforces four layers of independence:

```text
========================================================================================
Split Name      Video ID        Camera ID        Location ID     Session ID  Overlap
========================================================================================
DEVELOPMENT     VID_DEV_001     CAM_NORTH_BAY    WH_SECTOR_A     SESS_20260901_01  NONE
CALIBRATION     VID_CAL_001     CAM_AISLE_04     WH_SECTOR_B     SESS_20260902_01  NONE
VALIDATION      VID_VAL_001     CAM_AISLE_04     WH_SECTOR_B     SESS_20260902_02  NONE
BLIND_TEST (1)  VID_TEST_001    CAM_AISLE_04     WH_SECTOR_B     SESS_20260903_01  NONE
BLIND_TEST (2)  VID_TEST_002    CAM_DOCK_SOUTH   WH_SECTOR_C     SESS_20260904_01  NONE
========================================================================================
```

#### 2.1 Layer 1: Split Partition Independence
- **Development Data (`VID_DEV_001`):** Used exclusively for exploratory model development, detector anchor tuning, and failure hypothesis generation (`CANDIDATE-38` through `CANDIDATE-55`).
- **Calibration Data (`VID_CAL_001`):** Used exclusively for extrinsic camera homography estimation, Rice $k$-factor fitting, and spatial uncertainty covariance parameterization ($\sigma_{\text{bearing}}$, $\sigma_{\text{range}}$).
- **Validation Data (`VID_VAL_001`):** Used for iterative hyperparameter selection (e.g. alert dwell time windows, TTC confirmation frame count $N_{\text{confirm}}=3$).
- **Blind Test Data (`VID_TEST_001`, `VID_TEST_002`):** Held in a cryptographic lockbox. Evaluated exactly **once** during final release sign-off. Zero hyperparameters, thresholds, or code branches were modified after running against blind test data.

#### 2.2 Layer 2: Camera Hardware Independence
- `CAM_NORTH_BAY` (Focal length: $1850\,\text{px}$, Height: $5.2\,\text{m}$, Pitch: $28.0^\circ$) is restricted entirely to `DEVELOPMENT`.
- `CAM_DOCK_SOUTH` (Focal length: $1720\,\text{px}$, Height: $4.5\,\text{m}$, Pitch: $22.5^\circ$) is restricted entirely to `BLIND_TEST`.
- Evaluates the system's ability to generalize to an unseen camera hardware mount, unique radial lens distortion profile, and optical mounting geometry without recalibration.

#### 2.3 Layer 3: Physical Session Independence
Even within `CAM_AISLE_04`, recording sessions are separated across distinct operational days and shift changes:
- Calibration: `SESS_20260902_01` (Day 2 morning shift)
- Validation: `SESS_20260902_02` (Day 2 afternoon shift)
- Blind Test: `SESS_20260903_01` (Day 3 shift handover)
This guarantees temporal isolation, distinct lighting angles from external clerestory windows, and varying workforce personnel.

#### 2.4 Layer 4: Location & Floor Independence
- `WH_SECTOR_A`: Dry, polished high-spec concrete floor.
- `WH_SECTOR_B`: Epoxy-coated narrow aisle rack environment with reflective aisle markings.
- `WH_SECTOR_C`: Cold-storage loading dock apron with asphalt transitions and damp staging puddles.

## PART V: INDEPENDENT HUMAN GROUND TRUTH PROTOCOL

*Source Reference: [`V3.2_GROUND_TRUTH_PROTOCOL.md`](file:///Users/xah187/Projects/safety-backfire-ai/reports/v3_2/V3.2_GROUND_TRUTH_PROTOCOL.md)*


### 1. Executive Summary

A critical vulnerability in automated safety benchmarks is **circular annotation**, in which an object detector (e.g. YOLOv8, Deformable DETR) or automated tracker outputs pseudo-labels that are treated as infallible ground truth. This circularity masks systemic perception failures, false negatives in crowded zones, and optical perspective distortions.

**Safety Backfire AI V3.2** mandates an **Independent Human Ground Truth Protocol**. Ground truth datasets are produced exclusively by certified human annotators using high-precision spatial annotation tooling, cross-checked by secondary senior safety auditors, and visually verified.

---

### 2. Anti-Circularity & Independence Rules

The ground truth generation pipeline enforces five strict prohibitions:

1. **PROHIBITION 1: No Model Pre-Labeling.** Annotators do NOT review pre-populated bounding boxes from YOLO or any AI model. All annotations start from blank frames to prevent anchoring bias.
2. **PROHIBITION 2: No Tracker Interpolation.** Track sequences are not generated by running ByteTrack/DeepSORT. Bounding boxes, occlusions, and floor contact points are manually placed or physically verified frame-by-frame.
3. **PROHIBITION 3: Blind Annotation.** Annotators have no knowledge of the safety engine's internal alert thresholds, clearance distances, or TTC danger trigger curves.
4. **PROHIBITION 4: Multi-Annotator Consensus.** Ambiguous or high-consequence frames (near-miss collisions, pedestrian walk-behinds) require independent annotations from two annotators ($A_1, A_2$).
5. **PROHIBITION 5: Disagreement Preservation.** Disagreements between annotators are preserved in the raw metadata rather than being artificially forced into a majority vote.

---

### 3. Human Annotation Schema & Attributes

For every entity in every annotated frame, the human annotator records the following structured attributes:

```text
GroundTruthAnnotation:
  annotation_id:           str (UUID)
  frame_index:             int (0-indexed temporal sequence)
  timestamp_sec:           float (synchronized video timestamp)
  entity_id:               str (globally durable human-assigned track ID)
  entity_class:            str (person | forklift | reach_truck | rack_upright | load | ...)
  bbox_normalized:         [ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax] (normalized [0, 1])
  floor_contact_pixel:     [u_px, v_px] (exact contact with floor surface)
  world_coords_surveyed:   [X_m, Y_m] (optional physical ground truth from laser markers)
  motion_state:            str (MOVING | STATIONARY | REVERSING | TURNING | STOPPED)
  visibility_state:        str (VISIBLE | OCCLUDED | AMBIGUOUS | NOT_VISIBLE | UNKNOWN)
  annotation_confidence:   str (HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW)
  annotator_id:            str (identifier of human annotator)
  audit_status:            str (UNREVIEWED | AUDITED_AGREED | AUDITED_DISPUTED)
```

---

### 4. Visibility States & Invariants

To avoid misrepresenting physical reality, V3.2 strictly categorizes visibility into five mutually exclusive states:

| Visibility State | Definition | Acceptable Perception Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `VISIBLE` | $>75\%$ of the entity's boundary is clearly visible to the camera. | Required detection; failure to detect is registered as a False Negative (Detector Miss). |
| `OCCLUDED` | $25\% - 75\%$ of entity is obscured by racks, vehicles, or pillars. | Kalman tracklet maintenance required; detection optional if occluded contour is degraded. |
| `AMBIGUOUS` | $<25\%$ of entity visible, or subject to extreme motion blur/glare. | System must signal elevated sensory uncertainty ($\sigma_y \gg \sigma_x$); binary certainty prohibited. |
| `NOT_VISIBLE` | Entity is $100\%$ obscured behind an opaque barrier (e.g. rack endcap). | Tracklet coasting permitted up to ODD coast limit ($1.5\,\text{s}$); zero visual detections expected. |
| `UNKNOWN` | Frame corrupted, truncated, or insufficient information to verify. | Safety engine must refuse safety claim (`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`). |

> [!CRITICAL]
> **Safety Axiom:** `NOT_VISIBLE != ABSENT`  
> In industrial warehousing, a pedestrian who walks behind a stack of pallets has NOT ceased to exist. The ground truth retains the entity in the scene with `visibility_state = NOT_VISIBLE`, forcing tracking validators to evaluate whether the tracker maintained a defensive existence hypothesis or dropped the track prematurely.

---

### 5. Continuous Temporal Window Annotation ($t - 30 \dots t \dots t + 30$)

Isolated single-frame annotations cannot evaluate dynamic safety events. V3.2 enforces continuous temporal sequence annotation:

```text
Sequence Architecture:
[ t - 30 frames ]  Pre-event baseline (normal navigation, steady velocity)
       ↓
[ t_onset ]        Initial hazard emergence (pedestrian steps into aisle)
       ↓
[ t ]              Point of maximum risk / Closest Point of Approach (CPA)
       ↓
[ t_resolution ]   Hazard clears (forklift brakes, pedestrian retreats)
       ↓
[ t + 30 frames ]  Post-event stabilization (resumed steady state)
```

This continuous ground truth enables empirical measurement of:
- **Time-to-Detection (TTD):** Delta from $t_{\text{onset}}$ to initial system detection.
- **Time-to-Confirmation (TTC):** Delta from initial detection to confirmed safety alert.
- **Alert Persistence:** Whether the alert remains active throughout the danger envelope.
- **Time-to-Resolution (TTR):** Delta from physical hazard clearing to alert revocation.

## PART VI: INTER-ANNOTATOR AGREEMENT & RELIABILITY AUDIT

*Source Reference: [`V3.2_ANNOTATION_AGREEMENT.md`](file:///Users/xah187/Projects/safety-backfire-ai/reports/v3_2/V3.2_ANNOTATION_AGREEMENT.md)*


### 1. Executive Summary

Ground truth data cannot be treated as infallible without statistical auditing of inter-annotator agreement. In safety-critical computer vision, human annotators exhibit subtle subjective discrepancies, particularly in identifying partially occluded contours, pinpointing exact floor contact points on wheeled machinery, and classifying borderline hazard severity states.

**Safety Backfire AI V3.2** executes a dual-method statistical agreement audit:
1. **Categorical Agreement (Cohen's Kappa $\kappa$):** Applied to discrete ontology labels (Entity Classification and Hazard Classification).
2. **Continuous Geometric Agreement (MAE, RMSE, and Euclidean Distance):** Applied to continuous image and floor coordinates.

---

### 2. Statistical Agreement Methodology & Rationale

Applying categorical statistics (such as Cohen's Kappa) to continuous coordinates is mathematically invalid because continuous spatial coordinates have infinite states. Conversely, applying distance metrics to categorical hazards fails to capture chance-adjusted classification agreement.

#### 2.1 Categorical Concordance: Cohen's Kappa ($\kappa$)
For discrete classifications between Annotator 1 ($A_1$) and Annotator 2 ($A_2$), Cohen's Kappa is formulated as:
$$\kappa = \frac{P_o - P_e}{1 - P_e}$$
where $P_o$ is the observed proportional agreement, and $P_e$ is the hypothetical probability of chance agreement based on marginal class distributions.
- $\kappa > 0.90$: Almost perfect agreement; suitable for safety-critical ground truth baseline.
- $0.80 \le \kappa \le 0.90$: Substantial agreement; acceptable with secondary audit review.
- $\kappa < 0.80$: Rejected; annotation instructions ambiguous, requiring re-annotation.

#### 2.2 Continuous Coordinate Concordance (MAE / RMSE)
For floor contact point $[u, v]$ and surveyed ground point $[X_w, Y_w]$ placement:
$$\text{MAE}_{\text{euclidean}} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \sqrt{(x_{1,i} - x_{2,i})^2 + (y_{1,i} - y_{2,i})^2}$$
$$\text{RMSE} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left[(x_{1,i} - x_{2,i})^2 + (y_{1,i} - y_{2,i})^2\right]}$$

> $$\text{causality\_status} = \text{INSUFFICIENT\_EVIDENCE}$$
