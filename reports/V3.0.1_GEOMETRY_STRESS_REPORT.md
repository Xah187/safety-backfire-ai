# V3.0.1 Empirical Geometry Adversarial & Sensitivity Report

## Executive Summary

The V3.0 Geometry Engine introduced Method E to compensate for elevated camera perspective distortions on seated operators and vehicles (`FAIL-10`). In V3.0.1, the geometry module was audited for mathematical validity, naming precision, sensitivity across 100 physical camera configurations, and uncertainty propagation.

### Key Discoveries & Audit Results:
1. **Fixed Pixel Offset Sensitivity Breakdown (`FAIL-19`)**: The hardcoded $28\,\text{px}$ downward elevation shift breaks down dramatically across varying camera mount heights ($3.0 - 5.0\,\text{m}$), pitch angles ($20^\circ - 35^\circ$), and operator platform elevations ($0.4 - 1.5\,\text{m}$). Across 100 evaluated configurations, **100% of conditions exhibited residual pixel errors $> 25\,\text{px}$**, producing ground positioning errors exceeding $1.5\,\text{m}$.
2. **Technical Naming Audit**: Method E was previously referred to loosely as "3D Bounding Box Offset". An exhaustive code audit confirmed that the implementation is mathematically **"2D Perspective Elevation Compensation with Ground Homography"**, as it applies a heuristic 2D pixel translation followed by a planar homography, rather than full 3D oriented bounding box fitting.
3. **Confidence Inversion Bug Resolved (`FAIL-21`)**: Method E previously assigned a hardcoded confidence of $0.85$ when ankles were occluded, higher than clear ankle detections ($0.65$). This inversion was corrected, capping occluded estimates at $0.50$ and outputting explicit physical uncertainties.
4. **Physical Ground Truth Integrity**: In accordance with safety auditing standards, because physically surveyed laser distance ground truth is unavailable in the current video corpus, the metric error is declared **`REAL-WORLD METRIC ERROR = NOT COMPUTABLE`**.

## 2. Technical Naming & Architecture Audit

An architectural audit of `safety_backfire_v3/core/geometry_v3.py` was conducted to verify algorithmic naming integrity:

```
[AUDIT VERIFICATION]
Previous Label:     "Method E: 3D Bounding Box Vehicle Offset"
Code Implementation: Downward pixel translation (delta_y) + Planar Homography Matrix H
Audited Technical:  "2D Perspective Elevation Compensation with Ground Homography"
Status:             Alias METHOD_E_PERSPECTIVE_ELEVATION_OFFSET established in core.
```

The system does not solve 3D pose via PnP or 3D bounding box regression (e.g., Smoke or PointPillars). Using "3D Bounding Box" was inaccurate. The corrected technical name is strictly enforced in documentation and API aliases.

## 3. Geometric Uncertainty Propagation

Industrial safety standards forbid displaying point-estimate distances as absolute truth when underlying sensory data is degraded.

### Uncertainty Model
The V3.0.1 Geometry Engine outputs explicit covariance-derived uncertainty bounds:
$$\text{Distance} = d_{\text{nominal}} \pm u_d$$
where $u_d$ incorporates keypoint visibility, camera pitch vibration, and homography conditioning.

#### Empirical Verification Cases
| Case | Keypoint State | Estimated Ground Position | Confidence Score | Position Uncertainty ($\pm \text{m}$) | Distance Uncertainty ($\pm \text{m}$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Case 1: Clear Ankles** | Both ankles detected ($\text{conf} > 0.85$) | $(1.84, 4.02)\,\text{m}$ | **0.91** | $\pm 0.13\,\text{m}$ | $\pm 0.15\,\text{m}$ |
| **Case 2: Occluded Ankles** | Ankles occluded; Method E fallback | $(2.71, 5.61)\,\text{m}$ | **0.50** | $\pm 0.75\,\text{m}$ | $\pm 0.83\,\text{m}$ |

*Resolution of `FAIL-21`*: In Case 2, the old system assigned confidence $0.85$ despite complete ankle occlusion. V3.0.1 caps occluded confidence at $0.50$ and surfaces the large uncertainty margin ($\pm 0.83\,\text{m}$), preventing false precision in the downstream safety engine.

## 4. Physical Ground Truth Audit

To prevent self-fulfilling or synthetic validation traps:
- In synthetic simulations, homography reproduction yields an algebraic RMSE of $0.062\,\text{m}$.
- However, **no physical laser surveyor or LiDAR ground truth exists for the warehouse recordings (`test1.mp4`, `test2.mp4`)**.
- **Audit Mandate**: Synthetic accuracy cannot be substituted for physical accuracy.
