## 2. Invariants Strictly Preserved in V3.0.2

1. **`PREDICTED != NEW_SAFETY_EVIDENCE`**: Extrapolated tracker positions cannot initiate or confirm new hazard events.
2. **`UNKNOWN != SUPPRESS`**: Ambiguous operator classification never suppresses proximity warnings.
3. **`INVALID != SAFETY_ELIGIBLE`**: Observations failing integrity gates cannot enter risk calculations.
4. **`STALE != SAFETY_INPUT`**: Poses older than $150\,\text{ms}$ or with skew $> 100\,\text{ms}$ are strictly forbidden from operator suppression.
5. **`WORST_CASE != AUTOMATIC_CONFIRMED`**: Large uncertainty alone does not trigger blind emergency stops; kinematic convergence is required.

## 4. Known Core Limitations & Operational Boundaries

Before field deployment, operations must adhere to these established boundaries:

1. **Camera Extrinsic Calibration Requirement**: Sites must furnish mounting height, downward pitch angle, and focal length. Uncalibrated cameras will experience degraded confidence ($-0.25$) and wider caution intervals.
2. **Mechanical Pitch Vibration Threshold**: Camera pitch vibrations exceeding $\pm 1.5^\circ$ or unknown vehicle cab height discrepancies exceeding $\pm 0.2\,\text{m}$ produce $> 0.40\,\text{m}$ ground error, requiring hardware vibration dampeners.
3. **Severe Chromatic Degradation**: Under extreme monochromatic or sodium-vapor warehouse lighting, appearance-based ReID similarity degrades, safely forcing entity splits after 3.0s of occlusion.
