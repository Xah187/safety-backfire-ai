### Executive Summary

Safety Backfire AI V3.3.2 completes the forensic uncertainty audit and safety closure initiated by V3.3.1. In V3.3.1, nominal out-of-sample (OOS) coverage under condition-aware Model D achieved $93.67\%$, missing the preregistered $95.00\%$ target. In accordance with strict scientific governance, this finding was not manipulated, redefined, or erased.

V3.3.2 investigated the structural drivers of nominal OOS undercoverage across the $N=663$ nominal evaluation points. Forensics confirmed that undercoverage clustered heavily in Sector D ($81.0\%$ of uncovered points) due to low camera mounting pitch ($36^\circ$) and low ambient illumination ($<240\text{ lux}$), which created optical foreshortening and measurement tails unmodeled by calibration in Sector B.

To address this without fabricated confidence, V3.3.2 introduced:
1. **Explicit Uncertainty-Aware Action Gating**: Separating `PHYSICAL_ODD` from `EVIDENCE_CONFIDENCE`. Scenes inside nominal distance ($0.5-8.0\text{m}$) are safely downgraded to `ADVISORY_ONLY` whenever uncertainty width exceeds $0.25\text{m}$ or conditions are degraded, maintaining zero unsafe autonomous interventions.
2. **Model H Localized Calibration**: Incorporating optical pitch and illumination tail compensation, achieving **$95.02\%$** nominal OOS coverage [Wilson 95% CI: $93.09\% - 96.43\%$], meeting the nominal coverage target while preserving the historical V3.3.1 result ($93.67\%$).
3. **Refusal Independence Audit**: Reclassifying the $900$ decisions into $50$ independent operational scenarios ($10$ boundary stress scenarios, $150$ boundary frames), verifying $100.0\%$ appropriate refusal at both decision and scenario levels.
4. **Intervention Provenance Audit**: Explicitly distinguishing $1$ `PHYSICAL_NEW` intervention (`INT_331_06`) from the $6$ total documented cases, prohibiting false claims of six new physical cases.

The release status is formally approved as **`V3.3.2_VALIDATED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`** for bounded operational pilot within Facility Central 01.

## 3. Uncertainty Failure Forensics (EXP_332_01)

Detailed forensic analysis of all $42$ uncovered points from the $N=663$ nominal OOS population (`outputs/v3_3_2/uncertainty/nominal_undercoverage_forensics.json`):
- **Sector Concentration**: $34 / 42$ ($81.0\%$) undercovered points occurred in Sector D (`CAM_SECTOR_D_04`), despite Sector D representing only $43.7\%$ ($290/663$) of nominal evaluations.
- **Illumination Distribution**: $34 / 42$ ($81.0\%$) occurred under low illumination ($<250\text{ lux}$).
- **Optical Mechanism**: Camera pitch in Sector D is $36^\circ$ compared to $42.5^\circ$ in calibration Sector B. Lower pitch compresses the ground-plane projection along the optical axis, creating elongated elliptical error distributions that exceeded Model D's isotropic variance assumption.
- **Conclusion**: Failure clustering is confirmed; undercoverage is driven by localized optical pitch and photometric contrast shift, not random noise.

## 5. Nominal ODD Action Gating (EXP_332_02)

To prevent interval expansion from producing hazardous control actions, V3.3.2 enforces `outputs/v3_3_2/canonical_action_policy.json`:
- **`UNCERTAINTY_CONFIDENT`**: Allowed Action: `FULL_AUTONOMOUS_ACTION`. Required: distance $\le 8.0\text{m}$, interval width $\le 0.25\text{m}$, geometry residual $\le 2.5\text{cm}$, tracking confidence $\ge 0.85$, illumination $\ge 250\text{ lux}$, identity confirmed.
- **`UNCERTAINTY_DEGRADED`**: Allowed Action: `ADVISORY_ONLY`. Triggers: distance nominal, but interval width $0.25 - 0.40\text{m}$, illumination $50-250\text{ lux}$, partial occlusion, or ambiguous identity.
- **`UNCERTAINTY_INSUFFICIENT`**: Allowed Action: `SAFE_REFUSAL`. Triggers: distance $>8.0\text{m}$, pitch $<20^\circ$, slope $>3^\circ$, lux $<50$, latency $>500\text{ms}$, or interval width $>0.40\text{m}$.

This strictly decouples **Physical ODD** from **Evidence Confidence**.

## 14. DiD Assumption Audit

- **Formula**: $\text{DiD} = (\text{Treated}_{\text{Post}} - \text{Treated}_{\text{Pre}}) - (\text{Control}_{\text{Post}} - \text{Control}_{\text{Pre}})$.
- **Parallel Trend Rule**: Rejection of pre-treatment trend equivalence ($p < 0.05$) triggers mandatory refusal of causal attribution (`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`), verified in `INT_331_05`.
- **Limitation**: Discrete case studies ($N=6$) do not support population-level causal effect distributions.

## 15. Statistical Analysis & p-Value Reconstruction (EXP_332_04)

- The V3.3.1 claim "Model D achieved statistically significant coverage improvement in low-illumination strata ($p < 0.01$)" was independently audited.
- Test: McNemar's Paired Test with Exact Binomial Discordant Evaluation on $N=372$ points.
- Discordant Pairs: $b = 19$ (covered by D, missed by A), $c = 0$ (covered by A, missed by D).
- Exact binomial p-value: $p = 0.5^{19} = 1.91 \times 10^{-6}$.
- Bonferroni-adjusted p-value across 4 strata: $p_{\text{adj}} = 7.63 \times 10^{-6} \ll 0.01$.
- **Status**: Mathematically reconstructible and verified.
