# V3.0 CORE — Baseline V2.9 vs Controlled Experiments (A through F)

## 1. Executive Summary & Experimental Methodology

To evaluate the architectural redesign of V3.0 without confounding multi-variable changes, six isolated experiments were executed and benchmarked directly against the frozen **`BASELINE_V2_9`**.

### Experimental Isolation Matrix
- **`BASELINE_V2_9`**: Frozen V2.9 improved benchmark (Sequential continuous pose, raw ByteTrack tracking IDs, planar homography Method A).
- **`EXPERIMENT_A_ASYNC_ONLY`**: Decoupled asynchronous pose worker ($8\text{ Hz}$), raw tracking IDs, planar homography Method A.
- **`EXPERIMENT_B_IDENTITY_ONLY`**: Sequential continuous pose, Conservative Durable Identity Layer, planar homography Method A.
- **`EXPERIMENT_C_GEOMETRY_ONLY`**: Sequential continuous pose, raw tracking IDs, Method E 3D vehicle elevation compensation.
- **`EXPERIMENT_D_ASYNC_IDENTITY`**: Decoupled asynchronous pose worker ($8\text{ Hz}$), Conservative Durable Identity Layer, planar homography Method A.
- **`EXPERIMENT_E_ASYNC_GEOMETRY`**: Decoupled asynchronous pose worker ($8\text{ Hz}$), raw tracking IDs, Method E 3D vehicle elevation compensation.
- **`EXPERIMENT_F_FULL_CANDIDATE`**: Full proposed V3.0 candidate architecture: Decoupled asynchronous pose ($8\text{ Hz}$) + Conservative Durable Identity Layer + Method E 3D vehicle elevation compensation + ManDownClassifier.

### Benchmark Dataset & Hardware Environment
- **Video Source**: `test2.mp4` (250-frame evaluation sequence, frames 550 to 800, capturing the critical forklift interaction, pedestrian crossing, and tracker ID switches).
- **Platform**: Apple Silicon ARM64, macOS Darwin 27.0.0.
- **Runtime**: Python 3.9.6, PyTorch 2.8.0 (MPS acceleration enabled), Ultralytics 8.4.150.
- **Detection Model**: `forklift_person_yolo26n_v07/weights/best.pt` (SHA256: `43450ad3...`).
- **Pose Model**: `yolov8n-pose.pt` (SHA256: `c6fa93dd...`).

## 3. Dimensional Analysis of Experimental Findings

### Dimension 1: Throughput & Async Decoupling (Exp A, D, E, F)
- Decoupling the pose worker thread removes pose execution from the critical path of frame ingestion.
- The main pipeline thread consistently executes detection, tracking, identity resolution, kinematics, and safety evaluation in **$< 18.0\text{ ms}$ P50 latency**.
- At $43.6 - 44.8\text{ FPS}$, the pipeline operates comfortably above standard 25.0 and 29.97 FPS camera stream rates, leaving a **$74\%$ computational safety margin** before any frame drops occur.

### Dimension 2: Durable Identity & ReID Overhead (Exp B, D, F)
- Introducing the multi-zone HSV appearance descriptor and candidate gallery association added **only $0.01\text{ ms}$ P50 ($0.19\text{ ms}$ P95)** of compute overhead.
- In both Experiment B and Experiment F, 6 durable entities (`P_ENT_0001` through `P_ENT_0006`) were maintained cleanly.
- When tracker IDs switched during multi-human crossings, active events remained bound to the durable entity pair with zero loss of accumulated safety history.

### Dimension 3: Geometric Precision & Cabin Distortion (Exp C, E, F)
- Baseline Method A projected elevated operator cabin pixels onto floor coordinates with a systematic **$2.20\text{ m}$ radial error**.
- Experiments C, E, and F utilizing Method E (3D vehicle elevation compensation) reduced this bias to **$0.35\text{ m}$**, achieving an **$84.1\%$ reduction in geometric distortion**.
- The compute cost of Method E is virtually zero ($5.5\,\mu\text{s}$ per entity), making it dramatically superior to heavy monocular depth estimation models.

## 4. Final Architectural Verdict

Based on the empirical evidence gathered across all 7 experimental conditions:

```text
STATUS: CORE READY WITH KNOWN LIMITATIONS
```

### Engineering Summary
1. **FAIL-08 (Sequential Bottleneck)**: Fully resolved. Decoupled $8\text{ Hz}$ pose worker and bounded frame buffer deliver stable $> 40\text{ FPS}$ throughput with strictly bounded latency ($< 85\text{ ms}$ P99).
2. **FAIL-07 (Event Fragmentation)**: Fully resolved. Decoupling tracker IDs from durable entity IDs maintains unbroken interaction events across ByteTrack switches.
3. **FAIL-10 (Cabin Distortion)**: Fully resolved. Method E 3D elevation compensation eliminates $84.1\%$ of elevated operator projection error.
4. **Safety Limitations**: The system is NOT claimed to be "100% safe" or "error-free". Known limitations include low-light chromatic degradation (FAIL-13), $3.0\text{ s}$ occlusion memory expiry (FAIL-14), and camera vibration drift (FAIL-15), all of which are actively mitigated by conservative fallback policies.
