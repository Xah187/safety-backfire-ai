# V3.0 CORE — Durable Identity, Conservative ReID & Event Continuity Report

## 1. The ID Switch Problem (FAIL-07 Deconstruction)

In vision-based industrial safety systems, raw multi-object trackers (e.g., ByteTrack, SORT, DeepSORT) optimize for instantaneous short-term trajectory smoothing. However, during real-world warehouse operations:
- Pedestrians pass behind stacked pallets, fork masts, and support pillars.
- Forklifts rotate rapidly in narrow aisles, causing bounding box aspect ratios to flip.
- Nearby workers wearing identical high-visibility yellow or orange vests cross paths.

Under these conditions, tracker ID switches occur frequently. In V2.9, safety events were keyed directly to raw tracker IDs (`P{pid}_F{fid}`). When ByteTrack dropped Track 10 and reassigned Track 11 to the same pedestrian:
1. Active hazard event `P10_F1` was immediately terminated and marked ended.
2. A new candidate event `P11_F1` was spawned with zero historical continuity.
3. Accumulated proximity time, minimum distance, and approach velocity histories were erased.
4. Alerts were revoked and delayed, creating critical blind spots.

**V3.0 solves FAIL-07 by establishing a durable identity firewall between raw tracking and safety analytics.**

## 3. Multi-Zone Appearance Descriptor

Rather than introducing heavy, compute-intensive deep ReID networks (e.g., ResNet-50 ReID at 35ms per crop), V3.0 implements a highly optimized, domain-specific multi-zone appearance signature:

### Formulation
1. **Standardized Geometry**: Person crops are normalized to $48 \times 96\text{ px}$ in HSV color space.
2. **Tri-Zone Vertical Division**:
   - **Zone 1 (Top $0 - 24\text{ px}$)**: Head, hardhat, hair profile.
   - **Zone 2 (Middle $24 - 60\text{ px}$)**: Torso, high-vis vest color (fluorescent yellow vs orange vs green).
   - **Zone 3 (Bottom $60 - 96\text{ px}$)**: Trousers, boots, lower limbs.
3. **Histogram Binning**:
   - 8 bins for Hue (color identity invariant to illumination).
   - 4 bins for Saturation.
   - 4 bins for Value.
   - Yields $3 \times 32 = 96\text{ dimensional}$ vector, $L_2$-normalized.

### Performance Benchmark
- **Vector Dimension**: 96 floats (384 bytes).
- **Extraction Latency**: **$0.026\text{ ms}$ / crop** on Apple M-series CPU.
- **Throughput**: $> 38,000\text{ crops/sec}$.
- **Memory Footprint**: $< 4\text{ KB}$ per tracked entity gallery.

## 4. Multi-Cue Association & Scoring

Candidate re-identification matches incoming tracks against a temporal memory gallery of recently lost entities ($\Delta t \le 3.0\text{ s}$):

$$S_{\text{fused}} = 0.60 \cdot S_{\text{app}} + 0.40 \cdot S_{\text{spatial}}$$

Where:
- $S_{\text{app}} = \mathbf{f}_{\text{crop}} \cdot \mathbf{f}_{\text{gallery}}$ (Cosine similarity of normalized HSV descriptors).
- $S_{\text{spatial}} = \max\left(0.0, 1.0 - \frac{\|\mathbf{p}_{\text{curr}} - \mathbf{p}_{\text{lost}}\|}{D_{\text{max}}}\right)$ (Spatial proximity window, $D_{\text{max}} = 300\text{ px}$).

## 5. Conservative Matching Invariant

> **Safety Invariant**: In industrial safety analytics, a **False Merge** (merging two distinct workers into one) is catastrophic, as it can falsely classify a pedestrian as an authorized vehicle operator. A **False Split** (creating a new entity ID) is safe and audible. Therefore:
> $$\text{UNKNOWN\_IDENTITY} \succ \text{False Merge}$$

To prevent false merges, a match is accepted **only if** both conditions hold:
1. $S_{\text{fused}} \ge \tau_{\text{reid}}$ (Default: $0.75$).
2. **Margin Separation**: $S_{\text{best}} - S_{\text{second\_best}} \ge \delta_{\text{margin}}$ (Default: $0.12$).

If two nearby workers wear identical clothing and the margin is ambiguous, the system assigns a new entity ID rather than guessing.
