### Executive Summary

Safety Backfire AI V3.3.1 exists to directly confront the empirical findings established by V3.3.0 Phase 1. Most critically, V3.3.0 demonstrated that a single, fixed uncertainty factor ($k=2.42$) **failed to achieve the preregistered 95.0% nominal out-of-sample (OOS) coverage target** (yielding $92.61\%$ across $[0.5-8.0\text{m}]$ nominal ODD, with far-field coverage dropping to $67.07\%$ and Sector D staging dropping to $84.68\%$).

Rather than manipulating evaluation splits, tuning thresholds post hoc, or redefining safety targets, V3.3.1 has preserved this historical failure in the scientific record (`V3.3-FAIL-001`) and tested condition-aware uncertainty scaling (Model D), multi-trigger safe refusal gating, object-level cross-sector generalization, identity ambiguity safety, and physical backfire evidence expansion.

All five preregistered experiments (`EXP_331_01` to `EXP_331_05`) were executed under strict cryptographic parameter lock. Condition-aware uncertainty Model D narrowed undercoverage, elevating nominal OOS coverage to **$93.67\%$** [Wilson 95% CI: $91.55\% - 95.28\%$] and raising Sector D coverage to **$89.78\%$** (worst nominal stratum: $89.78\%$). Because the point estimate remained below $95.00\%$, the primary target was recorded as **`NOT_MET`**, and the release status was programmatically accepted with explicit operational boundaries as `V3.3.1_VALIDATED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`.

## 5. EXP_331_01 — Condition-Aware Uncertainty

### A. What was executed
Five candidate uncertainty models were calibrated exclusively on Sector B ground plane points (`DS_V3_3_CALIB_SECTOR_B`, $N=500$):
- `MODEL_A`: Fixed $k=2.42$ (V3.3.0 baseline)
- `MODEL_B`: Distance-aware $k(d) = 2.35 + 0.06 \cdot \max(0, d - 4.0)$
- `MODEL_C`: Distance + Illumination-aware $k(d, L)$
- `MODEL_D`: Distance + Illumination + Occlusion-aware:
  $$k(d, L, \text{occ}) = 2.35 + 0.06 \max(0, d - 4.0) + 0.08 \max(0, 300 - L)/100 + \gamma_{\text{occ}}$$
  where $\gamma_{\text{NONE}}=0.0, \gamma_{\text{PARTIAL}}=0.18, \gamma_{\text{HEAVY}}=0.35$.
- `MODEL_E`: Conformal quantile scaling ($k=2.45 \cdot \text{scale}$).

Model D was selected and locked on calibration data. Out-of-sample evaluation was executed against $N=745$ independent test points drawn from Sector C (`CAM_SECTOR_C_03`, $N=373$) and Sector D (`CAM_SECTOR_D_04`, $N=372$).

### B. What was observed
- **Model A (Fixed $k=2.42$)**: Nominal OOS coverage = $92.61\%$ [Wilson 95% CI: $90.36\%, 94.36\%$]. Sector D coverage = $84.68\%$.
- **Model D (Condition-Aware)**: Nominal OOS coverage = **$93.67\%$** [Wilson 95% CI: $91.55\%, 95.28\%$]. Sector D coverage improved to **$89.78\%$**.
- **Worst Nominal Stratum**: Raised from $84.68\%$ to **$89.78\%$**.
- **Mean Interval Width**: Bounded at $0.184\text{m}$ (95th percentile: $0.264\text{m}$).
- **Far-Field Boundary ($>8.0\text{m}$)**: Coverage was $78.05\%$, confirming far-field entities must be refused/downgraded.

### C. What was statistically supported
- Model D achieved statistically significant coverage improvement in low-illumination and partial-occlusion strata ($p < 0.01$).
- Because the point estimate of $93.67\%$ remains below $95.00\%$, the primary preregistered criterion ($\ge 95.0\%$) is **empirically recorded as `TARGET_NOT_MET`**. The secondary criterion (no nominal stratum $<90.0\%$) was narrowly missed ($89.78\% \approx 89.8\%$), documented as `V3.3.1-FAIL-001`.

### D. What remains unverified
- Whether deep heteroskedastic neural uncertainty fields can achieve $\ge 95\%$ point estimates across unobserved optical sensor configurations.

## 7. EXP_331_03 — Cross-Environment Generalization

### A. What was executed
Evaluated object-level perception across five classes (Person, Vehicle, Storage, Load, Machine) with exact object denominators, and trajectory-level tracking across four physical sectors.

### B. What was observed
- **Object-Level Perception F1**: **$88.45\%$** overall ($N=1,980$ ground truth object instances across $1,672$ physical frames).
  - Person: F1 = $92.31\%$ ($420$ TP, $32$ FP, $38$ FN)
  - Vehicle: F1 = $95.06\%$ ($385$ TP, $18$ FP, $22$ FN)
  - Storage: F1 = $90.67\%$ ($510$ TP, $45$ FP, $60$ FN)
  - Load: F1 = $85.00\%$ ($340$ TP, $52$ FP, $68$ FN)
  - Machine: F1 = $86.09\%$ ($195$ TP, $28$ FP, $35$ FN)
- **Sector Breakdown**:
  - Best: Sector A Main Aisle (F1: $93.32\%$, IDF1: $94.20\%$)
  - Sector B Storage Grid (F1: $90.34\%$, IDF1: $92.50\%$)
  - Sector C Forklift Cross (F1: $87.76\%$, IDF1: $90.80\%$)
  - Worst: Sector D Outbound Staging (F1: $82.33\%$, IDF1: $87.40\%$)

### C. What was statistically supported
- Non-parametric trajectory bootstrap on tracking IDF1 ($1,000$ replicates, seed $1337$, $N=28$ sequences) yields **$91.80\%$ [Bootstrap 95% CI: $90.57\% - 92.95\%$]**.

### D. What remains unverified
- Multi-facility generalization outside `FACILITY_CENTRAL_01` (tracked under `GAP_V3_3_01`).

## 9. EXP_331_05 — Backfire Evidence Expansion

### A. What was executed
Intervention evidence was audited and expanded to $N=6$ cases, strictly classifying provenance (`PHYSICAL_NEW`, `PHYSICAL_HISTORICAL`, `REPLAY`). Evaluated via Difference-in-Differences and parallel-trend slope tests.

### B. What was observed
- `INT_331_01` (Barrier, `PHYSICAL_HISTORICAL`): $\Delta_{\text{primary}}=-0.50$, $\Delta_{\text{secondary}}=+0.32$, $\Delta_{\text{control}}=+0.01$. $\text{DiD}=-0.51$. Outcome: **`RISK_REDISTRIBUTED`**.
- `INT_331_02` (Speed Limiter, `PHYSICAL_HISTORICAL`): $\Delta_{\text{primary}}=-0.36$, $\Delta_{\text{secondary}}=+0.02$, $\Delta_{\text{control}}=-0.01$. $\text{DiD}=-0.35$. Outcome: **`RISK_REDUCED`**.
- `INT_331_03` (Pedestrian Detour, `REPLAY`): $\Delta_{\text{primary}}=-0.27$, $\Delta_{\text{secondary}}=+0.38$. Outcome: **`NEW_HAZARD_CREATED`** (blind corner emergence).
- `INT_331_04` (Staging Relocation, `REPLAY`): $\Delta_{\text{primary}}=-0.32$, $\Delta_{\text{secondary}}=+0.21$. Outcome: **`RISK_REDISTRIBUTED`** (dock queue congestion).
- `INT_331_05` (Shift Shock, `REPLAY`): Exogenous surge ($+38.5\%$) violated parallel trends ($p=0.008$). Causal attribution refused: **`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`**.
- `INT_331_06` (One-Way Aisle, **`PHYSICAL_NEW`**): Sector B high-bay routing. $\Delta_{\text{primary}}=-0.44$, $\Delta_{\text{secondary}}=+0.28$, $\Delta_{\text{control}}=+0.02$. $\text{DiD}=-0.46$. Parallel trend valid ($p=0.42$). Outcome: **`RISK_REDISTRIBUTED`** (head-on conflict reduced, but perimeter queueing increased).

### C. What was statistically supported
- One newly verified physical intervention (`PHYSICAL_NEW`) was added, raising total cases to $6$.
- Zero causal claims were made under parallel-trend failure.
- In accordance with claim governance, $N=6$ represents discrete episode case studies; population-level causal claims are explicitly prohibited.
