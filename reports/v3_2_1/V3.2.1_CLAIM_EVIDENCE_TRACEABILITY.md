## 3. Disallowed Overclaims and Retractions

The following statements were audited, disallowed, and formally retracted from the release:
1. **RETRACTED:** "The system provides an unconditional mathematical guarantee of 95% safety coverage."  
   *Correction:* Coverage is an empirically calibrated factor under bivariate Gaussian assumptions.
2. **RETRACTED:** "Safety Backfire detector has 100% population-level general accuracy."  
   *Correction:* Two real intervention cases represent an empirical proof-of-concept demonstration, not a population-level accuracy score.
3. **RETRACTED:** "False alarm rate is 1.20 events per hour over 2.5 hours."  
   *Correction:* The 2.5-hour duration was an unverified modeling assumption. Over the actual 23.0-minute corpus, the false alarm rate is $7.83\,\text{events/hour}$ (and $5.45\,\text{events/hour}$ in the 11.0-minute blind test partition).
