# V3.2.1 — Event-Level Metric Audit & Rate Statistics Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This report establishes the audited, mathematically reconciled event-level safety metrics for **Safety Backfire AI V3.2.1**. Event-level validation represents the primary operational metric of the system, measuring continuous hazard episodes rather than isolated frame detections.

The audit resolved two critical defects from the V3.2 baseline:
1. **Removal of the Unsupported 2.5-Hour Denominator:** The $2.5\,\text{hour}$ figure was an unverified modeling assumption. It has been replaced with the verified durations: **$23.0\,\text{minutes}$ ($0.3833\,\text{hours}$)** for the full corpus and **$11.0\,\text{minutes}$ ($0.1833\,\text{hours}$)** for the blind test partition.
2. **Replacement of Binomial Confidence Intervals with Poisson Count-Rate Intervals:** Events per hour represent a Poisson arrival process. Applying a binomial Wilson score interval to a count-rate metric is statistically invalid. V3.2.1 implements the **Exact Garwood Chi-Square Poisson Confidence Interval**.

---

## 2. Reconciled Event Rate Mathematics

For an observed event count $X$ over continuous observation duration $T$ (in hours), the point estimate rate is:
$$\hat{\lambda} = \frac{X}{T}$$
The exact $100(1 - \alpha)\%$ two-sided Poisson confidence interval $[\lambda_{\text{lower}}, \lambda_{\text{upper}}]$ is derived from the Chi-Square distribution:
$$\lambda_{\text{lower}} = \frac{\chi^2_{2X, \, \alpha/2}}{2T}$$
$$\lambda_{\text{upper}} = \frac{\chi^2_{2(X + 1), \, 1 - \alpha/2}}{2T}$$

### 4.1 Fragmentation and Duplication Audit
- **Fragmented Events:** $2$ instances in full corpus ($1$ in blind test). Both instances were traced to prolonged occlusions ($> 45\,\text{frames}$), cataloged and remediated under `FAIL-41`.
- **Duplicated Events:** $1$ instance in full corpus ($0$ in blind test). Occurred during a near-miss where overlapping entity pairs generated duplicate episode triggers; resolved via disjoint pair grouping.
- **Partial Events:** $0$ instances. In all true positive events, the temporal overlap with human ground truth satisfied $\text{IoU} \ge 0.50$.
