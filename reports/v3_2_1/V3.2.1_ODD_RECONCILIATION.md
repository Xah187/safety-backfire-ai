# V3.2.1 — Operational Design Domain (ODD) Reconciliation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This report delivers the official forensic audit and statistical reconciliation of the **Operational Design Domain (ODD)** specification for Safety Backfire AI V3.2.1. The audit resolved two critical structural contradictions identified in the V3.2 baseline:
1. **Critical Issue #2 (Camera Pitch Contradiction):** The blind-test dataset included `CAM_DOCK_SOUTH` with a mounting pitch of $22.5^\circ$, while the ODD table simultaneously defined the certified pitch range as $35.0^\circ - 55.0^\circ$.
2. **Critical Issue #3 (Distance Range Ambiguity):** Discrepancies between reports stating $8.0\,\text{m}$ as an operational failure boundary versus $10.0\,\text{m}$ as an out-of-domain threshold.

---

## 2. Critical Issue #2: Camera Pitch ODD Reconciliation

### 2.1 The Contradiction
- In `V3.2_DATASET_SPEC.md`, `CAM_DOCK_SOUTH` was cataloged with:
  $$\text{camera\_pitch} = 22.5^\circ$$
- In `odd_metrics.json` and `V3.2_ODD_VALIDATION.md`, the certified ODD pitch was specified as:
  $$\text{valid\_range} = [35.0^\circ, 55.0^\circ]$$
- **Forensic Question:** How could `CAM_DOCK_SOUTH` simultaneously be hailed as an independent blind-test validation camera while physically violating the nominal ODD boundary?

### 2.2 Canonical Multi-Tier Pitch Architecture
The reconciliation establishes a clear three-tier physical mounting domain:

```text
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Pitch Angle Range     Operational State     Permitted System Actions        Safety Reasoning          |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 35.0° <= θ <= 55.0°   VALID (Nominal ODD)   Full autonomous braking & alarms Optimal ground ray angle |
| 20.0° <= θ < 35.0°    DEGRADED STRESS TEST  Advisory warnings only; AEB off Severe ground compression |
| θ < 20.0° or θ > 65.0°OUT_OF_DOMAIN         Strict refusal (INSUFFICIENT_EVID)Horizon glance / Nadir  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 2.3 Formal Classification of `CAM_DOCK_SOUTH`
`CAM_DOCK_SOUTH` ($22.5^\circ$) is formally designated as an **OUT-OF-NOMINAL-ODD / DEGRADED STRESS TEST**. It was successfully utilized to evaluate degraded surveillance performance under shallow viewing angles, but is **NOT** a nominal certified camera installation for autonomous emergency braking (AEB).

## 4. Master Canonical ODD Specification

The canonical ODD specification is codified in `safety_backfire_v3/outputs/v3_2_1/canonical_odd.json`:
- **Mounting Height ($H$):** $3.5\,\text{m}$ to $6.0\,\text{m}$ (Nominal: $4.5\,\text{m}$)
- **Mounting Pitch Angle ($\theta$):** $35.0^\circ$ to $55.0^\circ$ (Nominal: $45.0^\circ$; Degraded: $20.0^\circ - 35.0^\circ$)
- **Mounting Rigidity Tolerance:** $\le \pm 0.35^\circ$ maximum mechanical/thermal deflection drift (`FAIL-30`, `FAIL-44`)
- **Mounting Roll Tolerance:** $\le \pm 1.5^\circ$
- **Floor Slope / Planarity:** $\le 1.5^\circ$ slope (Ramps $> 3.0^\circ$ strictly `OUT_OF_ODD` under `FAIL-27`, `FAIL-45`)
- **Ambient Lighting:** $150\,\text{lux}$ to $1,200\,\text{lux}$ (Degraded: $50 - 150\,\text{lux}$; Out of domain: $< 50\,\text{lux}$)
- **Hardware Freshness Latency:** $\le 50.0\,\text{ms}$ (Degraded: $50 - 500\,\text{ms}$; Starvation refusal: $> 500\,\text{ms}$, `FAIL-51`)
- **Maximum Entity Velocity:** $\le 4.5\,\text{m/s}$ ($16.2\,\text{km/h}$)
