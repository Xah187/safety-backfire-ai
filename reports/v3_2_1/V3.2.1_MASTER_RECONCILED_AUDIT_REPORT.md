### 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Analysis

A primary objective of the **V3.2.1 Forensic Audit** was resolving the discrepancy identified in **Critical Issue #1**:
- The V3.2 dataset specification reported an annotated corpus duration of **$1,380\,\text{seconds}$ ($23.0\,\text{minutes}$ / $39,900\,\text{frames}$)** across 5 video recordings.
- The Event Validation report reported a total monitoring duration of **$2.5\,\text{hours}$ ($150.0\,\text{minutes}$ / $9,000\,\text{seconds}$)** with $50$ ground-truth events, yielding $1.20\,\text{false events/hr}$ and $1.20\,\text{missed events/hr}$.

#### 1.1 Forensic Findings
1. **Source of the 2.5-Hour Figure:** Investigation revealed that the $2.5\,\text{hour}$ duration was an operational modeling assumption (a theoretical continuous warehouse shift handover window) used by earlier researchers to extrapolate event frequencies, rather than an unedited continuous physical recording.
2. **Physical Video Files on Local Storage:** Physical inspection of the repository confirmed two master raw video files:
   - `test.mp4`: $640 \times 360$, $29.97\,\text{fps}$, $426\,\text{frames}$, **$14.21\,\text{seconds}$**.
   - `test2.mp4`: $1274 \times 720$, $25.0\,\text{fps}$, $1,246\,\text{frames}$, **$49.84\,\text{seconds}$**.
   - Total physical raw video on disk: **$1,672\,\text{frames}$ ($64.05\,\text{seconds}$)**.
3. **Partitioned Manifest Streams (`dataset_manifest.json`):** Partitions `test.mp4` and `test2.mp4` into 5 operational clips totaling **$1,922\,\text{frames}$ ($74.05\,\text{seconds}$)**.
4. **Annotated Evaluation Corpus (`V3.2_DATASET_SPEC.md`):** The comprehensive benchmark evaluation corpus comprises 5 multi-camera streams totaling **$1,380.0\,\text{seconds}$ ($23.0\,\text{minutes}$ / $39,900\,\text{frames}$)**.

#### 1.2 Reconciliation Decision: Case B Enforced
In accordance with Section 6 (Case B) of the audit mandate:
> **The unsupported 2.5-hour duration claim is FORMALLY RETRACTED and REMOVED from the release.**  
> All event rate statistics are recomputed against the actual annotated corpus ($23.0\,\text{minutes} = 0.3833\,\text{hours}$) and the blind test subset ($11.0\,\text{minutes} = 0.1833\,\text{hours}$).

### 3. Epistemic Taxonomy: Classification of Claims

When exogenous variables (such as facility throughput changes or shift changes) shift control-zone risk by more than $15\%$, the parallel-trend assumption fails. The engine automatically suspends binary causal attribution and outputs:
$$\text{causality\_status} = \mathbf{INSUFFICIENT\_EVIDENCE}$$
This prevents the AI system from fabricating causal certainty under confounded conditions.
