## 3. The 7 Documented Physical ODD Boundaries

Under V3.2.1 safety governance, physical environmental and optical limits are formally designated as **DOCUMENTED BOUNDARIES**, not software defects:

1. **`FAIL-13 / FAIL-40` (Identical PPE Uniform ReID Drift):** Visual embeddings cannot differentiate identical high-vis vests after crossing passes; spatial Kalman trajectory gating is mandatory.
2. **`FAIL-26 / FAIL-38` (Far-Field Monocular Ray Dilution):** Beyond $8.0\,\text{m}$, monocular perspective ground projection uncertainty exceeds defensive clearance buffers. Emergency braking is restricted to $D \le 8.0\,\text{m}$.
3. **`FAIL-27 / FAIL-45` (Ramp Incline Elevation Warping):** Planar homography ($Z_w = 0$) distorts ground coordinates on ramps $> 3.0^\circ$. Ramps must be modeled via 3D meshes or marked `OUT_OF_ODD`.
4. **`FAIL-30 / FAIL-44` (Camera Pitch Gantry Mounting Drift):** Projective sensitivity scales quadratically with depth. Gantry brackets must maintain mechanical rigidity within $\pm 0.35^\circ$.
