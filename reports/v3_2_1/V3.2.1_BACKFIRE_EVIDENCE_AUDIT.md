# V3.2.1 — Safety Backfire Empirical Evidence Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

This report establishes the forensic audit of the empirical evidence supporting the detection of **Safety Backfire** in Safety Backfire AI V3.2.1. The audit resolved **Critical Issue #8**:
> **A sample result of 2/2 observed cases must NEVER be reported as a 100% population-level general accuracy claim.**

The audit verified that while the system successfully identified the safety outcome in both evaluated intervention scenarios, **population-level generalization cannot be inferred from $N = 2$ case studies**. The metric is formally designated as an **empirical case demonstration**, preserving complete scientific integrity without sample fabrication.

## 2. Granular Case Study Verification

### 2.1 Case Study 01: Physical Guardrail Installation (`CASE_01_PHYSICAL_BARRIER`)
- **Facility Location:** `WH_SECTOR_B` (Aisle 04 Corridor)
- **Physical Intervention:** Heavy steel bollards and wire-mesh guardrail installed along `LANE_PEDESTRIAN_A`.
- **Intended Hazard Reduction:** Vehicle-pedestrian near-misses reduced from exposure index $2.94 \to 0.00$ ($\Delta = -2.94$, $100\%$ reduction).
- **Observed Unintended Side-Effects:**
  1. Manual hand trucks and stock pickers diverted into `AISLE_02`, where traffic density elevated collision exposure from $0.00 \to 1.47$ ($\Delta = +1.47$).
  2. The guardrail endcap encroached into the egress envelope of Emergency Exit 4, creating an unmodeled `BLOCKED_EGRESS_HAZARD` (exposure jumped from $0.00 \to 0.90$).
- **System Classification:** `NEW_HAZARD_CREATED / PARTIAL_BACKFIRE`.
- **Audit Finding:** **CLASSIFICATION CORRECT.** The system accurately detected that an intervention intended to eliminate a pedestrian hazard induced an emergency egress violation.

### 2.2 Case Study 02: Electronic Speed Governor (`CASE_02_SPEED_LIMIT_GOVERNOR`)
- **Facility Location:** `WH_SECTOR_B` (Fleet-wide Reach Trucks)
- **Physical Intervention:** Electronic firmware governor capping vehicle speeds to $v \le 1.50\,\text{m/s}$ ($5.4\,\text{km/h}$).
- **Intended Hazard Reduction:** Collision exposure reduced from $1.85 \to 0.05$ ($97.3\%$ reduction).
- **Observed Unintended Side-Effects:** Zero spatial displacement or secondary hazards observed.
- **System Classification:** `RISK_REDUCED / GENUINE_SAFETY_GAIN`.
- **Audit Finding:** **CLASSIFICATION CORRECT.** The system confirmed an unambiguous, genuine safety gain without collateral backfire.
