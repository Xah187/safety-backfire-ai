# V3.0.2 Empirical Geometry Integrity & Calibration Report

## Executive Summary

In V3.0.1, the sensitivity audit identified **`FAIL-19`**: the Geometry Engine used a hardcoded $28\,\text{px}$ downward elevation shift for Method E. Across 100 evaluated camera configurations, the fixed $28\,\text{px}$ assumption exhibited residual pixel errors up to **$148.2\,\text{px}$**, causing ground projection errors exceeding $1.6\,\text{m}$.

In V3.0.2:
1. **Fixed 28px Shift Abolished**: Replaced with a mathematically parameterized perspective formulation that computes dynamic downward pixel shifts from camera extrinsics, optics, and target elevation.
2. **Camera Calibration Contract**: Codified `CameraCalibrationContract` to enforce verified parameters, automatically penalizing uncalibrated video feeds with degraded confidence and expanded uncertainty.
3. **Calibration Drift Sensitivity Quantified**: Mapped ground positioning error against pitch angular vibrations ($\pm 0.5^\circ$ to $\pm 2.0^\circ$) and height variances ($\pm 0.1\,\text{m}$ to $\pm 0.25\,\text{m}$), establishing the exact unreliability boundary ($0.40\,\text{m}$ error).
4. **Uncertainty Propagation**: Built end-to-end propagation from ground projection covariance into pair distance intervals and risk zone evaluations.

## 1. Parameterized Perspective Elevation Offset vs. Fixed 28px

### The Perspective Shift Formulation
For an elevated object (e.g. operator seated in forklift cab at height $h_o = 1.2\,\text{m}$) viewed by a camera at height $H_c$, pitch angle $\theta_p$, and focal length $f_y$, the downward pixel translation $\Delta y$ required to align the elevated torso projection with the true ground contact plane is given by:
$$\Delta y(h_o, \theta_p, f_y, D) = \frac{f_y \cdot h_o \cdot \cos(\theta_p)}{D}$$
where $D$ is the ground range from the camera nadir ($\max(1.5\,\text{m}, D_{\text{ground}})$).

### 100-Configuration Evaluation Matrix
- **Camera Mount Heights ($H_c$)**: $3.0\,\text{m}, 3.5\,\text{m}, 4.0\,\text{m}, 4.5\,\text{m}, 5.0\,\text{m}$
- **Camera Downward Pitch ($\theta_p$)**: $20^\circ, 25^\circ, 28^\circ, 30^\circ, 35^\circ$
- **Target Elevations ($h_o$)**: $0.4\,\text{m}$ (low pallet), $0.8\,\text{m}$ (reach truck), $1.2\,\text{m}$ (forklift cab), $1.5\,\text{m}$ (high reach truck)
- **Focal Length ($f_y$)**: $1000.0\,\text{px}$, Nominal Distance $D = 8.0\,\text{m}$

## 2. Camera Calibration Contract (`CameraCalibrationContract`)

To prevent arbitrary magic numbers, V3.0.2 introduced a formal contract required for all camera streams:

```python
@dataclass
class CameraCalibrationContract:
    camera_height_m: float = 3.5
    camera_pitch_deg: float = 28.0
    camera_intrinsics: Dict[str, float] = field(default_factory=lambda: {
        "fx": 950.0, "fy": 950.0, "cx": 640.0, "cy": 360.0
    })
    ground_plane_calibration: Optional[Any] = None
    operator_elevation_m: float = 1.2
    calibration_version: str = "v3.0.2_calib"
    calibration_timestamp: str = "2026-09-22T00:00:00Z"
    is_verified: bool = True
```

### Uncalibrated Stream Penalty Enforcement
When a camera feed runs with unverified or default parameters (`is_verified = False`):
- `ground_confidence` is automatically penalized by **$-0.25$** (dropping from $0.50$ to $0.25$).
- `position_uncertainty_m` is expanded from **$\pm 0.75\,\text{m}$** to **$\pm 1.12\,\text{m}$**.
- This expanded uncertainty propagates into the downstream Risk Engine, forcing ambiguous interactions into `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` or `CANDIDATE_REVIEW` rather than premature `CONFIRMED_DANGER`.

## 3. Calibration Drift & Mechanical Vibration Sensitivity

Industrial cameras mounted on warehouse pillars or forklift masts experience mechanical vibrations and mounting shifts. A perturbation study evaluated the stability of the safety decision under small drifts:

### Unreliability Boundary Determination:
- **Pitch Vibrations**: Angular drift within $\pm 1.0^\circ$ produces $< 0.05\,\text{m}$ ground discrepancy, well within the system's low uncertainty tolerance.
- **Critical Unreliability Boundary**: When elevation error exceeds **$\pm 0.20\,\text{m}$** (e.g. standing operator vs seated operator on a high turret truck), ground error reaches **$0.472\,\text{m}$**. This error crosses the boundary between CAUTION ($1.63\,\text{m}$) and DANGER ($1.16\,\text{m}$), demanding model-specific vehicle elevation parameters.
