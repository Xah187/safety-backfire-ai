## 3. Dataset Provenance Gate

To prevent unverified data from contaminating scientific evaluation, `safety_backfire_v3_3/dataset/provenance_gate.py` and `validation/v3_3/test_dataset_provenance_gate.py` enforce seven mandatory provenance criteria:
1. Source file exists on disk.
2. SHA-256 hash is recorded and valid (64 hex characters, non-dummy).
3. Source type is known (`REAL`, `SYNTHETIC`, `REPLAY`).
4. Complete capture metadata exists (camera ID, FPS, resolution).
5. Split assignment is immutable (`V3.3_DEV`, `V3.3_CALIBRATION`, `V3.3_VALIDATION`, `V3.3_BLIND`, `V3.3_EXTERNAL_STRESS`).
6. Annotation provenance is documented.
7. ODD metadata is declared or explicitly designated `UNKNOWN`.

**Gate Enforcement Result**: The gate strictly rejected `DS_FULL_CORPUS_39900` (`METADATA_ONLY`) and `DS_V3_3_UNAVAILABLE_EXTERNAL_FACILITY_E` (`UNAVAILABLE`) from entering blind scientific evaluation, while validating all physical splits (`5/5 PASS`).

## 5. EXP_33_01 — Out-of-Sample Uncertainty Validation

### A. What was executed
Factor $k$ was fitted exclusively using calibration data (`DS_V3_3_CALIB_SECTOR_B`, $N=500$ points) targeting $95.0\%$ coverage, yielding $k=2.42$ ($95.00\%$ in-sample coverage). Factor $k=2.42$ was then permanently locked. Out-of-sample uncertainty evaluation was then executed against $N=745$ independent test points drawn from `DS_V3_3_VAL_INTERSECTION_C` and unblinded `DS_V3_3_BLIND_STAGING_D` across distance, illumination, and occlusion strata without parameter tuning.

### B. What was observed
- **Calibration (In-Sample)**: $N=500$, Covered: $475$, Coverage: $95.00\%$.
- **Out-of-Sample Overall**: $N=745$, Covered: $669$, Coverage: $89.80\%$.
- **Out-of-Sample Nominal ODD ($[0.5-8.0\text{m}]$)**: $N=663$, Covered: $614$, Coverage: $92.61\%$.
- **Out-of-Sample Far-Field Boundary ($>8.0\text{m}$)**: $N=82$, Covered: $55$, Coverage: $67.07\%$.
- **Camera Sector C (`CAM_SECTOR_C_03`)**: $N=373$, Coverage: $94.91\%$.
- **Camera Sector D (`CAM_SECTOR_D_04`)**: $N=372$, Coverage: $84.68\%$.

### C. What was statistically supported
- The Wilson Score 95% Confidence Interval for nominal out-of-sample coverage is **$[90.36\%, 94.36\%]$**.
- Because the upper bound of the confidence interval ($94.36\%$) is strictly below the pre-registered target of $95.00\%$, the hypothesis that a single calibration factor guarantees $\ge 95\%$ coverage across unseen nominal environments is **empirically refuted**.
- In the far-field ($>8.0\text{m}$), coverage severely degrades to **$67.07\%$ [Wilson 95% CI: $56.21\%-76.34\%$]**.

### D. What remains unverified
- Whether higher-order adaptive uncertainty scaling (dynamic heteroskedastic models conditioned on real-time lux and depth) can restore $\ge 95\%$ coverage across all warehouse sectors without triggering safe refusal.

## 6. EXP_33_02 — Cross-Environment Generalization

### A. What was executed
The complete perception, tracking, identity, geometry, hazard, and event detection pipelines were evaluated across four distinct warehouse sectors (Sector A: Wide Aisle; Sector B: High-Bay Storage; Sector C: T-Intersection; Sector D: Congested Staging) comprising 1,672 physical video frames.

### B. What was observed
- **Perception F1**: Achieved $88.45\%$ overall ($N=1,672$ frames, $220$ detection misses/false positives).
- **Tracking IDF1**: Achieved $91.80\%$ across $N=28$ tracked trajectory sequences.
- **Identity Switches**: $23$ total identity switch events observed across all sectors.
- **Geometry Error**: Mean homography reprojection residual was $0.0215\text{m}$ ($2.15\text{cm}$), with 95th percentile at $0.0418\text{m}$.
- **Hazard Recall**: $94.12\%$ ($N=34$ hazard episodes, 2 near-misses delayed).
- **Event Classification Accuracy**: $91.67\%$ ($N=48$ interaction events).

### C. What was statistically supported
- Tracking IDF1 was evaluated using a non-parametric trajectory-level bootstrap ($1,000$ replicates, seed $1337$), yielding an empirical estimate of **$91.80\%$ [Bootstrap 95% CI: $90.57\% - 92.95\%$]**.
- Stratified perception results confirm performance stability in near-field ($93.25\%$ F1) and normal illumination ($91.62\%$ F1), but bounded degradation in far-field ($75.89\%$ F1) and high occlusion ($72.04\%$ F1).

### D. What remains unverified
- Performance generalization across external commercial logistics hubs with multi-tier mezzanines, automated guided vehicles (AGVs), or temperatures $< 0^\circ\text{C}$ (e.g. proposed Facility E).

## 8. EXP_33_03 — Safety Backfire & Intervention Expansion

### A. What was executed
Intervention evidence was expanded from $N=2$ (V3.2.1) to $N=5$ documented episodes, evaluating pre/post/control zones under rigorous Difference-in-Differences (DiD) and pre-treatment trend validation:
1. `INT_33_01`: Physical Barrier Installation (Sector A).
2. `INT_33_02`: Vehicle Speed Limiter Activation (Sector B).
3. `INT_33_03`: Pedestrian Detour Routing (Sector C).
4. `INT_33_04`: Outbound Staging Relocation (Sector D).
5. `INT_33_05`: Exogenous Shift Shock Diagnostic (Cross-Site).

### B. What was observed
The five interventions exhibited distinct outcome states across the backfire taxonomy:
- **`INT_33_01` (Barrier)**: $\Delta_{\text{primary}} = -0.50$, $\Delta_{\text{secondary}} = +0.32$, $\Delta_{\text{control}} = +0.01$. $\text{DiD} = -0.51$. Outcome: **`RISK_REDISTRIBUTED`** (Crosswalk collisions dropped $50\%$, but bottleneck pinch-point risk increased $32\%$).
- **`INT_33_02` (Speed Limiter)**: $\Delta_{\text{primary}} = -0.36$, $\Delta_{\text{secondary}} = +0.02$, $\Delta_{\text{control}} = -0.01$. $\text{DiD} = -0.35$. Outcome: **`RISK_REDUCED`** (True safety benefit without secondary risk inflation).
- **`INT_33_03` (Pedestrian Detour)**: $\Delta_{\text{primary}} = -0.27$, $\Delta_{\text{secondary}} = +0.38$, $\Delta_{\text{control}} = +0.01$. $\text{DiD} = -0.28$. Outcome: **`NEW_HAZARD_CREATED`** (Detour created blind corner emergence hazard behind high racks).
- **`INT_33_04` (Staging Relocation)**: $\Delta_{\text{primary}} = -0.32$, $\Delta_{\text{secondary}} = +0.21$, $\Delta_{\text{control}} = +0.02$. $\text{DiD} = -0.34$. Outcome: **`RISK_REDISTRIBUTED`** (Pallet wall crushing reduced, but dock queue congestion increased).
- **`INT_33_05` (Shift Shock)**: Exogenous shift change surge ($+38.5\%$ volume) caused control zone baseline drift of $+0.27$ ($>0.15$ tolerance). $\text{DiD} = -0.47$. Outcome: **`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`**.

### C. What was statistically supported
- Under stable parallel trends ($\Delta_{\text{control}} \le 0.02$), the DiD estimator validly separated primary hazard reduction from secondary risk displacement in Cases 1-4.
- In Case 5, parallel-trend failure was detected ($p=0.008$), and the causal engine successfully refused attribution rather than producing a false safety claim.

### D. What remains unverified
- Long-term worker adaptation or risk compensation over multi-month operating horizons ($> 90$ days).

## 9. EXP_33_04 — Safe Refusal & Boundary Robustness

### A. What was executed
`SafeRefusalEngine` was evaluated across $N=850$ operational decisions comprising $500$ nominal inputs, $200$ degraded ODD inputs, and $150$ severe out-of-ODD violations (ramp slope $>3^\circ$, latency $>500\text{ms}$, distance $>10\text{m}$, pitch $<20^\circ$, illumination $<50\text{ lux}$).

### B. What was observed
- **Appropriate Refusal Rate**: **$100.0\%$** ($150 / 150$ boundary violations correctly refused).
- **Unsafe Non-Refusal Rate**: **$0.0\%$** ($0 / 150$).
- **False Refusal Rate**: **$0.0\%$** ($0 / 500$ nominal inputs refused; nominal intervention preserved).
- **Advisory Downgrades**: $100\%$ ($200 / 200$) of degraded ODD cases downgraded to advisory warnings.
- **Mean Boundary Detection Latency**: $25.8\text{ms}$.

### C. What was statistically supported
- Appropriate Refusal Rate: **$1.000$ [Wilson 95% CI: $0.9751 - 1.0000$]**.
- False Refusal Rate: **$0.000$ [Wilson 95% CI: $0.0000 - 0.0076$]**.
- Acceptance criteria ($\ge 98\%$ refusal, $\le 1\%$ unsafe, $\le 5\%$ false) were completely satisfied.

### D. What remains unverified
- Refusal behavior during simultaneous hardware sensor faults (e.g. combined camera lens smudge + GPU clock throttle).

## 14. Empirical Limitations

1. **Physical Footage Scope**: Validated empirical footage is strictly bounded to $1,672$ physical frames ($64.05$ seconds). Historical claims regarding $39,900$ frames represent aggregate metadata only.
2. **Nominal ODD Undercoverage**: Pre-calibrated factor $k=2.42$ achieves $92.61\%$ coverage on unseen nominal data, falling short of the $95.00\%$ target. Operations beyond $8.0\text{m}$ require safe refusal gating.
3. **Causal Generalizability**: All intervention backfire claims are valid within the documented case episodes; no claim of universal backfire inevitability is asserted.
4. **Planar Constraint**: Homography distance estimation is invalid on ramps ($>1.5^\circ$ slope); automated refusal must be active in non-planar environments.
