## 3. V3.3 Architectural Framework Components

The new version boundary `safety_backfire_v3_3/` has been established with the following modular subsystems:

1. **Dataset Strategy & Blind Quarantine (`safety_backfire_v3_3/dataset/`):**
   - Implements immutable metadata validation across `V3.3_DEV`, `V3.3_CALIBRATION`, `V3.3_VALIDATION`, `V3.3_BLIND`, and `V3.3_EXTERNAL_STRESS`.
   - Enforces automatic cryptographic check for zero data leakage between calibration and evaluation/blind sets.
   - Enforces strict permission quarantine on blind streams until final unblinding.

2. **Out-of-Sample Uncertainty Engine (`safety_backfire_v3_3/uncertainty/`):**
   - Implements `UncertaintyEvaluator` with hard pre-locking of coverage factor $k$.
   - Segregates in-sample calibration coverage from independent out-of-sample evaluation.
   - Evaluates coverage across distance strata, illumination strata, and occlusion states.

3. **Ground Geometry & Safe Refusal (`safety_backfire_v3_3/geometry/` & `safety_backfire_v3_3/refusal/`):**
   - Surface planarity classifier (`PLANAR_VALID`, `PLANAR_DEGRADED`, `NON_PLANAR_REFUSAL`).
   - Converts optical boundaries, ramp transitions, and frame processing latency spikes into safe refusal (`SAFE_REFUSAL_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`) rather than silent unsafe intervention.

4. **Multi-Zone Causal Attribution & Backfire (`safety_backfire_v3_3/causality/` & `safety_backfire_v3_3/intervention/`):**
   - Difference-in-Differences (DiD) engine with automated parallel-trend verification.
   - Refuses causal attribution under facility throughput shocks ($> 20\%$) or control baseline drift ($> 15\%$).
   - Categorizes intervention outcomes into explicit backfire states (`RISK_REDUCED`, `PARTIAL_BACKFIRE`, `NEW_HAZARD_CREATED`, `RISK_REDISTRIBUTED`, `NO_DETECTABLE_EFFECT`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`).
