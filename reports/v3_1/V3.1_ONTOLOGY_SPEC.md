# V3.1 — Formal Warehouse Safety Ontology Specification

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The **Safety Backfire AI V3.1 Ontology** establishes a mathematically rigorous, semantically grounded, entity-agnostic knowledge representation for industrial warehouse safety. Prior to V3.1 (V2.9 through V3.0.5), the core system was hardcoded for pairwise `Person ↔ Forklift` collision detection. While mathematically sound (preserving 2D Rice $k=2.24$ distribution coverage and Jensen second-order debiasing), it could not reason about:
1. Static structural threats (unstable pallet stacks, leaning storage racks, structural fatigue).
2. Infrastructure egress violations (blocked fire exits, narrowed pedestrian pathways).
3. Industrial machinery entrapment and conveyor pinch-point exposures.
4. Environmental surface threats (liquid chemical spills, icy loading dock edges).
5. Unintended safety redistribution and risk migration (**Safety Backfire**).

V3.1 decouples raw perception from semantic reasoning, defining formal contracts across 8 core ontological domains.

## 2. Core Ontological Axioms & Invariants

All modules within the Safety Backfire AI architecture must adhere strictly to seven non-negotiable safety axioms:

### Axiom 1: Epistemic Non-Equivalence of Unknown and Safe
$$\text{State}(\text{entity}) = \text{UNKNOWN} \centernot\implies \text{Safety}(\text{entity}) = \text{SAFE}$$
*Operational Rule:* When an entity's identity, posture, or operational state cannot be determined with sufficient confidence, the system must fail-closed to protective surveillance, maintaining full defensive clearance envelopes.

### Axiom 2: Insufficient Evidence Invariant
$$\text{Quality}(\text{Evidence}) < \tau_{\text{floor}} \implies \text{Decision} = \text{INSUFFICIENT\_EVIDENCE} \ne \text{SAFE}$$
*Operational Rule:* Low detection score, high camera vibration, or heavy occlusion must never be interpreted as an absence of hazard.

### Axiom 3: Non-Suppression of Unclassified Threats
$$\text{Class}(\text{object}) = \text{UNKNOWN\_ENTITY} \centernot\implies \text{Filter}(\text{object}) = \text{SUPPRESSED}$$
*Operational Rule:* Unclassified physical objects in active transit lanes must trigger candidate review rather than being silently filtered.

### Axiom 4: Decoupled ID Namespace Independence
$$\text{EntityID} \cap \text{PairID} \cap \text{EpisodeID} \cap \text{EventID} \cap \text{HazardID} \cap \text{AlertID} = \emptyset$$
*Operational Rule:* Identity, temporal tracking, hazard classification, and alarm dispatch maintain disjoint, globally unique identifiers to prevent tracker ID switches from corrupting hazard histories or alarm throttling.

### Axiom 5: Temporal Monotonicity & Causal Windowing
$$\forall t_{\text{baseline}} \in \mathcal{W}_{\text{base}}, \; \forall t_{\text{post}} \in \mathcal{W}_{\text{post}}: \quad t_{\text{baseline}} < T_{\text{intervention}} \le t_{\text{post}}$$
*Operational Rule:* No sensory observation or state estimate recorded at or after an intervention timestamp $T_{\text{intervention}}$ may retroactively mutate or inform baseline risk calculations.

### Axiom 6: Structural and Kinetic Decoupling
$$\lim_{v_{\text{rel}} \to 0} \text{TTC} = \infty \centernot\implies \text{Risk}(\text{Hazard}_{\text{structural}}) = \text{SAFE}$$
*Operational Rule:* Structural collapse, unstable high-tier loads, and blocked fire exits have relative velocities $v \approx 0$. Risk evaluation must assess spatial clearance and structural deformation completely independently of kinematic time-to-collision (TTC).

### Axiom 7: Worst-Case Multi-Hazard Aggregation
$$\text{Risk}_{\text{scene}}(\mathcal{H}) = \max_{h \in \mathcal{H}} \text{Risk}(h)$$
*Operational Rule:* A benign or safe relationship between entities $A$ and $B$ must never mask, suppress, or average down a concurrent critical hazard between entities $A$ and $C$.

## 3. Ontological Schema Hierarchy

The ontology is structured into 8 layered domains:

```text
[Domain 1: Evidence & Provenance]
  ├── ObservationV1 (2D BBox, Keypoints, Polygons, Sensor PTS)
  ├── ProvenanceRecord (Hardware PTS, Ingest PTS, SensorID, PolicyVersion)
  └── ConfidenceDecomposition (Detection, Tracking, Geometry, Kinematics, Hazard)
         │
         ▼
[Domain 2: Entities & States]
  ├── PersonEntity (Pedestrian, Operator, MaintenanceWorker, UnknownPerson)
  ├── VehicleEntity (Counterbalance, ReachTruck, PalletJack, AGV, Tugger)
  ├── StorageStructureEntity (SelectiveRack, CantileverRack, BulkStack)
  ├── LoadEntity (PalletLoad, UnitLoad, OverhangingLoad, Drum, TiltedLoad)
  ├── MachineEntity (Conveyor, StretchWrapper, Compactor, RoboticArm)
  └── InfrastructureEntity (Aisle, PedestrianLane, DockDoor, EmergencyExit)
         │
         ▼
[Domain 3: Generic Relations]
  ├── SpatialRelation (Distance, Clearance, 2D Support Polygon Footprint)
  ├── KinematicRelation (ApproachSpeed, TTC, CPA, TrajectoryAngle)
  ├── TopologicalRelation (InsideZone, CrossingBoundary, Occluding)
  └── StructuralRelation (Supporting, BearingLoad, LeaningAgainst)
         │
         ▼
[Domain 4: Interaction Matrix]
  └── InteractionRecord (Severity: Catastrophic -> Negligible; Imminence: Imminent -> None)
         │
         ▼
[Domain 5: Multi-Hazard Taxonomy]
  └── HazardRecord (20 Controlled Hazard Types, Dynamic Lifecycle States)
         │
         ▼
[Domain 6: Universal Risk Engine]
  └── RiskDecisionV1 (Confirmed Danger, Caution, Candidate Review, Safe, Insufficient Evidence)
         │
         ▼
[Domain 7: Counterfactual Interventions]
  └── InterventionRecord (Physical Barrier, Reroute, Speed Limit, Restricted Zone)
         │
         ▼
[Domain 8: Safety Backfire Engine]
  └── BackfireAssessmentRecord (Risk Shifted, New Hazard Created, Genuine Safety Gain)
```

## 4. Multi-Dimensional Confidence Decomposition

In safety-critical systems, scalar confidence values hide epistemic vulnerabilities. V3.1 enforces a decomposed vector contract $\mathbf{C} \in [0, 1]^8$:

$$\mathbf{C} = \begin{bmatrix} c_{\text{det}} \\ c_{\text{track}} \\ c_{\text{id}} \\ c_{\text{geom}} \\ c_{\text{pose}} \\ c_{\text{rel}} \\ c_{\text{kin}} \\ c_{\text{hazard}} \end{bmatrix}, \quad c_{\text{gate}} = \min_{c_i > 0} c_i$$

A high object detection score ($c_{\text{det}} = 0.98$) cannot override severe camera vibration causing geometric depth uncertainty ($c_{\text{geom}} = 0.35$). When $c_{\text{gate}} < 0.40$, the system automatically downgrades the operational domain status to `ODDStatus.DEGRADED` or emits `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
