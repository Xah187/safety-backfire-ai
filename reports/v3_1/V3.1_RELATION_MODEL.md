# V3.1 — Generic Relation Model & Continuous Metrics

## 1. Overview & Purpose

The **V3.1 Generic Relation Model** replaces ad-hoc entity pairing with an entity-agnostic relation engine. In industrial environments, safety risks emerge not from entities in isolation, but from their spatial, kinematic, topological, and structural interrelationships.

The relation evaluator processes any arbitrary entity pair $(E_i, E_j)$ and computes continuous physical metrics alongside discrete relational typologies.

---

## 2. Relation Typology Taxonomy

Relations are categorized into four orthogonal dimensions:

```text
RelationRecord
├── Spatial Relations
│     ├── ADJACENT_TO (< 1.5m clearance)
│     ├── NEARBY (1.5m - 3.5m clearance)
│     ├── ALIGNED_WITH (sharing corridor axis)
│     ├── CONTAINED_IN (boundary inclusion)
│     ├── OVERLAPPING (2D footprint intersection)
│     └── FAR (> 3.5m clearance)
├── Kinematic Relations
│     ├── APPROACHING (relative velocity v_approach > 0.3 m/s)
│     ├── RECEDING (relative velocity v_approach < -0.3 m/s)
│     ├── CROSSING_PATH (intersecting trajectories within 5s)
│     ├── FOLLOWING (collinear velocity vectors)
│     ├── PARALLEL_MOTION (coplanar velocities)
│     ├── CONVERGING (CPA < 1.0m within 3s)
│     └── STATIONARY_RELATIVE (|v_rel| < 0.2 m/s)
├── Topological Relations
│     ├── INSIDE_ZONE (e.g. inside pedestrian walkway)
│     ├── OUTSIDE_ZONE
│     ├── AT_BOUNDARY (within 0.5m of zone perimeter)
│     ├── CROSSING_BOUNDARY (velocity vector intersects perimeter)
│     ├── OCCLUDING (visual line-of-sight obstruction)
│     └── OCCLUDED_BY
└── Structural Relations
      ├── SUPPORTING (transmitting gravitational load)
      ├── SUPPORTED_BY
      ├── ATTACHED_TO (mechanical coupling)
      ├── LEANING_AGAINST (transverse force contact)
      └── COLLIDING_WITH (active physical impact)
```

## 3. Continuous Mathematical Formulation

### 3.1 Ground Clearance Distance ($d$)
Given 2D ground convex polygons $\mathcal{P}_i$ and $\mathcal{P}_j$:
$$d = \min_{\mathbf{p}_i \in \mathcal{P}_i, \, \mathbf{p}_j \in \mathcal{P}_j} \|\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{p}_j\|_2$$
For point approximations with centroids $\mathbf{c}_i, \mathbf{c}_j$ and effective radial radii $r_i, r_j$:
$$d \approx \max(0, \|\mathbf{c}_i - \mathbf{c}_j\|_2 - (r_i + r_j))$$

### 3.2 Propagation of Distance Uncertainty ($\sigma_d$)
Let $\mathbf{c}_i \sim \mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol{\mu}_i, \mathbf{\Sigma}_i)$ and $\mathbf{c}_j \sim \mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol{\mu}_j, \mathbf{\Sigma}_j)$. The relative separation vector is $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{c}_i - \mathbf{c}_j$ with covariance $\mathbf{\Sigma}_r = \mathbf{\Sigma}_i + \mathbf{\Sigma}_j$.
The gradient with respect to $\mathbf{r}$ is $\nabla_{\mathbf{r}} d = \frac{\mathbf{r}}{\|\mathbf{r}\|}$.
By first-order Taylor expansion, the distance variance is:
$$\sigma_d^2 = \nabla_{\mathbf{r}} d^T \, \mathbf{\Sigma}_r \, \nabla_{\mathbf{r}} d = \frac{\mathbf{r}^T (\mathbf{\Sigma}_i + \mathbf{\Sigma}_j) \mathbf{r}}{\mathbf{r}^T \mathbf{r}}$$

### 3.3 Relative Approach Speed ($v_{\text{approach}}$)
$$v_{\text{approach}} = -\frac{d}{dt} d(t) = -\frac{(\mathbf{c}_i - \mathbf{c}_j) \cdot (\mathbf{v}_i - \mathbf{v}_j)}{\|\mathbf{c}_i - \mathbf{c}_j\|}$$
When $v_{\text{approach}} > 0$, the entities are converging. When $v_{\text{approach}} \le 0$, the entities are diverging.

### 3.4 Kinematic Time-To-Collision (TTC) & Closest Point of Approach (CPA)
Under constant velocity assumption:
$$\mathbf{r}(t) = (\mathbf{c}_i - \mathbf{c}_j) + (\mathbf{v}_i - \mathbf{v}_j) t = \mathbf{r}_0 + \mathbf{v}_{\text{rel}} t$$
The time of minimum separation $t_{\text{CPA}}$ is obtained by setting $\frac{d}{dt} \|\mathbf{r}(t)\|^2 = 0$:
$$t_{\text{CPA}} = -\frac{\mathbf{r}_0 \cdot \mathbf{v}_{\text{rel}}}{\|\mathbf{v}_{\text{rel}}\|^2}$$

- If $t_{\text{CPA}} \le 0$, the entities are diverging; kinematic TTC is undefined ($\text{TTC} = \infty$).
- If $t_{\text{CPA}} > 0$, the minimum clearance distance at CPA is:
  $$d_{\text{CPA}} = \|\mathbf{r}_0 + \mathbf{v}_{\text{rel}} t_{\text{CPA}}\|$$
- If $d_{\text{CPA}} \le r_i + r_j + \delta_{\text{clearance}}$, an actual collision is predicted on the current vector, and:
  $$\text{TTC} = t_{\text{CPA}} - \frac{\sqrt{(r_i + r_j)^2 - d_{\text{CPA}}^2}}{\|\mathbf{v}_{\text{rel}}\|}$$

## 4. Entity-Agnostic Pairwise Matrix

The `EntityAgnosticRelationEvaluator` evaluates all combinations without hardcoded pair assumptions:
1. `Person ↔ Vehicle`: Collision, proximity, crossing trajectories.
2. `Vehicle ↔ Storage Rack`: Rack upright collision threat.
3. `Load ↔ Storage Rack`: Overload, pallet collapse, load overhang.
4. `Person ↔ Machine`: Entrapment, nip-point exposure, unauthorized maintenance.
5. `Vehicle ↔ Infrastructure`: Dock door collision, emergency exit obstruction.
6. `Person ↔ Environmental Hazard`: Slip hazard, fall at dock edge.
