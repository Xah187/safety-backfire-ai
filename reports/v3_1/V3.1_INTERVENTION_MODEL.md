# V3.1 — Intervention Model & Counterfactual Windowing

## 1. Overview & Purpose

The **V3.1 Intervention Model** formalizes safety modifications introduced to warehouse environments. In traditional computer vision systems, interventions are treated as unmodeled external changes. In Safety Backfire AI, an intervention is an explicit, typed first-class entity with strict causal and temporal semantics.

The purpose of modeling interventions is to answer the core safety engineering question:
> *Did the intervention truly eliminate risk, or did it merely shift, transform, delay, or aggregate risk elsewhere?*

---

## 2. Intervention Taxonomy

```text
InterventionType
├── Physical Infrastructure Modifications
│     ├── PHYSICAL_BARRIER_ADDED (Steel bollards, guardrails, mesh screens)
│     ├── RACK_GUARD_INSTALLED (End-of-aisle structural impact protectors)
│     └── SIGNAGE_ALERT_ADDED (Projected floor markings, flashing beacons)
├── Spatial Layout & Routing Changes
│     ├── PEDESTRIAN_ROUTE_REROUTED (Altering designated walking paths)
│     ├── RESTRICTED_ZONE_CREATED (Exclusion zones around hazardous cells)
│     └── STORAGE_REARRANGED (Relocating high-velocity inventory)
└── Operational & Behavioral Policies
      ├── SPEED_LIMIT_CHANGED (Forklift speed governors or lane speed limits)
      └── PROCESS_CHANGED (Altering picking order or loading protocols)
```

---

## 3. Counterfactual Windowing & Causal Boundary

To evaluate an intervention rigorously, the system constructs a `CounterfactualTimeline` governed by an immutable cutoff timestamp $T_{\text{intervention}}$:

```text
           Baseline Window (W_base)               Post-Intervention Window (W_post)
    [--------------------------------------) [--------------------------------------]
  t_base_start                         T_intervention                           t_post_end
```

### Strict Temporal Separation Invariants:
1. **Zero Backward Leakage:** No sensor frame or state estimate with timestamp $t \ge T_{\text{intervention}}$ may enter baseline window calculations. Any violation raises `TemporalLeakageError` immediately (**FAIL-33**).
2. **Zero Forward Contamination:** Baseline metrics cannot be retrospectively adjusted using post-intervention knowledge.
3. **Causal Normalization:** Risk rates are normalized by window duration:
   $$\text{Rate}_{\text{base}} = \frac{\text{Exposure}_{\text{base}}}{\Delta t_{\text{base}}}, \quad \text{Expected Exposure}_{\text{post}} = \text{Rate}_{\text{base}} \times \Delta t_{\text{post}}$$

---

## 4. Counterfactual Inference Formulation

Let $Y(1)$ be the factual risk exposure observed in the warehouse following an intervention, and $Y(0)$ be the counterfactual risk exposure that would have occurred had the intervention not taken place.

$$\text{Treatment Effect } \tau = Y(1) - Y(0)$$

In industrial video streams, $Y(0)$ during $\mathcal{W}_{\text{post}}$ is unobservable directly. Safety Backfire AI estimates $Y(0)$ using synthetic counterfactual projection:
1. **Kinematic Projection:** Simulates unconstrained baseline traffic patterns continuing forward under pre-intervention rules.
2. **Poisson Baseline Projection:** Projects expected baseline incident rates onto the post-window duration.
3. **Difference Evaluation:** If $Y(1) - Y(0) < 0$, a genuine safety gain is recorded. If $Y(1) - Y(0) \ge 0$, or if novel hazards emerge in $Y(1)$, safety backfire has occurred.
