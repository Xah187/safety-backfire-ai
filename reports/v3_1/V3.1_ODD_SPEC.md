# V3.1 — Operational Design Domain (ODD) Specification

## 1. Executive Definition & Scope

The **Operational Design Domain (ODD)** specifies the exact physical, environmental, optical, and kinematic boundary conditions under which Safety Backfire AI V3.1 is certified to operate.

In strict compliance with ISO 21448 (SOTIF — Safety of the Functionality) and ANSI/ITSDF B56.5:
$$\text{Outside ODD} \implies \text{Safety System Status} = \text{OUT\_OF\_DOMAIN} \implies \text{Decision} = \text{INSUFFICIENT\_EVIDENCE}$$
The system never attempts to issue unconditioned safety authorizations when sensory conditions violate the validated ODD envelope.

## 2. Quantitative ODD Parameters & Operating Boundaries

| Domain Parameter | Nominal Valid Boundary | Degraded Boundary (Surveillance Mode) | Out-of-Domain Boundary (Fail-Closed) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Mounting Height ($H$)** | $3.5\,\text{m} \le H \le 6.0\,\text{m}$ | $3.0\,\text{m} \le H < 3.5\,\text{m}$ | $H < 3.0\,\text{m}$ or $H > 7.0\,\text{m}$ |

## 3. Physical Failure Modes at ODD Boundaries

### 3.1 Camera Pitch Mechanical Drift Sensitivity (FAIL-30 Boundary)
- **Physics Proof:** The forward ground coordinate $Y_w$ is obtained from ray-plane projection:
  $$Y_w = H \tan(\theta + \phi_y)$$
  The sensitivity derivative with respect to mechanical pitch deflection $\theta$ is:
  $$\frac{\partial Y_w}{\partial \theta} = H \sec^2(\theta + \phi_y) = \frac{Y_w^2 + H^2}{H}$$
- **Numerical Impact:** At $H = 4.5\,\text{m}$ and $Y_w = 8.0\,\text{m}$, $\frac{\partial Y_w}{\partial \theta} \approx 19.8\,\text{m/rad} = 0.35\,\text{m/deg}$.
  A mounting bracket vibration causing a deflection of just $1.0^\circ$ produces an uncalibrated ground shift of $0.35\,\text{m}$ at $8\,\text{m}$ and $1.18\,\text{m}$ at $15\,\text{m}$.
- **ODD Boundary Enforcement:** If hardware IMU or horizon fiducial markers detect pitch drift exceeding $\pm 0.35^\circ$, the system automatically transitions to `ODDStatus.OUT_OF_DOMAIN`.

### 3.2 Far-Field Monocular Depth Dilution (FAIL-26 Boundary)
- **Physics Proof:** The image pixel elevation $v$ scales inversely with depth $Y_w$:
  $$\frac{dv}{dY_w} \propto -\frac{f H}{Y_w^2}$$
- **Resolution Limit:** For depths $Y_w > 10\,\text{m}$, a 1-pixel detection centroid error translates to $>0.45\,\text{m}$ ground error, exceeding the defensive clearance zone ($0.40\,\text{m}$). Consequently, detection beyond $10.0\,\text{m}$ is strictly classified as unconditioned surveillance.

### 3.3 Non-Planar Floor Incline Gradients (FAIL-27 Boundary)
- The projective homography model $\mathbf{H}$ assumes a planar warehouse floor ($Z_w = 0$). Ramps, vehicle ramps, and loading inclines with gradients $\ge 3^\circ$ violate this planarity constraint, projecting vehicle wheels as if they are floating or buried. Non-planar areas must be mapped as dedicated exclusion zones.

## 4. Degraded Mode & Fail-Closed Protocols

When an ODD boundary is breached:
1. **No False Assertions of Safety:** Emits `UniversalRiskDecision` with `risk_decision = INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` (satisfying **Axiom 2**).
2. **Conservative Action:** Emits a hardware advisory to human safety supervisors indicating the specific degraded sensor channel (e.g., `camera_pitch_drift`, `sensor_temporal_lag`).
3. **Worst-Case Braking Buffers:** If deployed on autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), degraded ODD forces maximum deceleration to a controlled stop.
