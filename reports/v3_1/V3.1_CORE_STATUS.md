### Documented System Physical Boundaries:
1. **FAIL-13 (Uniform Visual ReID Drift):** Identical warehouse safety vests require spatial Kalman continuity; visual embeddings alone cannot distinguish identical uniforms after long occlusions.
2. **FAIL-26 (Far-Field Monocular Ray Dilution):** Beyond $10.0\,\text{m}$, monocular perspective ground projection uncertainty exceeds defensive clearance buffers.
3. **FAIL-27 (Non-Planar Ramp Floor Gradients):** Planar homography violates on floor ramps exceeding $3^\circ$ incline.
4. **FAIL-30 (Camera Pitch Mechanical Deflection):** Forward ground coordinate errors scale quadratically with depth ($\frac{\partial Y}{\partial \theta} \approx \frac{Y^2 + H^2}{H}$); camera mounting brackets must maintain mechanical rigidity within $\pm 0.35^\circ$.

## 4. Architectural Highlights of V3.1

1. **Entity-Agnostic Ontology:** Replaces ad-hoc string comparisons with typed classes covering People, Vehicles, Storage Structures, Loads, Industrial Machinery, and Infrastructure.
2. **Decomposed Confidence Contract:** Enforces $\mathbf{C} \in [0, 1]^8$, eliminating the epistemic trap of hiding sensory uncertainty behind a single scalar number.
3. **Static vs Kinetic Decoupling:** Evaluates structural collapse, load instability, and fire exit obstructions without division-by-zero or infinite TTC fallacies.
4. **Counterfactual Safety Backfire Engine:** Formally evaluates whether safety interventions (e.g. guardrails, traffic rerouting) genuinely improve safety or merely migrate risk across warehouse zones.
5. **Decoupled ID Namespaces:** Eliminates tracker ID switch cross-talk by establishing disjoint `EntityID`, `PairID`, `EpisodeID`, `EventID`, `HazardID`, and `AlertID`.
