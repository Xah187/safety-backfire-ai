# V3.1 — Baseline vs Improved Architectural Comparison

## 1. Executive Summary

Safety Backfire AI V3.1 represents a paradigm shift from a narrow, single-pair collision detection system (V3.0.5) to a comprehensive, entity-agnostic warehouse safety intelligence architecture.

Importantly, V3.1 **does not discard or replace** the hard-won mathematical proofs and statistical calibrations achieved in V3.0.1 through V3.0.5. Instead, the validated V3.0.5 collision engine is encapsulated as a specialized policy component (`Policy A`) within the Universal Risk Engine, while new reasoning layers expand coverage across the entire industrial facility.

## 2. Dimension-by-Dimension Architectural Comparison

| Architectural Dimension | V3.0.5 Frozen Baseline | V3.1 Universal Safety Core | Engineering Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **System Scope** | Single Pair (`Person ↔ Forklift`) | Full Warehouse Multi-Entity Graph | Protects entire facility, not just vehicle encounters. |
| **Entity Taxonomy** | 2 ad-hoc classes (`person`, `forklift`) | 6 typed hierarchies, 25+ distinct subtypes | Distinguishes pedestrians, operators, racks, pallets, AGVs, machinery. |
| **Hazard Coverage** | 2 hazard modes (Collision, Man-Down) | 20 controlled standardized hazard types | Captures collapses, blocked egress, pinch points, chemical spills, dock edges. |
| **Static Threat Reasoning ($v_{\text{rel}} \approx 0$)** | Vulnerable (TTC undefined/infinite) | Explicit `STRUCTURAL_THREAT` & `OBSTRUCTION` | Prevents stationary hazards from being evaluated as SAFE (**FAIL-34**). |
| **Zone Management** | Bounding box spatial checks | Projected ground footprint polygons | Eliminates perspective bounding-box bleed across lanes (**FAIL-32**). |
| **Interventions** | Unmodeled external disturbances | First-class `InterventionRecord` with counterfactual timelines | Enables causal reasoning on safety modifications. |
| **Safety Backfire Detection** | None (Blind to risk migration) | Dedicated `SafetyBackfireEngine` | Detects risk displacement, zone migration, and collateral hazards (**FAIL-36**). |
| **Temporal Integrity** | Video playback hysteresis | Strict causal windowing with `TemporalLeakageError` | Guarantees zero post-intervention baseline pollution (**FAIL-33**). |
| **ID Decoupling** | Tied to tracker track IDs | 6 independent namespaces (`EntityID`, `PairID`, `HazardID`, `EventID`, `EpisodeID`, `AlertID`) | Eliminates tracker ID switch corruptions (**Axiom 4**). |
| **Epistemic Invariants** | Informal heuristics | 7 formal mathematical axioms (`UNKNOWN != SAFE`, etc.) | Eliminates silent failure modes and epistemic blind spots. |
| **Uncertainty Model** | 2D Rice $k=2.24$, Jensen debiasing | Fully preserved in `CollisionPolicy` + decomposed $\mathbf{C} \in [0, 1]^8$ | Maintains $>95\%$ coverage while tracking weakest pipeline link. |

## 4. Qualitative Engineering Evolution

1. **From Bounding Boxes to Physical Grounding:** V3.0.5 reasoned over pixel coordinates corrected to ground plane. V3.1 models entities with ground contact support polygons, physical mass distributions, structural states, and operational modes.
2. **From Heuristic Alerts to Causal Accountability:** Prior versions emitted alarms when thresholds crossed. V3.1 attributes alarms to specific hazard lifecycles, tracks whether corrective interventions occurred, and verifies whether the intervention produced unintended safety backfire.
