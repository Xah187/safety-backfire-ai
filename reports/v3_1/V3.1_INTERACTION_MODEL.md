# V3.1 — Interaction Model & Severity Taxonomy

## 1. Overview & Concept

The **V3.1 Interaction Model** bridges geometric relations and hazard classification. While a relation describes the physical state (e.g., $d = 1.4\,\text{m}, v = 1.2\,\text{m/s}$), an **Interaction** characterizes the physical nature of the encounter, the potential consequence severity, and the temporal urgency (imminence).

---

## 2. Interaction Typology

```text
InteractionType
├── COLLISION_RISK (Imminent kinematic vector collision between moving bodies)
├── IMPACT (Physical contact or crash detected)
├── NEAR_COLLISION (Close call requiring evasive maneuver)
├── BLOCKING (Physical obstruction preventing path traversal)
├── ENCROACHMENT (Unintended entry into defensive safety zone)
├── FALL_EXPOSURE (Worker exposed to unprotected edge or elevation drop)
├── COLLAPSE_EXPOSURE (Personnel positioned underneath deformed structure/load)
├── ENTRAPMENT_EXPOSURE (Personnel near pinch point or moving machine parts)
├── RESTRICTED_ZONE_INTRUSION (Unauthorized presence in hazardous zone)
├── UNSAFE_LOADING (Improper fork placement, off-center lift)
├── UNSAFE_UNLOADING (Unstable placement onto high-bay storage)
└── BENIGN_INTERACTION (Cooperative or distant safe coexistence)
```

---

## 3. Consequence Severity Scale

Severity reflects the worst-case physical outcome according to industrial safety engineering (ISO 13849 / ANSI B56):

| Severity Level | Physical Consequence Description | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `CATASTROPHIC` | Fatality, permanent disabling injury, or catastrophic collapse of structural bay. | Forklift running over prone worker; high-bay rack collapse onto occupied aisle; robotic arm impact. |
| `CRITICAL` | Severe injury, fracture, deep laceration, or major equipment destruction. | Vehicle-pedestrian impact at $>2\,\text{m/s}$; pallet load falling from $>3\,\text{m}$; dock edge vehicle plunge. |
| `MODERATE` | Minor reversible injury, contusion, sprain, or localized pallet damage. | Slow nudge ($<1\,\text{m/s}$); slip on wet floor; load tilt onto rack beam without collapse. |
| `MINOR` | Operational delay, minor scrape, or low-speed glancing contact. | Pallet dragging on lane floor; forklift encroaching into buffer without collision. |
| `NEGLIGIBLE` | No physical impact, damage, or operational perturbation. | Normal warehouse operations adhering to designated corridors. |

---

## 4. Temporal Imminence Criteria

Imminence determines the required response time window for warning alarms and vehicle interlocks:

$$\text{Imminence} = f(d_{\text{min}}, v_{\text{approach}}, \text{TTC}, \text{HazardType})$$

| Imminence Level | Physical Thresholds | System Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `IMMINENT` | $\text{TTC} < 1.5\,\text{s}$ or $d \le 1.0\,\text{m}$ with approaching velocity | **Emergency Alarm & Immediate Vehicle Deceleration** |
| `HIGH` | $\text{TTC} < 2.5\,\text{s}$ or $d \le 1.8\,\text{m}$ (or structural deformation $>2^\circ$) | **Audible Warning & Caution Alert to Operator** |
| `MEDIUM` | $1.8\,\text{m} < d \le 3.0\,\text{m}$ with converging trajectory | **Visual Advisory on Zone Display** |
| `LOW` | $d > 3.0\,\text{m}$ with converging heading | **Tracking & Trajectory Surveillance** |
| `NONE` | Diverging trajectories or static separation $> 5.0\,\text{m}$ | **Nominal Monitoring** |

---

## 5. Multi-Entity Interaction Resolution Matrix

When evaluating an entity $E_i$ interacting with multiple surrounding entities $\{E_1, E_2, \dots, E_k\}$, the system evaluates all pairwise interactions independently:
- **No Masking Guarantee:** A benign interaction $\text{Interaction}(E_i, E_{\text{forklift\_far}}) = \text{BENIGN}$ cannot suppress $\text{Interaction}(E_i, E_{\text{collapsing\_rack}}) = \text{COLLAPSE\_EXPOSURE}$ (**Axiom 7** / **FAIL-35**).
- **Composite Context:** If a vehicle is approaching a pedestrian while simultaneously traversing a liquid spill, the interaction severity escalates to `CRITICAL` due to compounded traction loss and braking distance degradation.
