# V3.1 — Failure Mode Registry & Defect Catalog

## 1. Governance & Strict Engineering Principles

In strict adherence to safety-critical robotics, computational geometry, and autonomous systems standards:
1. **NO FAILURE DELETION:** Every failure mode identified from V2.9 through V3.1 is permanently archived.
2. **NO ID REUSE:** Identifiers `FAIL-01` through `FAIL-37` are globally unique and immutable.
3. **SCIENTIFIC PROOF OF REMEDIATION:** A defect is only marked **RESOLVED** when verified on independent out-of-sample stress suites with formal physical, statistical, or invariant proofs.

## 3. Deep Dive on Newly Addressed Defects (FAIL-31 through FAIL-37)

### 3.1 FAIL-31: Entity Subtype Hallucination under Occlusion
- **Symptom:** A worker standing behind a pallet stack had low feature visibility ($c_{\text{class}} = 0.35$). The perception model hallucinated `PersonSubtype.OPERATOR`, falsely suppressing proximity alarms.
- **Root Cause:** Hard argmax classification without a confidence threshold floor.
- **Fix:** When classification confidence $< 0.60$, subtype defaults to `UNKNOWN_PERSON`. The risk engine treats `UNKNOWN_PERSON` with full defensive clearance buffers. Verified in `test_fail_31_entity_subtype_hallucination_under_occlusion`.

### 3.2 FAIL-32: Relation Zone False Positive from 2D Bbox Footpoint Bleed
- **Symptom:** A worker standing inside a pedestrian lane ($X = 2.8\,\text{m}$) triggered a false forklift-lane intrusion alert because their 2D bounding box extended to $X = 3.2\,\text{m}$.
- **Root Cause:** Evaluating zone containment on image-space bounding box extents.
- **Fix:** Containment queries operate strictly on projected ground contact coordinates $(X_w, Y_w)$, isolating perspective bleed. Verified in `test_fail_32_relation_zone_false_positive_footpoint_bleed`.

### 3.3 FAIL-33: Counterfactual Temporal Baseline Leakage
- **Symptom:** During retrospective safety audits, late-arriving frames with timestamps after an intervention altered baseline risk rates.
- **Root Cause:** Lack of an immutable temporal barrier in counterfactual timeline structures.
- **Fix:** `CounterfactualTimeline` strictly checks $t < T_{\text{intervention}}$ and raises `TemporalLeakageError` upon any post-intervention frame submission. Verified in `test_fail_33_counterfactual_temporal_baseline_leakage`.

### 3.4 FAIL-34: Inappropriate Kinetic TTC on Stationary Hazards
- **Symptom:** An unstable leaning rack ($v_{\text{rel}} = 0$) resulted in $\text{TTC} = \infty$. A kinematic risk engine evaluated this as `SAFE`.
- **Root Cause:** Over-reliance on kinetic TTC across static structural hazards.
- **Fix:** Specialized `StructuralLoadPolicy` decouples structural integrity and clearance from kinematic TTC. Verified in `test_fail_34_inappropriate_kinetic_ttc_on_stationary_hazard`.

### 3.5 FAIL-35: Cross-Hazard Risk Masking
- **Symptom:** A benign relationship with a distant forklift caused an aggregate scene score to report normal status while a worker was simultaneously beneath a collapsing pallet load.
- **Root Cause:** Scene-level score averaging or first-match exit logic.
- **Fix:** Max-risk aggregation rule guarantees that the worst-case hazard dictates scene safety status. Verified in `test_fail_35_cross_hazard_risk_masking`.

### 3.6 FAIL-36: Aggregate Risk Transfer Blindness
- **Symptom:** Total warehouse risk exposure dropped by 76% following barrier installation, while completely missing that the barrier blocked an emergency fire door.
- **Root Cause:** Scalar risk summation ignoring categorical qualitative hazards.
- **Fix:** The Safety Backfire Engine audits introduced hazard types independently of aggregate exposure sums, flagging `NEW_HAZARD_CREATED`. Verified in `test_fail_36_aggregate_risk_transfer_blindness`.

### 3.7 FAIL-37: Temporal Entity State Resurrection across Dropouts
- **Symptom:** A track lost for $2.5\,\text{s}$ re-emerged and immediately inherited a stale $2.5\,\text{m/s}$ velocity vector from 75 frames earlier, creating a phantom collision alarm.
- **Root Cause:** Unconditional state restoration upon track ID re-association.
- **Fix:** Latency $> 0.5\,\text{s}$ triggers `ODDStatus.DEGRADED`, resetting kinematic filters and emitting `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` until track stability is re-established. Verified in `test_fail_37_temporal_entity_state_resurrection_dropouts`.
