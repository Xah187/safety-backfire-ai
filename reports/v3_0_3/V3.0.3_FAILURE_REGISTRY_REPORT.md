## 3. Deep Dive on New V3.0.3 Discoveries (FAIL-23, FAIL-24, FAIL-25)

### 3.1 FAIL-23: Non-Linear Distance Metric Bias & Empirical Coverage Under-Coverage
- **Discovery**: During independent empirical coverage testing on 100 ground truth samples, nominal $95\%$ credible intervals achieved only **$89.0\%$ empirical coverage** ($[81.4\%, 93.7\%]$ Wilson CI), failing the $\ge 90\%$ acceptance threshold.
- **Root Cause**: Euclidean norm $d = \|\Delta \mathbf{x}\|$ is a strictly convex function. Independent 2D coordinate measurement noise creates a systematic positive bias ($+0.1269\,\text{m}$) and positive skewness ($+0.5676$). Symmetric first-order Gaussian bounds underestimate variance in the direction of approach.
- **Impact**: True clearance between vehicle and pedestrian is occasionally smaller than the nominal interval's lower bound.
- **Status**: **`OPEN DEFECT (FAIL-23)`**. Requires second-order bias compensation or conformal quantile calibration in V3.0.4.

### 3.2 FAIL-24: Linear Perspective Elevation Formula Truncation Breakdown
- **Discovery**: In V3.0.2, $\Delta y = \frac{f_y h_o \cos\theta}{D}$ was claimed to have "0.000px error". Formal evaluation against the exact projective transformation revealed truncation errors up to **$1112.55\,\text{px}$** at steep pitches ($45^\circ$) and close depths ($2.0\,\text{m}$).
- **Root Cause**: The formula is a first-order linear Taylor approximation assuming $h_o \ll H_c$ and $D \gg H_c$. At steep camera tilts, the denominator change in the camera frame coordinate $Z_c$ cannot be neglected.
- **Impact**: Significant miscalculation of ground footpoint if used on steep ceiling-mounted cameras ($> 35^\circ$).
- **Status**: **`RESOLVED WITH DOCUMENTED OPERATIONAL BOUNDARY`**. The exact projective method is now implemented in `GeometryEngineV3.compute_exact_projective_elevation_shift_px()`.

### 3.3 FAIL-25: Synthetic Benchmark Timestamp De-synchronization
- **Discovery**: During initial execution of Regime B, `arrival_to_decision_ms` reported negative values ($-11575\,\text{ms}$).
- **Root Cause**: In the benchmark script, packet PTS was incremented by $40\,\text{ms}$ while the consumer executed in $14\,\text{ms}$, allowing the simulated stream to drift $26\,\text{seconds}$ ahead of wall-clock time.
- **Impact**: Flawed latency reporting in synthetic benchmarks.
- **Status**: **`RESOLVED`**. Re-synchronized with strict real-time wall-clock pacing.
