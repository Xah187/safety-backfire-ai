## 2. Certified Operational Design Domain (ODD)

The mathematical and physical validity of the V3.0.5 Core rests upon the following six strict boundary constraints:

### 1. Camera Mount Physical Constraints
- **Mounting Height ($H_c$):** $2.5\,\text{m} \le H_c \le 4.5\,\text{m}$ (must be physically surveyed to within $\pm 0.08\,\text{m}$).
- **Depression Pitch Angle ($\theta$):** $20.0^\circ \le \theta \le 35.0^\circ$.
- **Mechanical Vibration & Deflection Ceiling:** $\Delta\theta \le \pm 0.35^\circ$.
  * *Physics Proof:* Forward ground error scales quadratically with depth ($\frac{\partial Y}{\partial \theta} \approx \frac{Y^2 + H^2}{H}$). A pitch deflection of $1.0^\circ$ produces $0.38\,\text{m}$ error at $8\,\text{m}$ and $1.18\,\text{m}$ error at $15\,\text{m}$. Brackets must maintain rigid mechanical stability under industrial vibration (FAIL-30).

### 2. Physical Metric Range Envelope

  * Spatial uncertainty remains tightly bounded ($\sigma_d \le 0.35\,\text{m}$, 95% interval width $\le 1.35\,\text{m}$).
- **Situational Awareness & Monitoring Zone ($8.0\,\text{m} < D \le 15.0\,\text{m}$):**
  * Restricted to candidate trajectory tracking and warning advisory. High-precision clearance decisions are de-certified due to inherent monocular depth dilution (FAIL-26).

### 3. Warehouse Floor Planarity
- **Planar Ground Invariant ($Z_w = 0$):**

  * Operation on loading dock ramps, thresholds, or transition inclines $> 3^\circ$ violates the planar contract (FAIL-27) and requires multi-plane homography.

### 4. Optical Lens Rectification
- **Pinhole Projection Invariant:**
