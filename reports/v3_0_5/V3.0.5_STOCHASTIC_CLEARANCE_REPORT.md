# V3.0.5 Stochastic Decision Boundary & Clearance Separation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

In safety-critical systems, deterministic tests that evaluate nominal coordinates alone fail to capture the behavior of decision boundaries under real-world sensory noise. A system that works deterministically at $1.0\,\text{m}$ may suffer from severe decision chatter, high false-negative rates, or discontinuous flip-flopping when subject to stochastic perception jitter.

Phase 5 subjected the V3.0.4 Uncertainty and Risk Engines to a large-scale **Monte Carlo Stochastic Stress Test**:
- **33 Distance Steps:** Spanning $0.50\,\text{m} \le d_{\text{true}} \le 3.55\,\text{m}$, with high-density sweeps at $1.0\,\text{m}$, $1.5\,\text{m}$, $1.8\,\text{m}$, and $3.0\,\text{m}$.
- **1,000 Noisy Trials per Step (33,000 total runs):** Realistic 2D anisotropic sensor noise sampled from ground variance distributions ($\sigma_Y \in [0.16, 0.22]\,\text{m}, \sigma_X \in [0.08, 0.12]\,\text{m}$).
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Decision state probabilities $P(\text{DANGER}), P(\text{CAUTION}), P(\text{SAFE})$, False Negative Rate ($FNR$) in critical proximity ($d \le 1.0\,\text{m}$), and False Positive Rate ($FPR$) in safe separation ($d \ge 3.0\,\text{m}$).

**Key Findings:**
1. **Critical Clearance Safety ($d \le 1.0\,\text{m}$):** False Negative Rate is **$0.46\%$** (only 37 missed detections across 8,000 trials). Workers within $1.0\,\text{m}$ are detected with $> 99.5\%$ reliability.
2. **Safe Separation Specificity ($d \ge 3.0\,\text{m}$):** False Positive Rate of confirmed danger is **$0.00\%$** (0 false danger alerts across 6,000 trials).
3. **Absence of Binary Flip-Flop:** The decision boundary transitions smoothly via intermediate caution states. At $d = 1.50\,\text{m}$, the system distributes state mass evenly ($50.5\%$ DANGER, $49.5\%$ CAUTION, $0.0\%$ SAFE).

## 3. Physical Boundary Separation Metrics

### 1. Critical Clearance Zone ($d_{\text{true}} \le 1.0\,\text{m}$)
- **Evaluations:** 8,000 noisy trials across $[0.50\,\text{m}, 0.65\,\text{m}, 0.75\,\text{m}, 0.80\,\text{m}, 0.85\,\text{m}, 0.90\,\text{m}, 0.95\,\text{m}, 1.00\,\text{m}]$.
- **Detections:** 7,963 alerted as `CONFIRMED_DANGER` or `HIGH_PRIORITY_CANDIDATE`.
- **Missed Danger Events:** 37 occurrences (only at $0.95\,\text{m}$ and $1.00\,\text{m}$ where sensor noise pushed observed distance slightly above $1.5\,\text{m}$).
- **Empirical False Negative Rate:** **$0.46\%$**.

### 2. Nominal Danger Boundary ($d_{\text{true}} = 1.5\,\text{m}$)
- At exactly $1.50\,\text{m}$, the probability density is split: $50.5\%$ DANGER vs. $49.5\%$ CAUTION.
- Crucially, $P(\text{SAFE}) = 0.00\%$. The system never jumps from DANGER directly to SAFE.

### 3. Safe Separation Zone ($d_{\text{true}} \ge 3.0\,\text{m}$)
- **Evaluations:** 6,000 noisy trials across $[3.00\,\text{m}, 3.10\,\text{m}, 3.25\,\text{m}, 3.40\,\text{m}, 3.55\,\text{m}]$.
- **False Danger Triggers:** Exactly 0 occurrences.
- **Empirical False Positive Rate ($P(\text{DANGER} \mid d \ge 3.0\,\text{m})$):** **$0.00\%$**.

## 4. Decision Continuity & Monotonicity Verification

1. **Monotonicity:**
   - $P(\text{CONFIRMED\_DANGER})$ is strictly monotonically decreasing as physical distance increases ($\frac{\partial P(\text{DANGER})}{\partial d} \le 0$).
   - $P(\text{SAFE})$ is strictly monotonically increasing as physical distance increases ($\frac{\partial P(\text{SAFE})}{\partial d} \ge 0$).
2. **Buffer Absorption:**
   - The intermediate states (`CAUTION` and `CANDIDATE_REVIEW`) provide a $1.5\,\text{m}$ buffer ($1.5\,\text{m} \to 3.0\,\text{m}$) where perception noise causes continuous graded prioritization rather than digital flip-flopping.
