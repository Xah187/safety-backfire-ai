# V3.0.5 — Camera Adversarial Sensitivity & Perturbation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This report investigates the vulnerability of the metric ground estimation pipeline to extrinsic and intrinsic calibration inaccuracies.
We subjected nominal warehouse calibration ($H_c = 3.5\,\text{m}$, $\theta = 28^\circ$, $f = 950\,\text{px}$) to 20 distinct adversarial perturbations:
- Focal length ($f_x, f_y \pm 1\%$)
- Principal point ($c_x, c_y \pm 10\,\text{px}$)
- Camera pitch ($\theta \pm 0.5^\circ, \pm 1.0^\circ, \pm 2.0^\circ$)
- Camera height ($H_c \pm 0.05\,\text{m}, \pm 0.10\,\text{m}, \pm 0.25\,\text{m}$)

## 3. Analytical Derivation of Error Propagation

Let $Y = H_c \cot(\theta + \alpha)$.
By differentiation with respect to each extrinsic and intrinsic parameter:

### 3.1 Pitch Angle Sensitivity ($\frac{\partial Y}{\partial \theta}$)
$$\frac{\partial Y}{\partial \theta} = - H_c \csc^2(\theta + \alpha) = - \frac{Y^2 + H_c^2}{H_c}$$
- **Critical Property**: The error scales **quadratically with range ($Y^2$)**!
- At range $Y = 12\,\text{m}$ and height $H_c = 3.5\,\text{m}$:
  $$\left|\frac{\partial Y}{\partial \theta}\right| = \frac{144 + 12.25}{3.5} \approx 44.64\,\text{m / rad} \approx 0.78\,\text{m / degree}$$
- **Safety Consequence**: An imperceptible camera mount vibration of $1.5^\circ$ (e.g. from thermal expansion or heavy machinery vibration on the roof truss) produces a **$1.17\,\text{m}$ error at $12\,\text{m}$**.

### 3.2 Mounting Height Sensitivity ($\frac{\partial Y}{\partial H_c}$)
$$\frac{\partial Y}{\partial H_c} = \frac{Y}{H_c}$$
- **Critical Property**: Scales **linearly with range**.
- A $10\,\text{cm}$ vertical survey error yields $0.10 \times \frac{8.0}{3.5} = 0.23\,\text{m}$ error at $8.0\,\text{m}$.

## 4. ODD Boundary Enforcement Mandates

Based on these sensitivity derivatives, V3.0.5 establishes strict operational boundaries:
1. **Mandatory Mechanical Rigidity**: Ceiling camera brackets must be verified rigid to within $\pm 0.35^\circ$ angular deflection under mechanical load.
2. **Fail-Closed Elevation Uncertainty**: If calibration is unverified, height uncertainty $\sigma_H = 0.20\,\text{m}$ and pitch uncertainty $\sigma_\theta = 1.5^\circ$ must be propagated into downstream covariance, preventing false high-confidence clearance claims.
3. **Certified Metric Range**: High-precision clearance decisions ($\le 1.0\,\text{m}$ safety envelope) are certified only within $D \le 8.0\,\text{m}$.
