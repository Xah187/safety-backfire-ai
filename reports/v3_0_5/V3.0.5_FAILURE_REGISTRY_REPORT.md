# V3.0.5 — Failure Mode Registry & Defect Tracking Report

## 1. Registry Policy & Governance

In strict compliance with safety-critical robotics and computer vision engineering principles:
- **NO FAILURE DELETION**: All failures identified from V2.9 through V3.0.5 are permanently recorded.
- **NO ID REUSE**: Every failure mode retains a globally unique, immutable identifier (`FAIL-01` through `FAIL-30`).
- **SCIENTIFIC PROOF OF REMEDIATION**: A failure is only marked **RESOLVED** when verified on out-of-sample data with zero data leakage and formal statistical or physical proofs.

## 3. Deep Dive on Newly Addressed Defects (FAIL-28, FAIL-29, FAIL-30)

### 3.1 FAIL-28: In-Sample Calibration Leakage in Uncertainty Engine
- **Identified**: Phase 1 forensic data leakage audit uncovered that V3.0.4 tuned $k=2.24$ on the same synthetic sample partition that reported $95.3\%$ empirical coverage.
- **V3.0.5 Investigation & Red-Team Action**:
  - Established two strictly disjoint partitions with zero sample overlap:
    * Partition A (Calibration): 250 pairs (Seed 5555).
    * Partition B (Blind Test): 500 pairs (Seed 9999).
  - Introduced realistic anisotropic monocular depth noise ($\sigma_Y \in [1.8\times, 3.2\times] \sigma_X$).
- **Resolution**:
  - Independent sweep on Partition A identified optimal $k = 2.10$ for nominal $95\%$ coverage.
  - Production parameter $k=2.24$ achieved **$96.8\%$** empirical coverage on the 500 blind test samples (Wilson 95% CI: $[94.9\%, 98.0\%]$).
  - Critical lower-bound safety violations were limited to **$1.0\%$**.
  - **Status: RESOLVED & INDEPENDENTLY FALSIFIED.**

### 3.2 FAIL-29: Trajectory Threat Inversion in Caution Zone Policy Branch
- **Identified**: Phase 6 kinetic threat testing revealed that an approaching forklift with active collision trajectory ($TTC = 1.5\,\text{s}$) in the Caution Zone ($d = 2.2\,\text{m}$) resulted in decision `CAUTION`, while the same collision threat at $3.5\,\text{m}$ (Safe Zone) correctly escalated to `HIGH_PRIORITY_CANDIDATE`.
- **Root Cause**: Policy Branch 3 (`nom_risk == "CAUTION"`) in `UncertaintyAwareRiskEngine.evaluate()` required both `plausible_danger` (which checked whether static distance uncertainty overlapped $1.5\,\text{m}$) AND `has_trajectory_threat`. At $2.2\,\text{m}$ with tight sensor covariance, lower bound was $> 1.5\,\text{m}$, causing the active collision trajectory to fall through to passive `CAUTION`.
- **Resolution**:
  - Refactored Policy Branch 3 to prioritize `has_trajectory_threat` independently of static `plausible_danger`.
  - When evidence quality $\ge 0.60$, an active collision trajectory in the Caution Zone immediately triggers `CONFIRMED_DANGER`.
  - Re-tested across 5,000 scenario pairs: 0 inversions detected.
  - **Status: RESOLVED & INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED.**

### 3.3 FAIL-30: Camera Pitch Mechanical Deflection Drift
- **Identified**: Phase 2 camera parameter sensitivity analysis evaluated 2,700 grid configurations across camera heights ($2.0-5.0\,\text{m}$), pitch angles ($10-45^\circ$), and ranges ($0.5-20.0\,\text{m}$).
- **Mathematical Physics Proof**:
  - Pitch sensitivity scales quadratically with forward distance:
    $$\frac{\partial Y}{\partial \theta} \approx \frac{Y^2 + H_c^2}{H_c}$$
  - A $1.0^\circ$ pitch drift (e.g. from forklift mast vibration or mounting flex) causes $0.38\,\text{m}$ error at $8\,\text{m}$ and $1.18\,\text{m}$ error at $15\,\text{m}$.
- **Operational Boundary Specification**:
  - Camera bracket deflection must be mechanically constrained to $\le \pm 0.35^\circ$ to guarantee $< 0.15\,\text{m}$ error within the certified $D \le 8.0\,\text{m}$ clearance envelope.
  - If vibration exceeds this threshold, auto-calibration or IMU pitch tracking is required.
  - **Status: DOCUMENTED SYSTEM BOUNDARY.**
