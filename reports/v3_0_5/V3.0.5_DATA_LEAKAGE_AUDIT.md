# V3.0.5 — Data Leakage Forensic Audit Report

## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate

In safety-critical systems, high reported accuracy can be an artifact of data leakage—where parameters, thresholds, or mathematical models are selected, tuned, or verified against the exact same data points.
This audit performs an end-to-end dependency trace across all datasets, configurations, and validation benchmarks from V3.0.3 to V3.0.4.

### Audit Verdict: **DATA LEAKAGE CONFIRMED (`FAIL-28`)**
The audit uncovered two distinct instances of methodological leakage:
1. **Uncertainty Calibration Leakage**: The expansion factor $k = 2.24$ and Jensen debiasing scale were tuned against the 100/150 synthetic distance pairs and subsequently evaluated on the same partition to claim $95.3\%$ empirical coverage.
2. **GCP Evaluation Reuse**: The 25 Ground Control Points (GCPs) used to diagnose the $-3.974\,\text{m}$ bias in V3.0.3 were reused in V3.0.4 as the primary validation evidence without an independent held-out camera geometry or spatial datum.

## 3. Forensic Trace of Identified Leakages

### 3.1 Leakage Item 1: Uncertainty Expansion Factor ($k=2.24$)
- **Investigation**: In V3.0.4 development, `test_uncertainty_recovery_v3_0_4.py` evaluated 8 ablations on a 150-sample dataset generated with `np.random.seed(101)`.
- **Finding**: The choice of $k = 2.24$ was selected by testing values $k \in [1.96, 2.10, 2.20, 2.24, 2.30]$ directly against the dataset until the empirical coverage crossed the $95.0\%$ threshold ($95.3\%$).
- **Defect**: This constitutes **in-sample optimization**. In statistical estimation, evaluating coverage on the training/tuning set guarantees optimistic coverage numbers and fails to represent true out-of-sample generalization.
- **Defect Classification**: **`FAIL-28 — In-Sample Calibration Leakage in Uncertainty Engine`**.

### 3.2 Leakage Item 2: Fixed 25 GCP Spatial Grid
- **Investigation**: The 25 GCPs were laid out on an exact regular grid:
  $$X \in [-3.0\,\text{m}, -1.5\,\text{m}, 0.0\,\text{m}, +1.5\,\text{m}, +3.0\,\text{m}]$$
  $$Y \in [3.0\,\text{m}, 4.5\,\text{m}, 6.0\,\text{m}, 8.0\,\text{m}, 10.0\,\text{m}]$$
  with fixed camera height $H = 3.5\,\text{m}$ and pitch $\theta = 28^\circ$.
- **Finding**: While Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) was performed across points, **zero variations in camera extrinsics (height, pitch, roll) or lens intrinsics were tested**.
- **Defect**: LOOCV over a fixed camera placement only proves spatial interpolation within that specific camera mount; it does not prove camera configuration invariance.

## 4. Remediation Protocol for V3.0.5

To eliminate all data leakage in V3.0.5:
1. **Strict Partitioning**:
   - **Partition A (Calibration Split)**: 250 independent pairs (Seed 5555) reserved exclusively for parameter sweeps and $k$-factor derivation.
   - **Partition B (Blind Out-of-Sample Red Team)**: 500 completely independent pairs (Seed 9999) reserved strictly for final validation.
2. **Multi-Camera Geometry Generalization**:
   - Evaluate across 6 camera heights ($2.0\,\text{m} \to 5.0\,\text{m}$) and 9 pitch angles ($10^\circ \to 45^\circ$) across 10 range bands.
3. **Formal Defect Registration**:
   - Register `FAIL-28` in `reports/v3_0_5/V3.0.5_FAILURE_REGISTRY_REPORT.md`.
