# V3.0.5 Adversarial Physics & Operator Boundary Report

## 1. Executive Summary

A critical safety perception challenge in warehouse automation is the **Operator Suppression Boundary**. Forklift operators seated inside the protective cabin must not trigger nuisance collision alerts. However, the system must never accidentally suppress:
1. A pedestrian physically contacting or leaning against the vehicle exterior.
2. A distant pedestrian whose 2D camera bounding box visually overlaps the forklift due to perspective projection.
3. Multiple people in the scene where one is the driver and another is an external worker in danger.
4. An operator who dismounts or steps out of the protective cabin ROI.

Phase 7 evaluated these adversarial boundary cases alongside temporal latency perturbations ($0\,\text{ms} \to 500\,\text{ms}$) and input timestamp skew.

**Key Findings:**
- **Zero False Suppressions:** External workers contacting the bumper or visually overlapping in perspective were 100% correctly flagged as `CONFIRMED_DANGER` or `SAFE`, never suppressed.
- **Concurrent Identity Decoupling:** In multi-person scenes, the cabin driver was suppressed while the nearby worker was alerted with zero cross-contamination.
- **Fail-Closed Temporal Boundary:** If pose latency exceeds $150\,\text{ms}$ (temporal skew contract) or $350\,\text{ms}$ (source age contract), suppression authority is immediately revoked, forcing the system into a safe state.

## 2. Operator Suppression Boundary Edge Cases

| Test Case | Scenario Physical Geometry | Keypoint / Spatial Configuration | Classified Relation | Risk Policy Decision | Safety Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Case A: Cabin Operator** | Driver seated in seat ROI | Torso: $(400, 310)$, Ankles: $(400, 420)$ inside ROI | `OPERATOR_ON_VEHICLE` | `SUPPRESSED_OPERATOR` | **PASS** |
| **Case B: Touching Bumper** | Worker leaning on vehicle ($d = 0.2\,\text{m}$) | Torso: $(190, 340)$, Ankles: $(190, 490)$ outside ROI | `UNKNOWN` | `CONFIRMED_DANGER` | **PASS** |
| **Case C: Perspective Overlap** | Background pedestrian ($d = 4.0\,\text{m}$) | 2D bbox intersects forklift, 3D feet at 4.0 m | `INDEPENDENT_PEDESTRIAN`| `SAFE` | **PASS** |
| **Case D: Concurrent 2 People** | Driver in cabin + Worker at $0.8\,\text{m}$ | Simultaneous multi-person scene | Driver: `OPERATOR`<br>Worker: `UNKNOWN` | Driver: `SUPPRESSED`<br>Worker: `DANGER` | **PASS** |

### Boundary Mechanisms
- **2D/3D Decoupling:** Bounding-box intersection in the image plane does NOT dictate physical proximity. 3D ground projection places the pedestrian at $4.0\,\text{m}$, overriding visual overlap.
- **Cabin ROI Gate:** Feet/ankles must fall within the elevated operator compartment ($X \in [0.3, 1.0], Y \in [0.2, 0.9]$ relative to forklift bounds). Contact with external bumpers falls outside this volume, immediately activating `CONFIRMED_DANGER`.
