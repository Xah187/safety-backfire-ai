# V3.0.5 Risk Monotonicity, Uncertainty Interaction & TTC Decoupling Report

## 1. Executive Summary & Registration of FAIL-29

Safety-critical risk policies must obey strict mathematical and physical monotonicity laws:
1. **Distance Monotonicity:** For two identical targets under matched conditions, the closer target must always yield an equal or higher risk score ($\frac{\partial \text{Risk}}{\partial d} \le 0$).
2. **Uncertainty Monotonicity:** Higher sensory uncertainty must elevate caution and review priority without causing false confirmed alarms or masking proximity hazards.
3. **Kinetic Decoupling:** Stationary objects within clearance envelopes must trigger proximity hazards regardless of missing velocity/TTC ($TTC = \infty$). Approaching collision trajectories must escalate risk even from caution distances.

During red-team adversarial evaluation of 5,000 scenario pairs and kinetic threat scenarios, an urgent architectural defect was discovered and permanently registered:
- **`FAIL-29: Trajectory Threat Inversion in Caution Zone Policy Branch`**
  - **Symptom:** An active collision trajectory ($TTC = 1.5\,\text{s}$) in the Caution Zone ($d = 2.2\,\text{m}$) resulted in decision `CAUTION`, while a collision trajectory in the Safe Zone ($d = 3.5\,\text{m}$) escalated to `HIGH_PRIORITY_CANDIDATE`.
  - **Root Cause:** In `UncertaintyAwareRiskEngine.evaluate()`, Policy Branch 3 required both `plausible_danger` (which checked whether static distance uncertainty overlapped $1.5\,\text{m}$) AND `has_trajectory_threat`. If uncertainty was tight such that $\text{lower\_95} > 1.5\,\text{m}$, the active collision trajectory was dropped, falling through to passive `CAUTION`.
  - **Remediation:** Refactored Policy Branch 3 so that `has_trajectory_threat` with high evidence quality ($\ge 0.60$) directly triggers `CONFIRMED_DANGER`, preserving correct threat hierarchy across all distance tiers.

Following remediation, 100% of the 5,000 pairwise monotonicity tests and all kinetic decoupling benchmarks passed with zero violations.

### Key Decoupling Behaviors
1. **Stationary Proximity Hazards:** In industrial warehouses, stationary workers (e.g., palletizing, inspecting) must be protected. The system preserves `CONFIRMED_DANGER` and `CURRENT_PROXIMITY_HAZARD` even when $TTC = \text{None}$ and velocity is zero.
2. **Dual Hazard Classification:** When both spatial proximity and active kinematic convergence coincide, the system issues `DUAL_HAZARD`, providing granular evidence telemetry for downstream vehicle braking.
3. **Receding Separation:** When a forklift reverses away from a pedestrian ($v < 0$), TTC is deactivated, preventing false collision alarms while maintaining proximity caution.

## 4. Safe Degradation under Severe Uncertainty

When sensory evidence degrades or measurement noise expands drastically:
- **At Danger Distance ($d = 1.2\,\text{m}, \sigma = 0.8\,\text{m}, \text{evidence} = 0.30$):**
  The engine safely degrades from `CONFIRMED_DANGER` to `CANDIDATE_REVIEW`, requesting temporal confirmation without asserting false confidence.
- **At Safe Distance ($d = 4.0\,\text{m}, \sigma = 0.8\,\text{m}, \text{evidence} = 0.30$):**
  The engine safely degrades from `SAFE` to `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, preventing false safety certification.
