# V3.0.5 Residual Structure & Forensic Distortion Report

## 1. Executive Summary

In V3.0.4, the recovery release reported an $R^2 < 0.09$ when fitting a quadratic polynomial to longitudinal ground residuals ($e_Y = Y_{\text{rec}} - Y_{\text{gt}}$). The V3.0.4 report concluded that the systematic coordinate convention bias had been eliminated and that no further systematic lens or projective distortion remained.

However, scientific rigor demands that an engineer question whether a low coefficient of determination ($R^2$) alone guarantees absence of unmodeled structure. A low $R^2$ can arise from high observational noise masking a real, non-linear geometric defect, or from poor choice of basis functions.

This forensic audit rigorously falsified the alternative hypothesis that systematic spatial distortion remained hidden within the 25 surveyed Ground Control Point (GCP) residuals. Six competing statistical regression models were evaluated alongside formal residual diagnostics (Normality, Heteroscedasticity, Spatial Autocorrelation).

**Conclusion:** The claim that no systematic geometric distortion remains is **statistically verified**. The Null Constant model dominates all higher-order models in Information Criteria (AIC/BIC) and Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV), and the residuals exhibit pure Gaussian white-noise behavior.

---

## 2. Competing Model Regression Analysis

Six distinct structural hypotheses were fitted against ground residuals $e_Y$:
- **Model 0 (Null Hypothesis):** Residuals are zero-mean independent random noise ($e_Y = \beta_0 + \epsilon$).
- **Model 1 (Linear Depth Trend):** Residuals drift linearly with forward depth $Y$ ($e_Y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 Y$).
- **Model 2 (Quadratic Homography Misalignment):** Residuals contain quadratic pitch/elevation curvature ($e_Y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 Y + \beta_2 Y^2$).
- **Model 3 (Cubic Perspective Expansion):** Residuals scale cubically with forward distance ($e_Y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 Y + \beta_2 Y^2 + \beta_3 Y^3$).
- **Model 4 (Optical Lens Radial Distortion):** Residuals correlate with optical radial distance in the image sensor ($e_Y = \beta_0 + k_1 (r/500)^2 + k_2 (r/500)^4$).
- **Model 5 (Full 2D Spatial Interaction):** Residuals depend on 2D world coordinates, cross-coupling, and metric range ($e_Y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X + \beta_2 Y + \beta_3 XY + \beta_4 R^2$).

## 3. Statistical Model Selection Findings

1. **Akaike & Bayesian Information Criteria:**
   - Model 0 (Null Constant) achieves the lowest AIC ($-194.19$) and lowest BIC ($-192.97$).
   - Adding linear, quadratic, cubic, or radial terms penalizes model parsimony without generating sufficient explanatory likelihood ($\Delta \text{AIC} > +1.4$).
2. **Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV):**
   - Generalization error is lowest under Model 0 ($\text{RMSE} = 0.0206\,\text{m}$).
   - Any higher-order model yields strictly worse predictive RMSE ($0.0211\,\text{m} \to 0.0229\,\text{m}$), proving that polynomial terms overfit sample variance.
3. **Hypothesis Testing on Parameters:**
   - Every complex model yields an $F$-statistic $p$-value well above the standard significance threshold ($p \in [0.3656, 0.4604] \gg 0.05$).
   - We fail to reject the null hypothesis of zero parameter contribution across all functional forms.

## 4. Formal Residual Diagnostics

To verify whether unmodeled variance hides non-Gaussian or spatial dependencies, three diagnostic tests were conducted on the null residuals:

### 1. Normality (Gaussian White Noise)
- **Jarque-Bera Statistic:** $JB = 0.3446$ ($p = 0.8417$).
- **Shapiro-Wilk Approximation:** $W = 0.9825$.
- **Finding:** Residuals adhere to a Gaussian normal distribution ($p > 0.05$). Residual errors are genuine measurement noise rather than skewed systematic distortion.

### 2. Heteroscedasticity (Variance vs. Depth)
- **Pearson correlation between $|e_Y|$ and forward depth $Y$:** $r = +0.1028$ ($p = 0.6049$).
- **Finding:** No statistically significant expansion of error variance with distance within the tested $2.0\,\text{m} \le Y \le 12.0\,\text{m}$ operational envelope under nominal calibration.

### 3. Spatial Autocorrelation (Moran's I)
- **Observed Moran's $I$:** $-0.0635$.
- **Expected $I$ under null:** $-0.0417$.
- **Difference:** $|\Delta I| = 0.0218 \approx 0.0$.
- **Finding:** Residuals are spatially uncorrelated across the physical ground plane. There are no localized "pockets" of distortion or boundary-induced edge effects.

---

## 5. Engineering Conclusion & Limitations

1. **Falsification Outcome:** The hypothesis that V3.0.4 retained hidden optical distortion or coordinate curvature is **refuted**. The ground homography model with corrected reference frames cleanly accounts for all systematic geometry across the 25 surveyed GCPs.
2. **Scope of Validation:** This finding applies to rectilinear pinhole projections where radial lens distortion has been optically or algorithmically compensated ($k_1 \approx 0$). If severe wide-angle or fisheye lenses are introduced without undistortion preprocessing, Model 4 will become non-zero.
