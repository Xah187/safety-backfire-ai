# V3.2 — Continuous Boundary Chatter & Hysteresis Damping Report

## 1. Executive Summary

In cyber-physical alerting systems, decision chatter—where an alarm rapidly toggles between `DANGER` and `SAFE` due to microscopic sensor noise—is catastrophic. It causes actuator wear, acoustic alarm flutter, and severe operator alarm fatigue, leading facility personnel to ignore or disable safety infrastructure.

This report evaluates boundary stability in **Safety Backfire AI V3.2** across $600$ continuous real-world frames ($20.0\,\text{seconds}$) evaluated precisely at the safety decision threshold ($d = 1.50\,\text{m}$) with realistic footpoint spatial noise ($\sigma = 0.035\,\text{m} = 3.5\,\text{cm}$).
- **Raw Unfiltered Boundary Transitions:** $873.3\,\text{transitions/minute}$
- **V3.2 Confirmed Alert Transitions:** **$0.0\,\text{transitions/minute}$** ($0$ confirm-revoke cycles)
- **Minimum Dwell Time Enforced:** $2\,\text{frames}$ ($67\,\text{ms}$)
- **Hysteresis Band Applied:** $\Delta_{\text{clearance}} = 0.15\,\text{m}$ ($15.0\,\text{cm}$)

---

## 2. Chatter Dynamics & Failure Mode Forensics: `FAIL-55`

### 2.1 Defect Reproduction & Physics (`FAIL-55`)
- **Hypothesis (`CANDIDATE-55`):** When an entity lingers within $\pm 3.5\,\text{cm}$ of the danger boundary ($1.50\,\text{m}$), Gaussian camera pixel noise causes the calculated Euclidean clearance $d$ to oscillate above and below the threshold on alternate frames:
  ```text
  Frame 101: d = 1.48m  -> TRIGGER ALARM
  Frame 102: d = 1.52m  -> REVOKE ALARM
  Frame 103: d = 1.49m  -> TRIGGER ALARM
  Frame 104: d = 1.51m  -> REVOKE ALARM
  ```
- **Empirical Evidence:** In `VID_DEV_001`, a pedestrian standing at the edge of a pedestrian walkway $1.49\,\text{m}$ from a parked forklift triggered $291$ state toggles in $20\,\text{seconds}$ ($873\,\text{per minute}$).
- **Classification:** **CONFIRMED AS FAIL-55.**

---

## 3. Engineering Remediation: Dual-Stage Hysteresis Architecture

To permanently resolve `FAIL-55`, V3.2 implements a dual-stage spatial-temporal stabilization filter:

```text
               +--------------------------------------------------+
               |        Dual-Stage Stabilization Architecture     |
               +--------------------------------------------------+
                                        │
                                        ▼
                  +--------------------------------------------+
                  |         Stage 1: Spatial Hysteresis        |
                  |  - Trigger Distance:   d <= 1.50m          |
                  |  - Revocation Buffer:  d >= 1.65m (+0.15m) |
                  +--------------------------------------------+
                                        │
                                        ▼
                  +--------------------------------------------+
                  |        Stage 2: Temporal Dwell Filter      |
                  |  - Confirmation: 3 consecutive frames      |
                  |  - Revocation:   5 consecutive frames      |
                  +--------------------------------------------+
                                        │
                                        ▼
                  +--------------------------------------------+
                  |        STABLE ALERT DISPATCH (0 Chatter)   |
                  +--------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 Verification Outcome
1. **Zero Confirm-Revoke Cycles:** Under the stabilized architecture, once an alert is asserted, it remains continuously asserted until clearance strictly exceeds $1.65\,\text{m}$ for at least 5 frames ($167\,\text{ms}$).
2. **Alarm Stability:** In the continuous 600-frame test, confirm-revoke oscillations dropped from $291$ cycles to **0 cycles**, providing smooth, authoritative alerts to human operators and vehicle ECUs.
