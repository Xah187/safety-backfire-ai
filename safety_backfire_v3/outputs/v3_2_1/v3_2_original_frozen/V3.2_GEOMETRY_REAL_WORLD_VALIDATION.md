# V3.2 — Level D: Real Geometry & World Projection Validation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

Safety claims in warehouse environments depend upon calculating metric distances in world ground coordinates ($X_w, Y_w$ in meters). Perspective projection from 2D pixel footpoints to 3D ground planes is mathematically ill-posed unless floor planarity, camera mounting extrinsics, and optical intrinsics are modeled with exact geometric rigor.

This report evaluates the physical projection accuracy of **Safety Backfire AI V3.2** against $N = 100$ physically surveyed laser ground truth markers in warehouse facility `WH_SECTOR_B`.
- **Planar Floor Euclidean MAE:** $0.0169\,\text{m}$ ($1.69\,\text{cm}$) [$N = 90$ points]
- **Planar Floor RMSE:** $0.0237\,\text{m}$ ($2.37\,\text{cm}$)
- **Median Metric Error:** $0.0113\,\text{m}$ ($1.13\,\text{cm}$)
- **Maximum Observed Error:** $0.1030\,\text{m}$ ($10.3\,\text{cm}$ at $11.5\,\text{m}$ depth)

## 3. Adversarial Geometry Red-Team Findings

### 3.1 Camera Mounting Pitch Drift (`FAIL-44`)
- **Red-Team Stress:** Camera pitch was mechanically deflected in calibrated increments ($\pm 0.1^\circ, \pm 0.2^\circ, \pm 0.35^\circ, \pm 0.5^\circ, \pm 1.0^\circ$).
- **Theoretical Derivation:** The sensitivity of forward ground coordinate $Y_w$ to pitch angle $\theta$ is:
  $$\frac{\partial Y_w}{\partial \theta} \approx \frac{Y_w^2 + H^2}{H}$$
  For a camera at height $H = 5.0\,\text{m}$ observing an object at $Y_w = 8.0\,\text{m}$:
  $$\frac{\partial Y_w}{\partial \theta} = \frac{64 + 25}{5} = 17.8\,\text{m/rad} \approx 0.31\,\text{m per degree}$$
- **Empirical Confirmation:** A $0.5^\circ$ pitch drift caused a $0.52\,\text{m}$ error at $8.0\,\text{m}$, breaching the $0.50\,\text{m}$ defensive clearance buffer.
- **Classification:** **CONFIRMED AS FAIL-44 (Extending FAIL-30).**
- **Boundary Requirement:** Mechanical mounting brackets must maintain structural rigidity within $\pm 0.35^\circ$. If dynamic vibration exceeds $\pm 0.35^\circ$, the system must signal `GEOMETRY_UNCERTAIN`.

### 3.2 Floor Incline Ramp Warping (`FAIL-45`)
- **Hypothesis (`CANDIDATE-45`):** Warehouse loading dock ramps with a $3.5^\circ$ gradient violate the planar $Z_w = 0$ homography assumption.
- **Empirical Evidence:** In `WH_SECTOR_C` dock ramp, an object at ground height $Z_w = +0.25\,\text{m}$ projected $0.74\,\text{m}$ closer to the camera than its true surveyed position.
- **Safety Impact:** False sense of clearance; a forklift on an incline is detected as farther away than it physically is.
- **Classification:** **CONFIRMED AS FAIL-45 (Extending FAIL-27).**
- **Remediation:** Ramps and non-planar floor zones must be defined via 3D polygon meshes with explicit piecewise height equations, or formally designated as `OUT_OF_ODD`.

### 3.3 Peripheral Lens Distortion (`FAIL-46`)
- **Hypothesis (`CANDIDATE-46`):** Unmodeled barrel/pincushion distortion near image edges ($u > 1100\,\text{px}$) warps ray projections.
- **Empirical Evidence:** At the peripheral boundary of `CAM_DOCK_SOUTH`, radial distortion ($k_1 = -0.12$) induced a $0.14\,\text{m}$ lateral ground coordinate shift.
- **Classification:** **CONFIRMED AS FAIL-46.**
- **Remediation:** Camera calibration pipeline upgraded to enforce full radial/tangential Brown-Conrady undistortion before homography ray casting.
