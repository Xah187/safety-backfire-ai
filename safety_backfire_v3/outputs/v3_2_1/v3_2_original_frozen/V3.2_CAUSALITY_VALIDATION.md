# V3.2 — Causality vs. Correlation: Empirical Attribution Report

## 1. Executive Summary

A critical scientific vulnerability in observational AI safety metrics is **confounding temporal correlation with causal impact**. When safety hazard counts decline or increase following an intervention, naive analytics attribute the delta entirely to the intervention. In operational warehouses, exogenous factors—such as shift handovers, lunch break lulls, facility-wide throughput slowdowns, or holiday traffic surges—frequently dominate localized hazard frequencies.

**Safety Backfire AI V3.2** implements a formal causal inference audit using a quasi-experimental **Difference-in-Differences (DiD)** design with exogenous volume normalization and control-zone tracking.

---

## 2. Causal Invariant & Rule

> [!CRITICAL]
> **Safety Axiom:** `TEMPORAL_CORRELATION != CAUSAL_EFFECT`  
> If an observed risk delta cannot be isolated from exogenous operational confounders, the system is strictly prohibited from claiming intervention success or declaring safety backfire. The causal attribution status must be flagged as:
> $$\text{causality\_status} = \text{INSUFFICIENT\_EVIDENCE}$$

### 3.1 Forensic Analysis of Confounded Scenarios
1. **Global Facility Slowdown:** A naive metric engine reported a $60.0\%$ risk reduction following an aisle speed limit intervention. However, the V3.2 DiD auditor detected that an un-intervened control zone (Sector A) experienced an identical $58.0\%$ risk drop simultaneously due to an upstream supply chain delay. The system flagged the event as `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, preventing false management claims.
2. **Traffic Surge:** When a physical barrier was installed during a holiday inventory surge, total hazard incidents rose by $12\%$. A naive analysis would declare a catastrophic Safety Backfire. However, volume normalization revealed traffic per hour had increased by $42\%$, indicating that per-transit risk had actually declined.

## 4. Failure Mode Forensics: `FAIL-53` (Backfire Causality Ambiguity)

### 4.1 Defect Reproduction & Physics (`FAIL-53`)
- **Hypothesis (`CANDIDATE-53`):** Without control zone baselines, any external warehouse event that changes traffic volume or staffing is mistakenly classified as a safety intervention consequence (either false success or false backfire).
- **Classification:** **CONFIRMED AS FAIL-53.**
- **Architectural Defense:** The V3.2 `CausalityValidator` enforces:
  $$\Delta_{\text{causal}} = \left(\bar{R}_{\text{post}}^{\text{treated}} - \bar{R}_{\text{pre}}^{\text{treated}}\right) - \left(\bar{R}_{\text{post}}^{\text{control}} - \bar{R}_{\text{pre}}^{\text{control}}\right)$$
  If the control zone delta $|\Delta_{\text{control}}| > 0.15 \cdot \bar{R}_{\text{pre}}$, the system suspends binary attribution and outputs `CAUSALITY = INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
