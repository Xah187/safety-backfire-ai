# V3.2 — Master Safety Failure Registry & Defect Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

A core discipline of Safety Backfire AI is the **Failure-First Engineering Workflow**. Defect modes are never concealed behind high aggregate benchmark scores, smoothed away by threshold inflation, or silently rewritten. Every discovered safety vulnerability is formally registered, reproduced with an immutable test fixture, root-caused, classified, remediated, and verified via regression tests.

This registry documents **49 total failure modes** (`FAIL-01` through `FAIL-37` historical, and 12 newly confirmed failures in V3.2: `FAIL-38` to `FAIL-55`). Furthermore, all 6 unreproduced candidate hypotheses are cataloged with empirical evidence.
