# V3.2 — Level C: Real Identity Validation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

In industrial warehouse environments, human workers wear identical high-visibility safety vests (fluorescent neon yellow/orange), identical hard hats, and standardized corporate workwear. Furthermore, fleets of warehouse forklifts and reach trucks are visually identical in model, color, and silhouette.

This report evaluates the durable identity preservation capabilities of **Safety Backfire AI V3.2** across $18$ real-world identity stress encounters.
- **Overall Identity Accuracy:** $88.88\%$ ($16 / 18$ encounters fully preserved)
- **Total Identity Swaps:** $2$
- **Total False Merges:** $1$
- **Total False Splits:** $2$
- **Mean Identity Age Survival:** $38.4\,\text{seconds}$

### 2.1 Key Empirical Findings
1. **Identical Forklifts:** Vehicle tracks maintained $100\%$ accuracy during crossing passes because spatial footprints are large ($2.4 \times 1.4\,\text{m}$) and vehicle kinematics (turning radius and non-holonomic motion constraints) strongly penalize sudden spatial teleportation.
2. **Similar PPE Neon Vests:** Human appearance feature embeddings (e.g. ResNet/OSNet ReID vectors) show high cosine similarity ($> 0.88$) between different workers wearing identical safety apparel. When two workers walk alongside each other, appearance vectors provide virtually zero discriminative power.
3. **Prolonged Occlusion:** When an occluded worker emerges after $> 3.0\,\text{seconds}$, appearance embeddings alone fail to distinguish the worker from another person standing nearby.

## 3. Failure Mode Forensics: `FAIL-40` (Identical PPE Uniform Swap)

### 3.1 Defect Reproduction & Safety Impact
- **Hypothesis (`CANDIDATE-40`):** Two warehouse workers wearing identical class-2 high-vis vests cross paths. Due to near-identical appearance embeddings, the tracker swaps their track identities.
- **Empirical Evidence:** In `VID_TEST_001` (Frame 780 to 825), Worker A (forklift operator dismounted in staging zone) and Worker B (pedestrian picking stock) crossed within $0.6\,\text{m}$. Upon separation, Worker A was assigned Worker B's track ID.
- **Safety Impact:** If the safety policy utilizes operator exclusion zones (where an authorized operator is permitted near their designated vehicle, but unauthorized pedestrians are flagged for collision risk), an identity swap suppresses an active safety alert when the unauthorized pedestrian approaches the active vehicle.
- **Classification:** **CONFIRMED AS FAIL-40 (Extending FAIL-13).**

### 3.2 Architectural Remediation
1. **Spatial Kalman Gating Supremacy:** In V3.2, visual appearance embeddings are strictly subordinated to spatial trajectory gating. A visual similarity match is forbidden from overriding a spatial Mahalanobis distance exclusion ($D_M > 3.0\,\sigma$).
2. **Ambiguity Escalation:** If two identical PPE tracks enter a spatial overlap zone ($D_{\text{clearance}} < 1.0\,\text{m}$), the system explicitly flags both entities with `identity_state = AMBIGUOUS_DISPUTED`.
3. **Defensive Policy:** During `AMBIGUOUS_DISPUTED` state, operator suppression privileges are revoked for both entities until spatial separation verifies distinct identities.
