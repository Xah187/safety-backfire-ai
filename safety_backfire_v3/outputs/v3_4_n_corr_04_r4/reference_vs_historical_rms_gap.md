## 1. Epistemic Principle: Non-Inheritance of Reference Assumptions

A critical defect in earlier audits was the tacit assumption that historical trial data was generated by the same algorithm and configuration formulated during analytical modeling. 

**Rule of Non-Inheritance:**  
The historical column in this audit **SHALL NOT** inherit R3's reference model assumptions. Every historical entry must reflect *solely* what is documented in primary historical records, manifests, and equipment logs. Where historical evidence is silent, the entry is marked `UNDOCUMENTED` or `MISSING`.

## 3. Detailed Forensic Analysis of Divergences

### 3.1 The Filter Divergence
The R3 reference model deliberately excluded any digital or analog filter because no filter topology, order, cut-off frequency, or group delay was ever formally specified with an implementation. In contrast, the historical CORR-04 test protocol explicitly claimed that a "hardware 4th-order 20 kHz low-pass filter" pre-conditioned the signal before the RMS stage. If such a filter had been physically present, it would have introduced an analog group delay of approximately $\sim 20.8\,\mu\text{s}$ (Butterworth) or more, altered the signal envelope, and attenuated high-frequency PWM switching harmonics. However, physical inspection of instrument specifications reveals that no such filter exists in the Keysight DSOX1204G. Thus, the reference model and the historical description represent two fundamentally divergent systems.

### 3.2 The Timestamping and Derived Timing Divergence
In the R3 reference model, the timestamp $t_{\text{RMS}}$ is strictly defined as the right edge of the sliding window, and the physical threshold crossing $t_{\text{phys}}$ is independently determined from the continuous physical decay curve $I(t)$. 

In the historical evidence manifest (`evidence_manifest_v3.csv`), however, the column `t_phys_envelope_cross_ms` is exactly equal to `t_rms_threshold_cross_ms - 1.12` across all 10 trials. This demonstrates that historical operators or analysts did not measure $t_{\text{phys}}$ from an oscillogram, but back-calculated it by subtracting the theoretical continuous flush lag $\Delta_{\text{flush}} \approx 1.12\,\text{ms}$. Consequently, the historical manifest cannot be used to validate the reference model without circular reasoning.

### 3.3 The Current Scale Divergence
The reference model operates in pure mathematical units of Amperes ($A$). In historical trials, current was measured using a Tektronix TCP303 probe connected to a TCPA300 amplifier. The amplifier has two switchable ranges: $5\,\text{A/V}$ and $50\,\text{A/V}$. As established in R3, the historical range setting is completely unrecorded (`HISTORICAL_CURRENT_SCALE = UNRESOLVED`). If historical trials were recorded at $50\,\text{A/V}$, a threshold of $0.410\,\text{A}$ corresponds to an oscilloscope input voltage of only $8.2\,\text{mV}$, where 8-bit digitizer quantization and vertical noise dominate. If recorded at $5\,\text{A/V}$, peak operating currents of $\sim 115\,\text{A}$ would violate continuous ratings.
