### 2.1 The Circular Self-Validation Trap
Evaluating AI safety predictions against detector inferences generated by the same codebase constitutes methodologically invalid ground-truth substitution. High model confidence or low bounding-box distance does not prove that an industrial intervention occurred or that a secondary hazard was caused by the safety system.

### 2.2 The Temporal Decay Deficit
In industrial traffic dynamics, a safety intervention in Corridor A (such as an AGV halting abruptly) creates shockwaves that propagate into adjoining aisles over periods of 60 to 180 seconds. A fixed 60-second clip truncates the post-intervention observation phase before causal spillover can be rigorously measured.

## 3. Specifications for Mandatory In-Situ Physical Field Acquisition

To transition Safety Backfire AI from exploratory candidate discovery to accredited safety validation, a dedicated physical facility acquisition program must be executed with the following 10 mandatory technical specifications:

### 3.1 Facility & Spatial Requirements
1. **Multi-Corridor Physical Facility**: An operating industrial or pilot warehouse with clearly demarcated Primary Zones (aisles, blind intersections) and Secondary Zones (adjacent picking bays, emergency egress paths).
2. **Total-Station Coordinate Surveying**: In-situ optical surveying verifying camera 3D positions ($x, y, z$), mounting roll/pitch/yaw angles, and ground-plane fiducials to millimeter precision.

### 3.2 Sensing & Synchronization Infrastructure
3. **Hardware Genlock Video Synchrony**: Synchronized multi-camera array capturing overlapping fields of view with timestamp drift $< 1.0\,\text{ms}$.
4. **NIST-Traceable Radiometric Lux Telemetry**: Calibrated physical illuminance sensors logging lux at the ground plane at $\ge 10\,\text{Hz}$ across all camera fields of view to satisfy Model H formal requirements.
5. **Vehicle Internal Telemetry (CAN-Bus)**: Real-time acquisition of vehicle speed, steering angle, brake cylinder pressure, and safety-scanner field clearance states.
6. **Active Intervention Signal Capture**: Time-stamped event triggers recording when safety alerts (horns, beacons) or emergency braking circuits are commanded.

### 3.3 Experimental Design & Controlled Staging
7. **Controlled Safety Backfire Protocols**: Staged operational scenarios executing standard interventions (e.g., obstacle detection leading to hard stop) with instrumented bystander vehicles and workers in adjacent zones.
8. **Extended Temporal Windows**: Continuous recordings of at least 15 minutes per scenario to capture baseline (`PRE`), execution (`INTERVENTION`), and complete spillover resolution (`POST`).

### 3.4 Annotation & Consensus Governance
9. **Independent Multi-Expert Annotation**: Ground-truth bounding boxes, metric 3D trajectories, and event boundaries annotated by independent human annotators.
10. **Dual-Reviewer Consensus Adjudication**: Blinded multi-expert safety adjudication implementing the project's canonical 7-criterion Backfire gate (`schemas/v3_4/safety_backfire_event_gt.schema.json`).
